We're Here to Help

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?+

Add pieces to your cart from any product page, then head to checkout. You can also message us on WhatsApp for a more personal styling conversation before you buy.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?+

Reach out to our customer care team within 4 hours of placing your order and we'll do our best to accommodate changes before it's packed.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?+

Yes — Cash on Delivery is available at checkout alongside UPI, cards, and net banking.

How long does delivery take?+

Most orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days and arrive within 5–8 business days depending on your location. You can check an estimate on any product page.

Do you deliver across India?+

Yes, we deliver pan-India. Enter your pincode on a product page to see an estimated delivery window.

Is shipping free?+

Shipping is free on orders above ₹5,000. A flat ₹149 fee applies below that.

What is your return policy?+

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused pieces with tags intact. Bridal and made-to-order pieces are final sale.

How do refunds work?+

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

How should I care for a handwoven silk saree?+

Dry clean recommended. Store folded in muslin cloth away from direct sunlight, and refold along different lines every few months to prevent permanent creasing.

Will the colour of my saree match the photos exactly?+

We photograph in natural light to stay as accurate as possible, but handwoven dyes can vary slightly piece to piece — that variation is part of the craft.
