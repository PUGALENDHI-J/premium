## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-clarity environment engineered for dual-purpose enterprise mobility: automated career advancement and deep skill acquisition. The target audience comprises hiring executives, talent directors, senior technical professionals, and enterprise-sponsored learners.

The emotional baseline is trust, institutional credibility, and hyper-competence. The interface minimizes decorative distraction, presenting complex algorithmic recommendations (AI matching scores, skill gap analyses, syllabus roadmaps) through disciplined structural cadence. 

The aesthetic is Modern Corporate with Technical Precision:
- High data density calibrated through deliberate micro-whitespace.
- Crisp, hairline slate boundary lines defining logical zones.
- Micro-interactions that emphasize speed, deterministic feedback, and executive gravitas.
- Purposeful visual tension between cool executive slate foundations and energetic algorithmic violet cues.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative stability with active intelligence. Color fulfills functional roles without generating cognitive fatigue across prolonged analytical or pedagogical sessions.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Represents structural authority. Applied to deep navigation headers, primary text, high-level administrative wrappers, and terminal action anchors. Paired with secondary navy (`#1E293B`) for layered structural surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: The enterprise engine color. Drives core interactive pathways, active job pipelines, confirmed enrollments, and high-priority primary triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1`) & Accent Violet (`#8B5CF6`)**: Reserved for predictive signals, AI match indexes, cognitive skill evaluations, and machine-synthesized career insights.
- **Neutral Palette (`#F8FAFC` to `#020617`)**: Base surface sits on `#F8FAFC`, card canvas on `#FFFFFF`, framing strokes on `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`, with metadata anchored by `#64748B`.
- **System Feedback**:
  - Success (Verification, Completed Courses): `#059669` (Dark Green)
  - Attention / Warning (Expiring Certification): `#D97706` (Amber)
  - Critical (Unfulfilled Core Prerequisite): `#DC2626` (Red)

All text-to-background combinations maintain a strict contrast ratio exceeding 4.5:1 for body elements and 3:1 for large display elements, maintaining complete WCAG AA fidelity.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the structural geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and metrics with the neutral, hyper-legible text performance of Inter.

- **Headlines & Metric Titles**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans. Tighter letter spacing ensures dense enterprise viewports remain legible without expansive vertical waste.
- **Body & Tabular Content**: Rendered in Inter with proportional vertical line-heights calibrated for multi-line job specifications, complex curriculum overviews, and dense student tracking logs.
- **Labels, Badges & AI Chips**: Set in uppercase or small semi-bold Inter to preserve readability down to 11px within status badges and match percentages.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: AI scores and currency indicators leverage Inter’s tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter across dynamic updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for analytical workspaces:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12 columns with 1.5rem (`gutter`) columns and 2rem canvas `margin`. Primary views split between persistent contextual navigation (280px fixed width), a dynamic work area (minimum 640px), and an contextual rail for AI analytics or candidate deep dives (360px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns with 1.25rem gutters. The intelligence rail collapses into a stacked drawer, prioritizing job pipelines or course video players.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px)**: 4 columns with 1rem gutters and margins (`gutter-mobile`, `margin-mobile`). Multi-column card grids collapse to a single vertical column, preserving full-bleed edge scans.

Component spacing obeys an intentional strict 4px/8px scale. High-frequency enterprise modules (such as candidate match scorecards and module progression trees) utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact information binding, while structural containers depend upon `space-lg` and `space-xl` to prevent layout collapse.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes precision framing through sharp, low-contrast hairline borders complemented by ambient, blue-tinted shadows. Surfaces rely on tonal stacking rather than heavy dropshadows:

1. **Canvas (Base)**: `#F8FAFC`. Foundational stage for panels and tables.
2. **Layer 0 (Card Panels & Inputs)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaced with a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Flat elevation.
3. **Layer 1 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Hovered Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a low-opacity, cold ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
4. **Layer 2 (Context Panels, Command Bars, Floating Modals)**: Elevated utility elements:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
5. **AI Cognitive Focus / Predictive Elements**: Framed by a subtle gradient border using an indigo highlight (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)` to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.05)`) and an ultra-subtle ambient glow:
   `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a "Soft" architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Controlled curvature communicates industrial reliability while sidestepping both harsh brutalist edges and juvenile rounded aesthetics:

- **Micro Controls & Inputs**: Form fields, table actions, buttons, and segmented pickers implement a base `4px` border-radius (`0.25rem`).
- **Standard Containers**: Candidate profiles, curriculum modules, and interactive metric cards leverage `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Modal Windows & Flyouts**: Major structural floating containers scale up to `12px` (`0.75rem`).
- **Pills / Status Chips**: Verification indicators and algorithmic percentage badges carry a full circular radius (`9999px`) to immediately visually delineate dynamic system status from static rectangular layout blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid enterprise blue (`#2563EB`) text in `#FFFFFF`, `4px` radius, hover shifts to `#1D4ED8`. Active state shifts to `#1E40AF`.
- **Administrative / Executive**: Solid deep navy (`#0F172A`) with `#FFFFFF` label.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background, hairline border (`#CBD5E1`), text in `#0F172A`. Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **AI Action**: Deep navy-to-indigo subtle gradient with a clean 1px border (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`), `#FFFFFF` label, paired with a leading violet spark icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, inset padding of `0.5rem 0.75rem`.
- Focus state: Replaces border with `#2563EB` and emits an accessible ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Error state: 1px solid `#DC2626` paired with an alert message in `label-sm`.

### Status Tags & System Badges
- Constructed with `roundedness: 3` (pill), padding `2px 8px`, `label-sm` font.
- **Verified Enterprise Badge**: `#F0FDF4` background, `#166534` text, accompanied by a verified check icon.
- **Application Status (Interviewing, In Review)**: `#EFF6FF` background, `#1E40AF` text, 1px solid `#DBEAFE`.
- **Compliance Warning**: `#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text.

### AI Match Indicator
- Distinctive hybrid indicator. Features an outer pill using `#EEF2FF` with `#4338CA` text, displaying a bold tabular match percentage (e.g., "94% Match").
- Accompanying tooltip or sub-badge reveals a two-tone breakdown: Skills Match (Indigo) and Experience Alignment (Blue).

### Cards
- **Job Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, interior padding `1.25rem`. Layout organizes company iconography and title at top-left, AI Match Indicator top-right, salary and location metrics along the mid-deck, and action triggers anchored along a clean separator border below.
- **Course / Module Card**: Integrated video thumbnail/preview header with a 2px bottom progress bar (indigo-to-blue gradient). Displays course difficulty pill, duration metrics, and a trailing credential verification icon.

### Candidate & Student Data Lists
- Alternating subtle rows or clean dividers (`1px solid #F1F5F9`).
- Column headers leverage `label-sm` in `#64748B` with active sort indicators. Rows maintain a fixed height of `56px` to prioritize high-speed scanability.
