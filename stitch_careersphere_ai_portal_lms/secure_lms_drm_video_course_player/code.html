Does the new Cilium BPF masquerade fully supersede kube-proxy iptables mode when routing ingress across node boundaries?

Rajiv Mehta Instructor 1 hour ago

Yes, Siddharth. When Cilium is initialized with kubeProxyReplacement=true, eBPF programs attached to the TC (traffic control) hook inspect and rewrite L3/L4 packet headers directly, skipping the iptables conntrack overhead completely.
