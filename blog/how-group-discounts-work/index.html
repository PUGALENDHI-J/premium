Budget and premium travel

How group discounts work on holidays

How group travel can lower costs per person, where the savings come from, what affects them and how to organise a group trip to benefit.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Travelling as a group can reduce the cost per person, but the savings depend on how the trip is structured.

Where the savings come from

  • Shared transport: a minibus or coach costs less per person than several cars.
  • Shared guides: one guide for a whole group.
  • Room sharing: twin or triple sharing lowers the cost per person.
  • Group rates: some hotels, attractions and activities offer group pricing above a certain number.
  • Group fares: airlines sometimes offer group fares, though these come with conditions on names and payments.

What affects the savings

  • Group size: larger groups usually save more, but very large groups may need multiple vehicles or hotels.
  • Season: in peak season, group rates may be limited.
  • Flexibility: groups that accept a range of dates or hotels get better deals.
  • Payment terms: group bookings often need deposits earlier.

When group travel costs more

  • Single rooms carry supplements.
  • Last-minute changes in numbers can raise the price per person.
  • Large groups may need bigger hotels in less central locations.

Organising your own group

See How to plan a group trip with friends or family without the stress and our Group tours page.

Tips for getting the most value

Confirm numbers early, collect deposits before booking, agree on room sharing and be flexible on dates. Groups that travel mid-week or outside peak dates usually save the most.
