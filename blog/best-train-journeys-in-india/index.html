The most scenic train journeys in India for holidaymakers

From UNESCO-listed toy trains to coastal and mountain routes, these Indian rail journeys are worth building a holiday around.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · 2 min read

Some of India's most memorable views are seen from a train window. These journeys are worth planning a trip around.

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway

The rack railway from Mettupalayam to Ooty climbs through tunnels, over bridges and past tea estates. It is part of the UNESCO-listed Mountain Railways of India. See Chennai to Ooty by the Nilgiri toy train: how it works.

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway

The original "toy train" winds through the streets of Darjeeling, with loops and reverses to gain height. The joy ride from Darjeeling to Ghum, past the Batasia Loop, is the easiest way to try it.

The Kalka–Shimla Railway

More than a hundred tunnels and hundreds of bridges take this narrow-gauge line up to Shimla. Ride it one way and drive the other.

The Araku Valley line

From Visakhapatnam to Araku Valley, the line passes through dozens of tunnels and across viaducts in the Eastern Ghats. Choose a glass-roofed vistadome coach if available.

The Konkan Railway

Running down India's west coast between Mumbai and Mangaluru, it crosses rivers and passes through tunnels with views of paddy fields and the Western Ghats. It is a lovely way to reach Goa or Gokarna.

The Pamban Bridge to Rameswaram

The railway across the sea to Rameswaram gives an unforgettable view of the water on both sides.

The Kangra Valley Railway

A narrow-gauge line in Himachal Pradesh with views of the Dhauladhar range, between Pathankot and Jogindernagar.

Tips

  • Book early; scenic trains have limited seats. See Booking Indian train tickets for a holiday: classes, timing and tips.
  • Sit on the side with the best views; ask the staff.
  • Plan a road alternative in case seats are not available.
