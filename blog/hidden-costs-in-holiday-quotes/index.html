The hidden costs that make a cheap holiday expensive

Common costs left out of cheap holiday quotes, from resort transfers and local taxes to entry tickets and meals, and how to compare quotes fairly.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

A very cheap holiday quote can end up expensive once you add everything it leaves out. These are the most common hidden costs.

Transfers

  • Seaplane or speedboat transfers in the Maldives can be a large part of the cost.
  • Local union taxis in Kashmir.
  • Island ferries.
  • Airport transfers at odd hours.

Taxes and fees

  • GST and TCS.
  • Tourist taxes paid at hotels in some cities and countries.
  • Resort fees and green taxes.

Entry tickets

A quote that "includes sightseeing" may include only transport, not tickets to the Burj Khalifa in Dubai, Universal Studios or national parks.

Meals

  • Breakfast-only packages at remote resorts mean expensive lunches and dinners.
  • Drinks are rarely included.

Activities

Water sports, desert safaris, shows and spa treatments may be listed as "optional".

Visas and insurance

Not always included, and sometimes significant.

Upgrades

The cheapest room may be very small or have no view. The upgrade you want may cost more than the difference between two quotes.

How to compare fairly

  • Ask for hotel names and room types.
  • Read the inclusions and exclusions line by line.
  • Ask what local charges to expect.

See How to read a travel quote and compare packages fairly and How to budget a holiday: the costs people forget.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Are all transfers included, including seaplanes and ferries?
  • Which entry tickets are included?
  • What meals are included each day?
  • Are there local taxes or fees payable on arrival?
  • What does the quote not include?

A trustworthy planner answers clearly and puts it in writing.
