Diwali holiday ideas: celebrate at home or travel away

Ideas for the Diwali holidays, from celebrating in Varanasi, Ayodhya or Jaipur to short breaks in Goa, Singapore, Dubai and Thailand.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Diwali brings a few days off for many families. Whether you want to celebrate somewhere special or escape for a quiet break, here are some ideas.

Celebrate Diwali somewhere special

  • Varanasi: Diwali is followed about two weeks later by Dev Deepawali, when the ghats glow with lamps.
  • Ayodhya: the Deepotsav celebration lights the Sarayu ghats with lakhs of lamps.
  • Jaipur: markets and buildings are illuminated.
  • Amritsar: the Golden Temple is beautifully lit for Bandi Chhor Divas.

Short breaks in India

  • Goa: the start of the season, before peak prices.
  • Rajasthan: pleasant weather.
  • Kerala and the hills of the south.

Abroad

  • Singapore: Deepavali is a public holiday, and Little India is decorated with lights and bazaars.
  • Malaysia: Deepavali is a public holiday, with celebrations in Kuala Lumpur and Penang.
  • Dubai: large Indian community celebrations.
  • Thailand: a quick beach or city break.
  • Mauritius: Diwali is a public holiday, with homes lit by lamps.

Tips

  • Diwali dates change each year; check them early.
  • Flights from India are busy; book two to three months ahead.
  • Some shops and restaurants in India close for the main day.

See Travelling during Diwali, Pongal and Christmas holidays: how to plan and our Short breaks page.

Getting the timing right

If you want to celebrate Diwali at home with family, consider travelling a few days after the festival, when demand drops. If you want to experience a famous celebration elsewhere, arrive a day early.

Safety

Fireworks are part of Diwali in many places. Families with young children and pets may prefer a quieter destination.

Combine with the weekend

Add a day or two of leave to Diwali to make a four- or five-night break.
