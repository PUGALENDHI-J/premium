Book early or wait for a last-minute deal?

When it pays to book a holiday early, when last-minute deals make sense, and how flights, hotels, visas and festivals change the answer.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Should you book early or wait for a bargain? The answer depends on when and where you are travelling.

When to book early

  • Peak seasons: Christmas and New Year, Diwali, summer school holidays and long weekends. Good-value rooms and flights sell out first.
  • Festivals: Pushkar, Hornbill, Durga Puja, Yi Peng and cherry blossom.
  • Limited-capacity experiences: safari permits, toy trains, Machu Picchu entry and Jungfraujoch slots.
  • Visa destinations: Europe, the UK, the USA and Japan need time for visas.
  • Small or unique properties: Maldives water villas and heritage hotels.
  • Groups: large groups need blocks of rooms and seats.

When last-minute can work

  • Off-season travel to destinations with plenty of hotels.
  • Flexible travellers who can go wherever there is a deal.
  • Short trips within India, when you are not tied to specific hotels.

Flights

Fares often rise as the date approaches, especially on popular routes and dates. Booking a few months ahead usually gives better choice.

Hotels

Many hotels offer flexible rates that allow free cancellation up to a point; these give you security while you keep watching prices.

Our advice

For peak dates in places like Goa or the Maldives, book three to six months ahead. For off-season trips, six to eight weeks is often enough.

See our Short breaks and Budget holidays pages.

Protect yourself when booking early

  • Choose refundable or flexible rates where the price difference is small.
  • Buy travel insurance as soon as you book.
  • Check cancellation terms carefully.
  • Keep dates flexible by a day or two if you can; it often unlocks better prices.
