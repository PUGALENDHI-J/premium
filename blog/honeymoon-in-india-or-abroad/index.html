Honeymoon in India or abroad? How to decide

Comparing honeymoons in India and abroad on budget, travel time, paperwork, experiences and season, with the best choices for each.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

One of the first decisions couples face is whether to honeymoon in India or abroad. Both have advantages.

Reasons to choose India

  • No visa or passport worries, useful if the wedding is close and paperwork is not ready.
  • Familiar food and language.
  • Great variety: snow in Kashmir, backwaters in Kerala, palaces in Rajasthan and beaches in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands Islands.
  • Often better value for luxury hotels.

Reasons to choose abroad

  • A sense of adventure and novelty.
  • Island experiences such as the Maldives that are hard to match in India.
  • Destinations like Bali (South Coast) and Thailand that are designed for couples, with villas and spas at good prices.
  • Short flights from South India to many destinations.

Questions to ask yourselves

  1. How much time do we have? For four to five days, choose somewhere with a short journey.
  2. Do we have passports and time for visas?
  3. What experience do we want? Beach, mountains, culture or luxury?
  4. When is the wedding? The season decides a lot. Winter weddings suit Rajasthan, Kerala, Thailand and the Maldives; summer weddings suit Kashmir, Bali and Switzerland.

Popular choices

  • India: Kashmir, Kerala, Andamans, Udaipur, Manali, Coorg.
  • Abroad: Bali, Maldives, Thailand, Mauritius, Switzerland, Sri Lanka.

Can we do both?

Some couples take a short trip right after the wedding and a bigger trip abroad later. That works well when leave is limited.
