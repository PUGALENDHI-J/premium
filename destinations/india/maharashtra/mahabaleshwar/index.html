Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Mahabaleshwar

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Marathi.

Getting around

Mumbai and Pune have the main airports; Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) serves the caves. The Mumbai–Pune expressway connects to Lonavala.

Planner's tip

Ajanta is closed on Mondays and Ellora on Tuesdays, so plan the caves on two different days.

Planner's tip

Mumbai local trains are fast but crowded at rush hour; use a car for sightseeing.

Planner's tip

Pair Mahabaleshwar with Panchgani; they are 20 km apart and share a hotel base.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is the highest hill station in the Sahyadri, with viewpoints over deep valleys, strawberry farms and Venna Lake. Nearby Panchgani has a table-land plateau, and Pratapgad Fort is a short drive away.

Highlights include Arthur's Seat, Venna Lake, Pratapgad Fort and Mapro Garden. Local food worth trying: Strawberries with cream, Corn pattice and Maharashtrian thali. Plan 2 days here; it suits couples, families. Our Mahabaleshwar itineraries run from 4 to 5 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to June. Strawberry season is December to March; the monsoon brings heavy rain and closures.

How to reach Mahabaleshwar

  • By air: Pune Airport (PNQ), about 120 km
  • By rail: Satara or Pune stations
  • By road: About 3 hours from Pune

Travel tips

Mahabaleshwar travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to June. Strawberry season is December to March; the monsoon brings heavy rain and closures.

Getting around

Mumbai and Pune have the main airports; Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) serves the caves. The Mumbai–Pune expressway connects to Lonavala.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites, a compact umbrella or rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
