Mahabaleshwar

About Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is the highest hill station in the Sahyadri, with viewpoints over deep valleys, strawberry farms and Venna Lake. Nearby Panchgani has a table-land plateau, and Pratapgad Fort is a short drive away.

Top things to see in Mahabaleshwar

  • Arthur's Seat – Viewpoint over the Savitri valley
  • Venna Lake – Boating and horse rides
  • Pratapgad Fort – Shivaji's fort, site of the Afzal Khan encounter
  • Mapro Garden – Strawberry products and café
  • Table Land, Panchgani – Flat volcanic plateau
  • Elephant's Head Point – Rock formation and viewpoint

Things to do

Strawberry picking (winter)BoatingFort visitSunset points

What to eat

  • Strawberries with cream
  • Corn pattice
  • Maharashtrian thali

Best time to visit Mahabaleshwar

October to June. Strawberry season is December to March; the monsoon brings heavy rain and closures.

How to reach Mahabaleshwar

  • By air: Pune Airport (PNQ), about 120 km
  • By rail: Satara or Pune stations
  • By road: About 3 hours from Pune

Planner's tip

Pair Mahabaleshwar with Panchgani; they are 20 km apart and share a hotel base.
