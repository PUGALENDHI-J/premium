Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Assam

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Assamese.

Getting around

Guwahati is the main airport and rail hub. Kaziranga is about 4–5 hours by road.

Planner's tip

Book an early-morning elephant or jeep safari at Kaziranga; the light and wildlife activity are best at dawn.

Planner's tip

Bihu festivals in April and January are a lively time to visit.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Assam

Assam is the gateway to the Northeast and the land of the Brahmaputra. Kaziranga National Park protects the world's largest population of one-horned rhinos, Majuli is one of the largest river islands on earth, and tea estates roll across the upper valley.

We plan trips to 3 destinations in Assam: Kaziranga National Park, Guwahati and Majuli. Highlights include Central (Kohora) range, Kamakhya Temple and Kamalabari and Auniati satras. Local food worth trying: Assamese thali, Duck curry, Assam tea and Assamese fish tenga. Our Assam itineraries run from 3 to 8 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

November to April. Kaziranga is open from November to April; the park closes in the monsoon.

Getting around

Guwahati is the main airport and rail hub. Kaziranga is about 4–5 hours by road.

Travel tips

Assam travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

November to April. Kaziranga is open from November to April; the park closes in the monsoon.

Getting around

Guwahati is the main airport and rail hub. Kaziranga is about 4–5 hours by road.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a compact umbrella or rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
