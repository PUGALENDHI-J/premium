Rann of Kutch

Best time: November to February  ·  Ideal stay: 2–3 days

About Rann of Kutch

The Great Rann of Kutch is a vast white salt desert that glows under the full moon. During the Rann Utsav (roughly November to February), a tent city at Dhordo hosts crafts, folk music and camel rides; the villages around Bhuj are famous for embroidery and block printing.

Top things to see in Rann of Kutch

  • White Rann at Dhordo – Salt flats stretching to the horizon
  • Kalo Dungar – Highest point in Kutch with views over the Rann
  • Bhuj Aina Mahal and Prag Mahal – Palaces in the old capital
  • Bhujodi and Hodka villages – Weaving and embroidery crafts
  • Mandvi Beach and Vijay Vilas Palace – Seaside palace and shipbuilding yards

Best time to visit Rann of Kutch

November to February. Rann Utsav season; full-moon nights are the highlight.

How to reach Rann of Kutch

  • By air: Bhuj Airport (BHJ), about 80 km from Dhordo
  • By rail: Bhuj railway station
  • By road: About 7–8 hours from Ahmedabad

Planner's tip

Time your trip with a full moon for the white desert at night; a border permit is needed and is handled at the checkpoint or online.
