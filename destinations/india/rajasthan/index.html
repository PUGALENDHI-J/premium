Rajasthan overview

Rajasthan is the land of maharajas: hilltop forts, lake palaces, painted havelis and the golden dunes of the Thar Desert. Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer each have a distinct colour and character, and Ranthambore adds one of India's best tiger reserves.

When to go

October to March. Cool, dry days for forts and desert camps. Summer (April to June) is extremely hot.

Getting around

Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer have airports. Distances are long, so most visitors travel by car with driver and use trains for longer hops.

Tips from our planners

  • Book Ranthambore safari zones and gypsy/canter seats as early as possible; they open months ahead.
  • Carry a scarf or jacket in winter; desert nights get cold even when the days are warm.
