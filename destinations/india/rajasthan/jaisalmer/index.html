About Jaisalmer

Jaisalmer, the Golden City, rises from the Thar Desert, its sandstone fort still home to families living inside the walls. Camel rides and desert camps at the Sam and Khuri dunes complete the picture.

Top things to see in Jaisalmer

  • Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Qila) – Living fort of golden sandstone
  • Patwon ki Haveli – Five carved merchant havelis
  • Gadisar Lake – Lake with chhatris and boats
  • Sam Sand Dunes – Camel safaris and sunset
  • Kuldhara – Abandoned village
  • Bada Bagh – Royal cenotaphs

Things to do

Camel safariDesert camp night with folk musicJeep dune bashingFort walkStargazing

What to eat

  • Ker sangri
  • Dal baati churma
  • Ghotua laddoo

Best time to visit Jaisalmer

October to March. Desert festival in February; summers are scorching.

How to reach Jaisalmer

  • By air: Jaisalmer Airport (JSA), with seasonal flights; Jodhpur (JDH), about 285 km
  • By rail: Jaisalmer station
  • By road: About 5 hours from Jodhpur

Planner's tip

Spend one night in a desert camp at Sam or Khuri; the sunset and night sky are the highlight.
