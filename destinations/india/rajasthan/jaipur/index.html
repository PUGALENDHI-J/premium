About Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City, was planned in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. Amber Fort's palaces, the honeycomb façade of the Hawa Mahal and the astronomical instruments of Jantar Mantar make it the third corner of the Golden Triangle.

Top things to see in Jaipur

  • Amber Fort – Hilltop palace-fort of red sandstone and marble
  • Hawa Mahal – Palace of Winds with 953 windows
  • City Palace – Royal residence and museum
  • Jantar Mantar – UNESCO-listed astronomical observatory
  • Nahargarh Fort – Sunset views over the city
  • Albert Hall Museum – Indo-Saracenic museum

Things to do

Bazaar shopping for gems and textilesBlock-printing workshopHot-air balloon ride (seasonal)Sunset at NahargarhFolk dinner at Chokhi Dhani

What to eat

  • Dal baati churma
  • Laal maas
  • Pyaaz kachori
  • Ghewar

Best time to visit Jaipur

October to March. Pleasant days; the Jaipur Literature Festival is in January.

How to reach Jaipur

  • By air: Jaipur International Airport (JAI)
  • By rail: Jaipur Junction
  • By road: About 5 hours from Delhi by road

Planner's tip

Reach Amber Fort at opening time to avoid the heat and the crowds.
