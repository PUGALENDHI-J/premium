Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Jaipur

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Hindi, Rajasthani.

Getting around

Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer have airports. Distances are long, so most visitors travel by car with driver and use trains for longer hops.

Planner's tip

Book Ranthambore safari zones and gypsy/canter seats as early as possible; they open months ahead.

Planner's tip

Carry a scarf or jacket in winter; desert nights get cold even when the days are warm.

Planner's tip

Reach Amber Fort at opening time to avoid the heat and the crowds.

Everything you need to know about Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City, was planned in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. Amber Fort's palaces, the honeycomb façade of the Hawa Mahal and the astronomical instruments of Jantar Mantar make it the third corner of the Golden Triangle.

Highlights include Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar. Local food worth trying: Dal baati churma, Laal maas, Pyaaz kachori and Ghewar. Plan 2 days here; it suits first-time visitors, families, shoppers. Our Jaipur itineraries run from 3 to 11 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to March. Pleasant days; the Jaipur Literature Festival is in January.

How to reach Jaipur

  • By air: Jaipur International Airport (JAI)
  • By rail: Jaipur Junction
  • By road: About 5 hours from Delhi by road

Travel tips

Jaipur travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to March. Pleasant days; the Jaipur Literature Festival is in January.

Getting around

Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer have airports. Distances are long, so most visitors travel by car with driver and use trains for longer hops.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
