Incredible India, planned for you

India packs deserts, rainforests, Himalayan passes, coral islands and thousands of years of living history into one country, and most of it is a short flight or an overnight train from Chennai.

India packs deserts, rainforests, Himalayan passes, coral islands and thousands of years of living history into one country, and most of it is a short flight or an overnight train from Chennai. The trick is matching the region to the season: the hills of the south and the northeast shine after the monsoon, Rajasthan and the Golden Triangle are best in the cool months, and Ladakh and Spiti open only in summer.

Our India itineraries are private and built around your dates. We handle hotels, a car and driver, trains or flights from Chennai, and the small details such as temple timings, permits for restricted areas and the best day of the week for markets.
