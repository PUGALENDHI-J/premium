Best time: March to October  ·  Ideal stay: 2 days

About Katra (Vaishno Devi)

Katra is the base for the Mata Vaishno Devi shrine, one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in India, set in a cave about 12 km up the Trikuta hills. Pilgrims walk, ride ponies, use battery cars or take a helicopter part of the way.

Top things to see in Katra (Vaishno Devi)

  • Mata Vaishno Devi Bhawan – Holy cave shrine
  • Bhairon Nath Temple – Visited after darshan, reached by ropeway
  • Ardhkuwari – Halfway cave shrine
  • Banganga – Start of the trek
  • Jammu's Raghunath Temple – Temple complex in Jammu city

Things to do

Pilgrim trekHelicopter to SanjichhatRopeway to BhaironNight darshan

What to eat

  • Kalari cheese

Best time to visit Katra (Vaishno Devi)

March to October. Navratri is the busiest time; winters are cold but the shrine stays open.

How to reach Katra (Vaishno Devi)

  • By air: Jammu Airport (IXJ), about 50 km
  • By rail: Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra station
  • By road: About 1.5 hours from Jammu

Planner's tip

Register for the yatra (RFID card) before starting; helicopter seats must be booked on the official shrine board site.
