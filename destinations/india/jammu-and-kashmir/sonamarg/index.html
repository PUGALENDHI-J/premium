Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Sonamarg

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Kashmiri, Dogri, Urdu.

Getting around

Srinagar and Jammu have airports. A car with driver is the usual way to travel within the valley.

Planner's tip

Prepaid mobile SIMs from other states may not work in J&K; postpaid connections usually do.

Planner's tip

Local taxi unions operate at Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg for sightseeing beyond the main town; this is normal and included in most quotes.

Planner's tip

The Z-Morh tunnel now keeps Sonamarg open longer, but Zoji La still closes in heavy snow.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Sonamarg

Sonamarg, the meadow of gold, lies on the road to Ladakh beside the Sindh river, with the Thajiwas Glacier a short pony ride away. It is the last big stop before the Zoji La pass.

Highlights include Thajiwas Glacier, Sindh river, Zoji La viewpoint and Zero Point. Local food worth trying: Kashmiri kahwa and Simple dhaba meals. Plan 1 day here; it suits families, road-trippers. Our Sonamarg itineraries run from 4 to 7 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

April to October. Snow remains near the glacier into summer.

How to reach Sonamarg

  • By air: Srinagar Airport (SXR), about 80 km
  • By rail: Srinagar station
  • By road: About 2.5 hours from Srinagar

Travel tips

Sonamarg travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. Some places here are at high altitude: rest on arrival, drink plenty of water and ascend gradually.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

April to October. Snow remains near the glacier into summer.

Getting around

Srinagar and Jammu have airports. A car with driver is the usual way to travel within the valley.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack warm layers, a down jacket and gloves, a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
