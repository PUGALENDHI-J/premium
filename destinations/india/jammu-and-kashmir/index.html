Jammu & Kashmir overview

Kashmir's valley of lakes, meadows and pine forests is one of India's classic honeymoon and family destinations. Srinagar's houseboats and Mughal gardens, Gulmarg's gondola, Pahalgam's riverside meadows and Sonamarg's glacier views make a natural circuit, while Katra is the base for the Vaishno Devi pilgrimage.

When to go

April to October; December to February for snow. Spring tulips and summer meadows; winter brings skiing at Gulmarg.

Getting around

Srinagar and Jammu have airports. A car with driver is the usual way to travel within the valley.

Tips from our planners

  • Prepaid mobile SIMs from other states may not work in J&K; postpaid connections usually do.
  • Local taxi unions operate at Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg for sightseeing beyond the main town; this is normal and included in most quotes.
