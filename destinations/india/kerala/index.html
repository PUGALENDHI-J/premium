Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Kerala

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Malayalam.

Getting around

Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram have the main airports; Kozhikode serves the north and Wayanad. Distances are short but hill roads are slow, so allow 3–4 hours for most transfers.

Planner's tip

Houseboat cruises usually start around noon and end the next morning; overnight stays have the boat moored at night by regulation.

Planner's tip

Carry light rain gear in any season; afternoon showers are common in the hills.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Kerala

Kerala packs tea-covered hills, spice plantations, wildlife reserves, houseboat-filled backwaters and palm-lined beaches into a narrow strip between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea. The classic route runs Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady and Alleppey, with Kovalam or Varkala added for the beach.

We plan trips to 15 destinations in Kerala: Kochi, Munnar, Alleppey, Kumarakom, Thekkady and Wayanad and more. Highlights include Chinese fishing nets, Eravikulam National Park, Houseboat cruise, Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary and Periyar Lake boat safari. Local food worth trying: Appam with stew, Karimeen pollichathu (pearl spot fish), Seafood at the nets and Kerala sadhya. Our Kerala itineraries run from 3 to 9 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

September to March. Post-monsoon greenery and dry, comfortable days. The monsoon (June to August) is the traditional season for Ayurveda treatments.

Getting around

Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram have the main airports; Kozhikode serves the north and Wayanad. Distances are short but hill roads are slow, so allow 3–4 hours for most transfers.

Travel tips

Kerala travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

September to March. Post-monsoon greenery and dry, comfortable days. The monsoon (June to August) is the traditional season for Ayurveda treatments.

Getting around

Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram have the main airports; Kozhikode serves the north and Wayanad. Distances are short but hill roads are slow, so allow 3–4 hours for most transfers.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, a compact umbrella or rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

Kerala FAQs

What is Kerala famous for?
Kerala is known for Backwaters, Tea hills and Ayurveda. Kerala packs tea-covered hills, spice plantations, wildlife reserves, houseboat-filled backwaters and palm-lined beaches into a narrow strip between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea.
What is the best time to visit Kerala?
September to March. Post-monsoon greenery and dry, comfortable days. The monsoon (June to August) is the traditional season for Ayurveda treatments.
How many days do I need for Kerala?
Our Kerala itineraries run from 3 to 9 days. A first trip usually covers Kochi, Munnar and Alleppey.
Is tap water safe to drink in Kerala?
Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.
What are the emergency numbers in Kerala?
112 (all emergencies).
How much does a Kerala tour package cost?
It depends on your travel month, hotel category, the number of travellers and whether flights are included, so we quote every trip individually. Share your dates and we send a day-by-day plan with a price on WhatsApp.
