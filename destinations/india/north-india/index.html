North India holidays

From the Mughal monuments of Delhi and Agra to the forts of Rajasthan and the snowfields of Kashmir, North India covers the classic first-time circuits and the high Himalaya.

From the Mughal monuments of Delhi and Agra to the forts of Rajasthan and the snowfields of Kashmir, North India covers the classic first-time circuits and the high Himalaya.

Winters (November to February) suit the plains and the desert, while summer is the season for Kashmir, Himachal, Uttarakhand and Ladakh. Many travellers combine both on separate trips.
