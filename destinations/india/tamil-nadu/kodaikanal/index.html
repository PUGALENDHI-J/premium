Kodaikanal

About Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal, the princess of hill stations, sits on a plateau in the Palani Hills around a star-shaped lake. Pine forests, cliff-edge walks and a cool climate make it a favourite honeymoon and summer destination from the southern cities.

Top things to see in Kodaikanal

  • Kodaikanal Lake – Star-shaped lake with boating and a cycling path
  • Coaker's Walk – Paved cliff path with valley views
  • Pillar Rocks – Three tall granite pillars rising from the valley
  • Pine Forest – Tall pines planted in the colonial era
  • Bryant Park – Gardens beside the lake
  • Guna Caves – Deep chasms made famous by a Tamil film

Things to do

BoatingCycling around the lakeHorse ridingShopping for homemade chocolates and eucalyptus oil

What to eat

  • Homemade chocolates
  • Hot corn and bonda by the lake
  • Plum cake

Best time to visit Kodaikanal

October to June. April–May is peak season; the post-monsoon months are green and quieter.

How to reach Kodaikanal

  • By air: Madurai Airport (IXM), about 120 km
  • By rail: Kodaikanal Road station, about 80 km
  • By road: About 3.5 hours from Madurai through the ghat road

Planner's tip

Do Coaker's Walk early in the morning, before the mist closes in on the valley.
