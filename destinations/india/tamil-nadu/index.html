Tamil Nadu overview

Tamil Nadu is home to some of the largest temple complexes on earth, from Madurai's Meenakshi Amman to the Chola temples around Thanjavur. Inland, the Nilgiris and Palani hills offer tea estates and cool weather, and the coast runs from the shore temples of Mahabalipuram to the southern tip at Kanyakumari.

When to go

October to March. The plains are hot from April to June; that is when the Nilgiris and Kodaikanal are at their best.

Getting around

Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Thoothukudi have airports. Trains link every major town, and a car with driver is the easiest way to string temples together.

Tips from our planners

  • Most large temples ask men to remove shirts in the inner sanctum and everyone to remove footwear; carry socks for hot stone floors.
  • Temple inner shrines usually close for a few hours in the early afternoon, so plan visits for the morning or evening.
