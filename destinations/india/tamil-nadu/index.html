Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Tamil Nadu

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Tamil.

Getting around

Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Thoothukudi have airports. Trains link every major town, and a car with driver is the easiest way to string temples together.

Planner's tip

Most large temples ask men to remove shirts in the inner sanctum and everyone to remove footwear; carry socks for hot stone floors.

Planner's tip

Temple inner shrines usually close for a few hours in the early afternoon, so plan visits for the morning or evening.

General guidance, reviewed October 2026. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is home to some of the largest temple complexes on earth, from Madurai's Meenakshi Amman to the Chola temples around Thanjavur. Inland, the Nilgiris and Palani hills offer tea estates and cool weather, and the coast runs from the shore temples of Mahabalipuram to the southern tip at Kanyakumari.

We plan trips to 21 destinations in Tamil Nadu: Chennai, Mahabalipuram, Kanchipuram, Madurai, Rameswaram and Kanyakumari and more. Highlights include Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Shore Temple, Ekambareswarar Temple, Meenakshi Amman Temple and Ramanathaswamy Temple. Local food worth trying: Idli, dosa and filter coffee, Chettinad chicken, Kothu parotta and Sundal on the beach. Our Tamil Nadu itineraries run from 3 to 11 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to March. The plains are hot from April to June; that is when the Nilgiris and Kodaikanal are at their best.

Getting around

Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Thoothukudi have airports. Trains link every major town, and a car with driver is the easiest way to string temples together.

Travel tips

Tamil Nadu travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to March. The plains are hot from April to June; that is when the Nilgiris and Kodaikanal are at their best.

Getting around

Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli and Thoothukudi have airports. Trains link every major town, and a car with driver is the easiest way to string temples together.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

FAQs

Tamil Nadu FAQs

What is Tamil Nadu famous for?
Tamil Nadu is known for Dravidian temples, Nilgiri hill stations and Coromandel coast. Tamil Nadu is home to some of the largest temple complexes on earth, from Madurai's Meenakshi Amman to the Chola temples around Thanjavur.
What is the best time to visit Tamil Nadu?
October to March. The plains are hot from April to June; that is when the Nilgiris and Kodaikanal are at their best.
How many days do I need for Tamil Nadu?
Our Tamil Nadu itineraries run from 3 to 11 days. A first trip usually covers Chennai, Mahabalipuram and Kanchipuram.
Is tap water safe to drink in Tamil Nadu?
Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.
What are the emergency numbers in Tamil Nadu?
