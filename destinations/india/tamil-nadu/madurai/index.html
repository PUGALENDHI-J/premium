Madurai

About Madurai

Madurai is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in India and grew around the Meenakshi Amman Temple, whose fourteen gopurams are covered in thousands of painted figures. The temple's evening ceremonies and the city's jasmine markets make it the soul of Tamil Nadu.

Top things to see in Madurai

  • Meenakshi Amman Temple – Vast temple complex with painted gopurams and the hall of a thousand pillars
  • Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal – Seventeenth-century palace with Indo-Saracenic arches
  • Gandhi Memorial Museum – Museum holding the cloth Gandhi wore when he was shot
  • Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam – Giant temple tank used for the float festival
  • Thiruparankundram – Rock-cut Murugan temple on a hill

Things to do

Night ceremony at Meenakshi templeFlower market walkPalace sound-and-light showStreet food crawl

What to eat

  • Jigarthanda (cold milk dessert)
  • Kari dosa
  • Mutton chukka
  • Paruthi paal

Best time to visit Madurai

October to March. Pleasant temple-visiting weather; the Chithirai festival in April–May is spectacular but hot.

How to reach Madurai

  • By air: Madurai Airport (IXM), about 12 km
  • By rail: Madurai Junction, with direct trains from Chennai
  • By road: About 7–8 hours from Chennai by road

Planner's tip

Visit the temple twice: once in the morning to see the halls, and again at night for the ceremony that carries Shiva to Meenakshi's chamber.
