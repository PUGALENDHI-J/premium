India

East India holidays

East India is where Kolkata's colonial streets, the Sundarbans mangroves, the Sun Temple at Konark and the Buddhist sites of Bodh Gaya meet.

East India is where Kolkata's colonial streets, the Sundarbans mangroves, the Sun Temple at Konark and the Buddhist sites of Bodh Gaya meet. Darjeeling's tea gardens add a cool Himalayan escape.

October to March suits the plains and coast. Darjeeling is at its clearest in October–November and spring.
