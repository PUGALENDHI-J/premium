Goa overview

Goa is India's smallest state and its favourite beach holiday. North Goa has the busier beaches, markets and nightlife; South Goa is quieter, with long sandy beaches and resort stays. Between them, Old Goa's churches and the Latin Quarter of Panaji show four centuries of Portuguese influence.

When to go

November to February. Dry and sunny with calm seas. Many beach shacks close during the monsoon (June to September).

Getting around

Goa has two airports: Dabolim in the south-centre and Manohar International at Mopa in the north. Hire a car with driver or a scooter; public transport is limited.

Tips from our planners

  • Pick your base by mood: North Goa for buzz, South Goa for calm. The drive between them takes 1.5–2 hours.
  • Christmas and New Year week is the most expensive time; early December and late January are better value.

South Goa

South Goa is slower and greener, with long, clean beaches like Colva, Benaulim, Palolem and Agonda, spice farms inland and the UNESCO-listed churches of Old Goa on the way north.
