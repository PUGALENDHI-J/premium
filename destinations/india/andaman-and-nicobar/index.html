Andaman & Nicobar Islands overview

The Andaman Islands sit in the Bay of Bengal closer to Myanmar than to mainland India. White beaches, coral reefs and rainforest make them India's best island holiday, with Havelock (Swaraj Dweep) and Neil (Shaheed Dweep) a short ferry from the capital.

When to go

October to May. Calm seas and clear water for diving and ferries. The monsoon (June to September) cancels some sailings.

Getting around

Flights from Chennai take about two hours. Government and private ferries link the islands; we pre-book private ferry seats.

Tips from our planners

  • Ferries are weather-dependent; avoid booking your flight home on the same day you return from Havelock.
  • Carry cash for the islands; card machines and ATMs can be unreliable outside the capital.
