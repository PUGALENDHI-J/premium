Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, English.

Getting around

Flights from Chennai take about two hours. Government and private ferries link the islands; we pre-book private ferry seats.

Planner's tip

Ferries are weather-dependent; avoid booking your flight home on the same day you return from Havelock.

Planner's tip

Carry cash for the islands; card machines and ATMs can be unreliable outside the capital.

Planner's tip

Do a try-dive on your first full day; if you love it you have time for a second dive.

Everything you need to know about Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

Havelock, renamed Swaraj Dweep, is the Andamans' best-known island, with Radhanagar Beach often ranked among Asia's finest. It is the main base for scuba diving and snorkelling in India.

Highlights include Radhanagar Beach, Elephant Beach, Kalapathar Beach and Dive sites. Local food worth trying: Grilled fish, Seafood curries and Beach café food. Plan 2–3 days here; it suits honeymooners, divers, beach lovers. Our Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) itineraries run from 4 to 9 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to May. Best visibility for diving from November to April.

How to reach Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

  • By air: Veer Savarkar Airport (IXZ), then ferry
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: Private ferries take about 1.5–2.5 hours from Port Blair

Travel tips

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights. Parts of Andaman & Nicobar Islands need an Inner Line Permit or Protected Area Permit for visitors; we arrange these as part of your trip.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to May. Best visibility for diving from November to April.

Getting around

Flights from Chennai take about two hours. Government and private ferries link the islands; we pre-book private ferry seats.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
