Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Chhattisgarh

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi.

Getting around

Fly into Raipur. Jagdalpur, the base for Bastar, is about 6 hours by road or a short flight when operating.

Planner's tip

Visit a weekly haat (market) in Bastar; they are colourful and the dhokra metalwork is excellent.

Planner's tip

Some forest areas have restricted access; we plan routes using local advice at the time of travel.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Chhattisgarh

Chhattisgarh is central India's least-visited state and one of its most rewarding for curious travellers. Bastar in the south has Chitrakote, the widest waterfall in India, limestone caves, weekly tribal haats and the famous Dussehra that lasts 75 days, while Raipur and Sirpur hold temples and Buddhist ruins.

We plan trips to 2 destinations in Chhattisgarh: Raipur and Jagdalpur (Bastar). Highlights include Sirpur and Chitrakote Falls. Local food worth trying: Chila, Faraa, Bafauri and Chapda chutney (red ant chutney). Our Chhattisgarh itineraries run from 5 to 5 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to February. Comfortable weather; Chitrakote is at its fullest from August to October.

Getting around

Fly into Raipur. Jagdalpur, the base for Bastar, is about 6 hours by road or a short flight when operating.

Travel tips

Chhattisgarh travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to February. Comfortable weather; Chitrakote is at its fullest from August to October.

Getting around

Fly into Raipur. Jagdalpur, the base for Bastar, is about 6 hours by road or a short flight when operating.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
