Odisha

Puri

Best time: October to March  ·  Ideal stay: 1–2 days

About Puri

Puri is one of the Char Dham pilgrimages and home to the Jagannath Temple, famous for its annual Rath Yatra. It also has a long beach on the Bay of Bengal, where sand artists work on the shore.

Top things to see in Puri

  • Jagannath Temple – Twelfth-century temple (Hindus only)
  • Puri Beach – Long beach and sand art
  • Gundicha Temple – Destination of the Rath Yatra
  • Raghurajpur – Heritage crafts village of pattachitra painters
  • Chilika Lake (Satapada) – Dolphin-spotting boat rides

Things to do

Rath Yatra (June/July)Pattachitra workshopBeach sunriseTemple visit

What to eat

  • Mahaprasad
  • Khaja
  • Chhena poda
  • Odia fish curry

Best time to visit Puri

October to March. Pleasant beach weather; the Rath Yatra is in June or July.

How to reach Puri

  • By air: Biju Patnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar (BBI), about 60 km
  • By rail: Puri station
  • By road: About 1.5 hours from Bhubaneswar

Planner's tip

Visit Raghurajpur village to see pattachitra paintings made by families of artists.

Puri FAQs

What is the best time to visit Puri?
October to March. Pleasant beach weather; the Rath Yatra is in June or July.
How many days are enough for Puri?
Plan 1–2 days to see the main sights without rushing. Our Puri itineraries range from 4 to 6 days in total, usually combined with nearby places.
How do I reach Puri?
Biju Patnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar (BBI), about 60 km. Puri station.
