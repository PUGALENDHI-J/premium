South India holidays

South India is our home ground.

South India is our home ground. Temple towns with towering gopurams, the tea hills of Munnar and the Nilgiris, Kerala's backwaters and the boulder-strewn ruins of Hampi are all within a day's travel of Chennai.

The region is warm most of the year. October to March is the most comfortable time for the plains and coast, while the hill stations are a cool escape in April and May.
