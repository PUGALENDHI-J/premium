Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Varanasi

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Hindi, Urdu.

Getting around

Agra, Varanasi, Lucknow and Ayodhya have airports. The Yamuna Expressway links Delhi and Agra in about three and a half hours.

Planner's tip

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays; sunrise visits are the least crowded.

Planner's tip

In Varanasi, a boat on the Ganga at dawn is the single most memorable experience; book it for your first morning.

Planner's tip

Take the dawn boat on your first morning; the ghats are at their most atmospheric then.

Everything you need to know about Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the oldest living cities in the world and the holiest place in Hinduism. Life, prayer and death happen side by side on its ghats along the Ganga, and the evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat is unforgettable.

Highlights include Dashashwamedh Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath Temple and corridor, Manikarnika Ghat and Sarnath. Local food worth trying: Kachori sabzi, Banarasi paan, Malaiyyo (winter) and Lassi in clay cups. Plan 2 days here; it suits spiritual travellers, photographers, culture lovers. Our Varanasi itineraries run from 3 to 8 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to March. Dev Deepawali (November) lights up every ghat with lamps.

How to reach Varanasi

  • By air: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS), about 25 km
  • By rail: Varanasi Junction and Banaras stations
  • By road: About 5 hours from Prayagraj or Ayodhya region by road

Travel tips

Varanasi travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to March. Dev Deepawali (November) lights up every ghat with lamps.

Getting around

Agra, Varanasi, Lucknow and Ayodhya have airports. The Yamuna Expressway links Delhi and Agra in about three and a half hours.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
