Andhra Pradesh overview

Andhra Pradesh is best known for the Tirumala Venkateswara temple, one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in the world, but it also has the beaches and hills of Visakhapatnam, the coffee-growing Araku Valley and long stretches of the Bay of Bengal coast.

When to go

October to March. Comfortable temperatures along the coast and in the Eastern Ghats.

Getting around

Tirupati and Visakhapatnam have airports. The Kirandul line from Visakhapatnam to Araku is one of India's most scenic train rides.

Tips from our planners

  • Book Tirumala darshan slots online well ahead; special-entry tickets sell out quickly in festival months.
  • Choose the vistadome coach on the Araku train for glass-roof views through the tunnels.
