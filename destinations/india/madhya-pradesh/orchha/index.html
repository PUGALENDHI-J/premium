Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Orchha

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Hindi.

Getting around

Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur and Khajuraho have airports. Overnight trains are handy for the longer hops between parks.

Planner's tip

Core zones of the tiger reserves close one day a week (usually Wednesday afternoon) and during the monsoon.

Planner's tip

Combine Khajuraho with Orchha; they are about four hours apart by road.

Planner's tip

Arrive on the Gatimaan or Shatabdi to Jhansi and add Orchha on the way to Khajuraho.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Orchha

Orchha is a small medieval town on the Betwa river, with Bundela palaces, temples and riverside chhatris that are hauntingly quiet compared to Rajasthan. Its Ram Raja Temple is unique in worshipping Rama as a king.

Highlights include Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal, Ram Raja Temple and Chaturbhuj Temple. Local food worth trying: Bundelkhandi thali. Plan 1 day here; it suits heritage lovers, photographers. Our Orchha itineraries run from 4 to 5 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to March. Comfortable winter days.

How to reach Orchha

  • By air: Gwalior (GWL), about 120 km
  • By rail: Jhansi Junction, about 16 km
  • By road: About 20 minutes from Jhansi

Travel tips

Orchha travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to March. Comfortable winter days.

Getting around

Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur and Khajuraho have airports. Overnight trains are handy for the longer hops between parks.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
