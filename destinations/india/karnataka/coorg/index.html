Karnataka

Coorg

Best time: October to March  ·  Ideal stay: 2–3 days

About Coorg

Coorg (Kodagu) is Karnataka's coffee country, a district of misty hills, estates shaded by silver oak and waterfalls in the forest. The Kodava people have their own culture, cuisine and martial traditions, and homestays on working estates are the best way to experience it.

Top things to see in Coorg

  • Abbey Falls – Waterfall set in coffee and spice plantations
  • Raja's Seat – Sunset viewpoint over the valleys
  • Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple) – Tibetan monastery at Bylakuppe
  • Dubare Elephant Camp – Riverside elephant camp
  • Talacauvery – Source of the Kaveri River
  • Mandalpatti – Jeep ride to a hilltop viewpoint

Things to do

Coffee estate tourWhite-water rafting at Barapole (seasonal)Trek to TadiandamolEstate homestayElephant camp visit

What to eat

  • Pandi curry (Kodava pork)
  • Akki roti
  • Fresh estate coffee

Best time to visit Coorg

October to March. Clear skies; coffee blossoms in March–April. Monsoon is lush but leech season.

How to reach Coorg

  • By air: Mangaluru International Airport (IXE), about 140 km, or Kannur (CNN), about 90 km
  • By rail: Mysuru Junction, about 120 km
  • By road: About 5–6 hours from Bengaluru

Planner's tip

Stay on a working coffee estate; the hosts often include a plantation walk.

October to March. Clear skies; coffee blossoms in March–April. Monsoon is lush but leech season.

Mangaluru International Airport (IXE), about 140 km, or Kannur (CNN), about 90 km. Mysuru Junction, about 120 km.
