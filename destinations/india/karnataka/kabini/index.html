About Kabini

Kabini is a river and reservoir on the edge of Nagarhole National Park, known for large elephant herds in summer and the famous black panther. Lodges on the riverbank offer jeep and boat safaris in a quiet forest setting.

Top things to see in Kabini

  • Nagarhole jeep safari – Forest drives for elephants, tigers and leopards
  • Kabini river boat safari – Wildlife along the backwaters
  • Kabini reservoir – Elephants gather on the shores in summer
  • Kakanakote forest – Historic elephant-capture site
  • Birdwatching trails – Over 250 bird species recorded

Things to do

Jeep safariBoat safariCoracle ridesNature walks with naturalists

What to eat

  • Resort buffets
  • Coorgi and Mysuru dishes

Best time to visit Kabini

October to May. Summer (March–May) brings the biggest elephant gatherings.

How to reach Kabini

  • By air: Mysore Airport (limited flights); Bengaluru (BLR), about 230 km
  • By rail: Mysuru Junction, about 80 km
  • By road: About 5 hours from Bengaluru

Planner's tip

Book lodges that include safaris; park safari slots are limited and sell out.
