Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Mysuru

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Kannada.

Getting around

Bengaluru is the main airport, with Mangaluru and Hubballi for the coast and north. Overnight trains reach Hospet (for Hampi) from Bengaluru.

Planner's tip

Mysuru Palace is lit with nearly 100,000 bulbs on Sunday and public-holiday evenings for a short window.

Planner's tip

Hampi is spread out; hire a guide for the first day and a bicycle or auto-rickshaw for the second.

Planner's tip

Visit the palace by day for the interiors and come back on a Sunday evening for the lights.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Mysuru

Mysuru (Mysore) was the seat of the Wodeyar kings, and its palace is one of the most visited monuments in India. The city is known for sandalwood, silk, yoga and the ten-day Dasara festival, when the palace is lit up every night.

Highlights include Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, Brindavan Gardens and St Philomena's Church. Local food worth trying: Mysore pak, Mysore masala dosa and Nanjangud bananas. Plan 1–2 days here; it suits families, heritage and festival lovers. Our Mysuru itineraries run from 3 to 9 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to February. Dasara (September–October) is the highlight; winters are mild.

How to reach Mysuru

  • By air: Mysore Airport has limited flights; Bengaluru (BLR) is about 170 km
  • By rail: Mysuru Junction, with frequent trains from Bengaluru
  • By road: About 3 hours from Bengaluru by expressway

Travel tips

Mysuru travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

October to February. Dasara (September–October) is the highlight; winters are mild.

Getting around

Bengaluru is the main airport, with Mangaluru and Hubballi for the coast and north. Overnight trains reach Hospet (for Hampi) from Bengaluru.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
