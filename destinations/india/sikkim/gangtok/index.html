Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Gangtok

Currency and payments

UPI and cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; keep some cash for small shops, rural areas, hill towns and tips.

Tipping

Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Emergency numbers

112 (all emergencies)

Language

Languages spoken: Nepali, Sikkimese, Lepcha.

Getting around

Fly to Bagdogra (or Pakyong, with limited flights) and drive 4–5 hours to Gangtok. Permits are needed for North Sikkim, Tsomgo Lake and Nathula; we arrange them.

Planner's tip

Plastic water bottles are restricted in parts of Sikkim; carry a refillable bottle.

Planner's tip

Nathula Pass is closed to visitors on Mondays and Tuesdays.

Planner's tip

Carry ID photos and copies; permits for Tsomgo and Nathula are issued a day ahead.

General guidance, reviewed October 2026. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Gangtok

Gangtok is Sikkim's clean, lively capital on a ridge facing Kanchenjunga. MG Marg is car-free, monasteries like Rumtek sit on the surrounding hills, and day trips go up to Tsomgo Lake and the Nathula Pass on the China border.

Highlights include MG Marg, Tsomgo (Changu) Lake, Nathula Pass and Rumtek Monastery. Local food worth trying: Momos, Thukpa, Sha phaley and Gundruk soup. Plan 3 days here; it suits families, honeymooners. Our Gangtok itineraries run from 4 to 8 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

March to May and October to December. Clear views in autumn; spring flowers.

How to reach Gangtok

  • By air: Pakyong Airport (PYG), about 35 km (limited flights); Bagdogra (IXB), about 125 km
  • By rail: New Jalpaiguri (NJP), about 120 km
  • By road: About 4–5 hours from Bagdogra

Travel tips

Gangtok travel tips

Visa and entry

Indian citizens do not need a visa; carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or a passport for hotels and flights. Parts of Sikkim need an Inner Line Permit or Protected Area Permit for visitors; we arrange these as part of your trip.

Health and safety

Travel insurance is a good idea, especially for adventure activities. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. Some places here are at high altitude: rest on arrival, drink plenty of water and ascend gradually.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indian rupee (INR). Tipping is not compulsory; around 5–10% in restaurants and a small tip for drivers and guides is appreciated.

Best time to visit

March to May and October to December. Clear views in autumn; spring flowers.

Getting around

Fly to Bagdogra (or Pakyong, with limited flights) and drive 4–5 hours to Gangtok. Permits are needed for North Sikkim, Tsomgo Lake and Nathula; we arrange them.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack warm layers, a down jacket and gloves, a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Mobile data is good in towns and cities. Coverage can be patchy in remote, hill and forest areas, so download offline maps before you set off.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

FAQs

Gangtok FAQs

What is Gangtok famous for?
Gangtok is known for MG Marg, Tsomgo (Changu) Lake, Nathula Pass and Rumtek Monastery. Gangtok is Sikkim's clean, lively capital on a ridge facing Kanchenjunga.
What is the best time to visit Gangtok?
March to May and October to December. Clear views in autumn; spring flowers.
How many days are enough for Gangtok?
Plan 3 days to see the main sights without rushing. Our itineraries that include Gangtok run from 4 to 8 days, usually combined with nearby places.
How do I reach Gangtok?
Pakyong Airport (PYG), about 35 km (limited flights); Bagdogra (IXB), about 125 km. New Jalpaiguri (NJP), about 120 km. About 4–5 hours from Bagdogra.
Is tap water safe to drink in Sikkim?
Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.
Do I need a permit to visit Gangtok?
Parts of Sikkim require an Inner Line Permit or Protected Area Permit for visitors. Requirements depend on where you go; we arrange the permits for your route.
What are the emergency numbers in Sikkim?
112 (all emergencies).
How much does a Gangtok tour package cost?
