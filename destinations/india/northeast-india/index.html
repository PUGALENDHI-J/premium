Northeast India holidays

The Northeast is India's least-visited corner and one of its most rewarding: living-root bridges in Meghalaya, one-horned rhinos at Kaziranga, monasteries in Sikkim and Tawang, and the Hornbill Festival in Nagaland.

The Northeast is India's least-visited corner and one of its most rewarding: living-root bridges in Meghalaya, one-horned rhinos at Kaziranga, monasteries in Sikkim and Tawang, and the Hornbill Festival in Nagaland. We also group India's two island territories, the Andamans and Lakshadweep, here because they are planned the same way, with permits and limited connections.

October to April is the best window for most of the region. Some areas need an Inner Line Permit or Protected Area Permit, which we arrange.
