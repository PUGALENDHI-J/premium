Europe holidays

Europe rewards every travel style: Swiss mountain railways, Paris and Rome for art and history, Greek islands for beaches, Scandinavia for fjords and the northern lights, and Turkey for bazaars and balloon flights.

Europe rewards every travel style: Swiss mountain railways, Paris and Rome for art and history, Greek islands for beaches, Scandinavia for fjords and the northern lights, and Turkey for bazaars and balloon flights. Excellent trains make multi-country trips easy.

May to September is the main season, with July and August the busiest. Spring and early autumn are quieter and better value; December brings Christmas markets and Lapland's snow.
