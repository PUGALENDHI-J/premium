Greece overview

Greece combines the ancient monuments of Athens and the Acropolis with island life: Santorini's white villages on volcanic cliffs, Mykonos's windmills and beach clubs, and dozens of quieter islands linked by ferry.

When to go

May to June and September to October. Warm seas with fewer crowds than July–August.

Getting around

Ferries and short flights link Athens with the islands; fast ferries reach Santorini in about five hours.

Tips from our planners

  • Santorini sunsets in Oia draw huge crowds; book a caldera-view hotel or a sunset cruise instead.
  • Ferry schedules shrink outside summer; we confirm routes when booking.

Good to know

Greece travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Greece?
May to June and September to October. Warm seas with fewer crowds than July–August.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Greece?
Most first-time visitors pick Athens, Santorini and Mykonos. Our shortest Greece itinerary is 5 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Greece?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Greece?
Euro (EUR)
