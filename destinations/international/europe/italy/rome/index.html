About Rome

Rome is three thousand years of history in one city: the Colosseum and Roman Forum, the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain and Vatican City with St Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel.

Top things to see in Rome

  • Colosseum and Roman Forum – Ancient Rome
  • Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel – Michelangelo's ceiling
  • St Peter's Basilica – Largest church
  • Trevi Fountain – Baroque fountain
  • Pantheon – Ancient temple
  • Spanish Steps – Famous staircase

Things to do

Guided Colosseum tourVatican visitGelato tastingTrastevere food walk

What to eat

  • Cacio e pepe
  • Pizza al taglio
  • Gelato
  • Supplì

Best time to visit Rome

April to June and September to October. Pleasant; summer is hot and crowded.

How to reach Rome

  • By air: Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO)
  • By rail: Roma Termini station
  • By road: High-speed trains to Florence and Naples

Planner's tip

Toss a coin in the Trevi early in the morning before crowds arrive.
