Paris

Best time: April to June and September to October  ·  Ideal stay: 3–4 days

About Paris

Paris needs little introduction: the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre and the Mona Lisa, Notre-Dame (reopened in December 2024), Montmartre and the Seine. Disneyland Paris is a short train ride away.

Top things to see in Paris

  • Eiffel Tower – Summit and second-floor views
  • Louvre Museum – The Mona Lisa and much more
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral – Restored Gothic cathedral
  • Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur – Artists' hill
  • Champs-Élysées and Arc de Triomphe – Grand avenue
  • Disneyland Paris – Two theme parks east of the city

Things to do

Seine river cruisePalace of Versailles day tripCabaret showShopping in Le Marais

What to eat

  • Croissants
  • Crêpes
  • Macarons
  • Indian food near Gare du Nord

Best time to visit Paris

April to June and September to October. Mild and less crowded than mid-summer.

How to reach Paris

  • By air: Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)
  • By rail: Gare du Nord and Gare de Lyon for trains
  • By road: Eurostar to London; TGV to Switzerland
