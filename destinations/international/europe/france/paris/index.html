Everything you need to know about Paris

Paris needs little introduction: the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre and the Mona Lisa, Notre-Dame (reopened in December 2024), Montmartre and the Seine. Disneyland Paris is a short train ride away.

Highlights include Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral and Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur. Local food worth trying: Croissants, Crêpes, Macarons and Indian food near Gare du Nord. Plan 3–4 days here; it suits honeymooners, families, culture lovers. Our Paris itineraries run from 5 to 15 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

April to June and September to October. Mild and less crowded than mid-summer.

How to reach Paris

  • By air: Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)
  • By rail: Gare du Nord and Gare de Lyon for trains
  • By road: Eurostar to London; TGV to Switzerland

Travel tips

Paris travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for France before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in France.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Euro (EUR). Service is included by law (service compris); leaving small change is optional.

Best time to visit

April to June and September to October. Mild and less crowded than mid-summer.

Getting around

TGV high-speed trains link Paris to Nice, Lyon and onward to Switzerland and Italy.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
