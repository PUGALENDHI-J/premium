Austria overview

Austria pairs imperial Vienna's palaces and coffee houses with Mozart's Salzburg, the lake village of Hallstatt and the Tyrolean Alps around Innsbruck. It combines naturally with Switzerland, Germany and Hungary.

When to go

May to September; December for Christmas markets and skiing. Summer for lakes and hiking; winter for snow and festive markets.

Getting around

Railjet trains link Vienna, Salzburg and Innsbruck; buses reach Hallstatt.

Tips from our planners

  • Hallstatt limits tour buses; visit early or stay overnight.
  • Book a classical concert in Vienna; there are performances most evenings.

Good to know

Austria travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Austria?
May to September; December for Christmas markets and skiing. Summer for lakes and hiking; winter for snow and festive markets.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Austria?
Most first-time visitors pick Vienna, Salzburg & Hallstatt and Innsbruck & Tyrol. Our shortest Austria itinerary is 6 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Austria?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Austria?
Euro (EUR)
