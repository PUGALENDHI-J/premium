Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to United Kingdom

Currency and payments

Pound sterling (GBP). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

10–12.5% in restaurants, often added to the bill as a service charge.

Drinking water

Tap water is generally safe to drink.

Power plugs

Type G · 230 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter.

Emergency numbers

999 or 112

Language

Languages spoken: English.

Getting around

Trains link London with Edinburgh in about 4.5 hours; a car is best for the Highlands and countryside.

Planner's tip

Many major London museums are free; spend the savings on a West End show.

Planner's tip

Use contactless cards on London transport; daily caps apply automatically.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Travel tips

United Kingdom travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for United Kingdom before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in United Kingdom.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Pound sterling (GBP). 10–12.5% in restaurants, often added to the bill as a service charge.

Best time to visit

May to September. Long days and the best chance of good weather.

Getting around

Trains link London with Edinburgh in about 4.5 hours; a car is best for the Highlands and countryside.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites, comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
