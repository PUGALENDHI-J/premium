Cappadocia

Best time: April to June and September to November  ·  Ideal stay: 2 days

About Cappadocia

Cappadocia is a landscape of fairy chimneys, cave churches and underground cities, best seen from a hot-air balloon at sunrise. Cave hotels add to the magic.

Top things to see in Cappadocia

  • Hot-air balloon flight – Sunrise over the valleys
  • Göreme Open-Air Museum – Rock-cut churches
  • Derinkuyu Underground City – Multi-level underground city
  • Love Valley and Pigeon Valley – Hiking trails
  • Uçhisar Castle – Rock fortress

Things to do

Balloon flightATV tourValley hikesPottery in Avanos

What to eat

  • Testi kebab
  • Gözleme
  • Dried apricots

Best time to visit Cappadocia

April to June and September to November. Best balloon weather.

How to reach Cappadocia

  • By air: Kayseri (ASR) or Nevşehir (NAV) airports
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: About 1 hour by road from either airport

Planner's tip

Book two nights so you have a back-up morning for the balloon.
