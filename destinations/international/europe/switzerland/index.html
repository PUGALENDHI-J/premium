Switzerland overview

Switzerland is the Alps at their most polished: lakeside Lucerne, Interlaken between two lakes, the car-free village of Zermatt under the Matterhorn and Jungfraujoch, the top of Europe. Panoramic trains such as the Glacier Express and GoldenPass make travel part of the holiday.

When to go

June to September; December to March for snow. Green summers for hiking and lakes; winter for snow and skiing.

Getting around

Swiss trains are punctual and scenic; a Swiss Travel Pass often covers trains, boats, buses and museum entry.

Tips from our planners

  • Book panoramic train seat reservations early in summer.
  • Weather changes fast at altitude; check webcams before going up Jungfraujoch or Titlis.

Most first-time visitors pick Lucerne, Interlaken & Jungfrau, Zermatt and Zurich. Our shortest Switzerland itinerary is 5 days.
