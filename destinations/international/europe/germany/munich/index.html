Essential tips for travelling to Munich & Bavaria

Currency and payments

Euro (EUR). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Round up or add 5–10% in restaurants.

Drinking water

Tap water is generally safe to drink.

Power plugs

Types C and F · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

112 (ambulance and fire), 110 (police)

Language

Languages spoken: German.

Getting around

ICE high-speed trains connect the main cities; regional passes are good value for day trips.

Planner's tip

Neuschwanstein tickets are timed and sell out in summer.

Planner's tip

Everything you need to know about Munich & Bavaria

Munich is Bavaria's capital, known for its beer halls, Marienplatz's glockenspiel, the BMW Museum and Oktoberfest. Neuschwanstein Castle, the inspiration for Disney's castle, is a day trip away.

Highlights include Marienplatz, Neuschwanstein Castle, Nymphenburg Palace and BMW Welt and Museum. Local food worth trying: Pretzels, Weisswurst and Apple strudel. Plan 2–3 days here; it suits families, festival lovers. Our Munich & Bavaria itineraries run from 5 to 7 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

May to October; December for markets. Oktoberfest begins in late September.

How to reach Munich & Bavaria

  • By air: Munich Airport (MUC)
  • By rail: München Hauptbahnhof
  • By road: Trains to Salzburg and Innsbruck

Travel tips

Munich & Bavaria travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Germany before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Germany.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Euro (EUR). Round up or add 5–10% in restaurants.

Best time to visit

May to October; December for markets. Oktoberfest begins in late September.

Getting around

ICE high-speed trains connect the main cities; regional passes are good value for day trips.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
