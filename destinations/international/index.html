International holidays

From Chennai, Bangkok and Singapore are about four hours away, Dubai about four and a half, and Bali, Kuala Lumpur and Colombo are similarly easy.

From Chennai, Bangkok and Singapore are about four hours away, Dubai about four and a half, and Bali, Kuala Lumpur and Colombo are similarly easy. Further afield, Europe, Japan and Australia are a one-stop flight. That makes international holidays far more accessible than most people expect, whether it is a five-day beach break or a two-week multi-country tour.

We plan every international trip as a private itinerary: flights from Chennai or your city, hotels, transfers, sightseeing and visa guidance in one written quote. Entry rules for Indian passport holders change regularly, so we always check the official source when you book rather than relying on old information.
