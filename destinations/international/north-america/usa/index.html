Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to United States

Currency and payments

US dollar (USD). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

15–20% in restaurants is expected; tips are also usual for taxis and hotel staff.

Drinking water

Tap water is generally safe to drink.

Power plugs

Types A and B · 120 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter. Check that your chargers accept 100–240 V.

Emergency numbers

911

Language

Languages spoken: English.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect regions; rent a car or use chauffeured transfers for national parks.

Planner's tip

US visa appointment waits can be long; apply as early as possible.

Planner's tip

Tipping of 15–20% is expected in restaurants and for many services.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Travel tips

United States travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for United States before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in United States.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the US dollar (USD). 15–20% in restaurants is expected; tips are also usual for taxis and hotel staff.

Best time to visit

April to June and September to October. Mild weather in most regions; summer suits national parks and Alaska.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect regions; rent a car or use chauffeured transfers for national parks.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
