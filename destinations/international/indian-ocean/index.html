Indian Ocean Islands holidays

The Maldives, Mauritius and Seychelles are the classic island honeymoons: turquoise lagoons, coral reefs and resorts designed for doing very little.

The Maldives, Mauritius and Seychelles are the classic island honeymoons: turquoise lagoons, coral reefs and resorts designed for doing very little. Each has its own character, from the Maldives' one-island-one-resort model to the volcanic landscapes of Mauritius and the granite boulders of the Seychelles.

The Maldives is a short flight from South India. The best weather across the region is roughly November to April.

Maldives

The Maldives is a chain of coral atolls in the Indian Ocean, about an hour's flight from Thiruvananthapuram or Bengaluru, where each resort usually occupies its own island.

Best timeNovember to April

Mauritius

Mauritius is a volcanic island ringed by coral reefs, with a strong Indian heritage: most Mauritians have Indian ancestry and Hindi, Bhojpuri and Tamil are widely heard.

Best timeMay to December

Seychelles

The Seychelles are 115 islands in the western Indian Ocean, famous for granite boulders on white beaches, giant tortoises and the coco de mer palm.

Best timeApril to May and October to November
