Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Maldives

Currency and payments

Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR); US dollars used at resorts. Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Resorts add a 10% service charge; extra tips for staff are optional.

Drinking water

Drink the bottled or filtered water provided by your resort.

Power plugs

Type G (also D, J, K, L) · 230 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter.

Emergency numbers

119 (police), 102 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Dhivehi, English.

Getting around

Resorts arrange transfers from Velana Airport by speedboat, seaplane or domestic flight. Local islands with guesthouses are a budget alternative.

Planner's tip

Transfer type changes the price a lot: seaplanes are scenic but costly and fly only in daylight.

Planner's tip

Choose a resort with a good house reef if snorkelling matters to you.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Maldives

The Maldives is a chain of coral atolls in the Indian Ocean, about an hour's flight from Thiruvananthapuram or Bengaluru, where each resort usually occupies its own island. Overwater villas, house reefs full of fish and sandbank dinners make it India's favourite luxury honeymoon.

Highlights include Overwater villa stay, House reef snorkelling, Sandbank picnic and Malé city. Local food worth trying: Mas huni (tuna and coconut), Garudhiya (fish soup) and Resort international dining. Plan 4–5 days here; it suits honeymooners, luxury travellers, divers. Our Maldives itineraries run from 4 to 8 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

November to April. Dry season with calm seas; May to October is greener, cheaper and has more rain.

How to reach Maldives

  • By air: Velana International Airport (MLE), direct flights from several Indian cities
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: Speedboat, seaplane or domestic flight to the resort

Travel tips

Maldives travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Maldives before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Maldives.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR); US dollars used at resorts. Resorts add a 10% service charge; extra tips for staff are optional.

Best time to visit

November to April. Dry season with calm seas; May to October is greener, cheaper and has more rain.

Getting around

Resorts arrange transfers from Velana Airport by speedboat, seaplane or domestic flight. Local islands with guesthouses are a budget alternative.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, a compact umbrella or rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
