Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Mauritius

Currency and payments

Mauritian rupee (MUR). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not obligatory but appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types C and G · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

999 (police), 114 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: English, French, Mauritian Creole.

Getting around

Hire a car with driver for island tours; the island is small and most sights are within 1.5 hours.

Planner's tip

The east coast is breezier in winter (June–August); the west coast is calmer.

Planner's tip

Visit Grand Bassin (Ganga Talao) during Maha Shivaratri for a huge pilgrimage.

Everything you need to know about Mauritius

Mauritius is a volcanic island ringed by coral reefs, with a strong Indian heritage: most Mauritians have Indian ancestry and Hindi, Bhojpuri and Tamil are widely heard. Lagoon beaches, Chamarel's coloured earth, Grand Bassin's sacred lake and Île aux Cerfs make it a honeymoon and family favourite.

Highlights include Île aux Cerfs, Chamarel Seven Coloured Earth, Grand Bassin (Ganga Talao) and Black River Gorges National Park. Local food worth trying: Dholl puri, Mauritian curries and Seafood rougaille. Plan 5–6 days here; it suits honeymooners, families. Our Mauritius itineraries run from 4 to 9 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

May to December. Cooler, drier months; January to March is cyclone season.

How to reach Mauritius

  • By air: Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU)
  • By rail: No railway; Metro Express in Port Louis area
  • By road: Island roads; about 1.5 hours end to end

Travel tips

Mauritius travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Mauritius before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Mauritius.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Mauritian rupee (MUR). Not obligatory but appreciated.

Best time to visit

May to December. Cooler, drier months; January to March is cyclone season.

Getting around

Hire a car with driver for island tours; the island is small and most sights are within 1.5 hours.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
