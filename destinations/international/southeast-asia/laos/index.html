Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Laos

Currency and payments

Lao kip (LAK). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not traditional but appreciated in tourist areas.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types A, B, C, E and F · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

1191 (police), 1195 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Lao.

Getting around

The Laos–China Railway connects Vientiane, Vang Vieng and Luang Prabang in a few hours. Fly into Luang Prabang or Vientiane via Bangkok.

Planner's tip

Watch the morning alms-giving in Luang Prabang quietly and from a respectful distance.

Planner's tip

Combine Laos with Thailand or Cambodia for a longer Mekong journey.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Travel tips

Laos travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Laos before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Laos.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Lao kip (LAK). Not traditional but appreciated in tourist areas.

Best time to visit

November to March. Cool, dry days and clear rivers.

Getting around

The Laos–China Railway connects Vientiane, Vang Vieng and Luang Prabang in a few hours. Fly into Luang Prabang or Vientiane via Bangkok.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.
  • Watch the morning alms-giving in Luang Prabang quietly and from a respectful distance.

What to pack

Pack comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
