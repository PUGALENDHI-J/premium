Krabi

Best time: November to April  ·  Ideal stay: 3 days

About Krabi

Krabi is a coastal province famous for dramatic limestone cliffs, clear water and beautiful offshore islands. Ao Nang is the main base; Railay is a cliff-backed peninsula reached only by longtail boat.

Top things to see in Krabi

  • Railay Beach – Cliff-backed beach reached by boat
  • Ao Nang – Main beach town
  • Hong Islands – Lagoons and snorkelling
  • Four Islands tour – Poda, Chicken, Tup and Phra Nang Cave
  • Emerald Pool – Natural pool in the forest
  • Tiger Cave Temple – 1,260 steps to a hilltop shrine

Things to do

Island hopping by longtailRock climbingKayakingSnorkellingSunset at Railay West

What to eat

  • Seafood
  • Southern Thai curries
  • Roti pancakes

Best time to visit Krabi

November to April. Dry season with clear water; some boat trips stop in the monsoon.

How to reach Krabi

  • By air: Krabi International Airport (KBV)
  • By rail: No railway (nearest is Surat Thani)
  • By road: About 2.5 hours from Phuket by road

Planner's tip

Take the earliest boat to the Four Islands to beat the day-trip crowds.
