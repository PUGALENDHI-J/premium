Chiang Rai

Best time: November to February  ·  Ideal stay: 1–2 days

About Chiang Rai

Chiang Rai is a northern destination known for striking contemporary temples, mountain scenery and distinctive local culture. The White Temple, Blue Temple and Black House are all modern works by Thai artists.

Top things to see in Chiang Rai

  • Wat Rong Khun (White Temple) – Dazzling white contemporary temple
  • Wat Rong Suea Ten (Blue Temple) – Sapphire-blue interior
  • Baan Dam Museum (Black House) – Dark artistic compound
  • Golden Triangle – Where Thailand, Laos and Myanmar meet
  • Doi Tung and Mae Fah Luang Garden – Royal gardens in the hills

Things to do

Temple toursScenic drivesGolden Triangle boat rideTea plantations at Choui Fong

What to eat

  • Khao soi
  • Northern Thai sausage
  • Local tea

Best time to visit Chiang Rai

November to February. Cool and clear; burning season haze in March and April.

How to reach Chiang Rai

  • By air: Mae Fah Luang–Chiang Rai International Airport (CEI)
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: About 3 hours from Chiang Mai by road

Planner's tip

Do the White Temple early; it is busiest by late morning.
