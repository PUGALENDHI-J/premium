Pattaya

Best time: November to February  ·  Ideal stay: 2–3 days

About Pattaya

Pattaya is a seaside city two hours from Bangkok with island trips, cabaret shows, family attractions and a famous nightlife strip. It is the most popular stop for Indian groups and our own friends' batches, with Coral Island a short speedboat ride away.

Top things to see in Pattaya

  • Coral Island (Koh Larn) – Speedboat trips, beaches and water sports
  • Sanctuary of Truth – Giant hand-carved wooden temple
  • Alcazar Cabaret Show – Pattaya's famous stage show
  • Nong Nooch Tropical Garden – Landscaped gardens and cultural shows
  • Walking Street – Nightlife and street entertainment
  • Tiger Topia – Tiger park and animal shows

Things to do

Speedboat to Coral IslandParasailing and jet skisFloating marketCabaret showNight markets

What to eat

  • Seafood barbecue
  • Thai curries
  • Many Indian restaurants on Beach Road

Best time to visit Pattaya

November to February. Sunny days and calm seas for island trips.

How to reach Pattaya

  • By air: U-Tapao Airport (UTP), about 40 km; Suvarnabhumi (BKK), about 120 km
  • By rail: Pattaya railway station (limited service)
  • By road: About 2 hours from Bangkok by road

Planner's tip

Do Coral Island on a weekday morning; the beaches get crowded after noon and on weekends.
