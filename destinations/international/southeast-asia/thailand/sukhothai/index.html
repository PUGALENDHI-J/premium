Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Sukhothai Historical Park

Currency and payments

Thai baht (THB). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not compulsory; leaving small change or 20–100 baht for good service is appreciated.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types A, B, C and O · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

1155 (tourist police), 191 (police), 1669 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Thai.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bangkok with Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai and Koh Samui in about an hour. Private transfers are the easiest way to travel between Bangkok and Pattaya; ferries and speedboats link the islands.

Planner's tip

Fill in the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online before you land.

Planner's tip

Cover shoulders and knees at temples and the Grand Palace, and remove shoes where asked.

Planner's tip

Phuket and Krabi are best from November to April; plan the Gulf islands instead in July and August.

Planner's tip

Rent bicycles at the park gate; the central zone is flat and shaded.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Sukhothai Historical Park

Sukhothai preserves the remains of the first Thai kingdom, from the 13th century, with ancient temples, Buddha statues and tranquil ponds set in parkland. It is quieter than Ayutthaya and best explored by bicycle.

Highlights include Wat Mahathat, Wat Si Chum, Wat Sa Si and Ramkhamhaeng National Museum. Local food worth trying: Sukhothai noodles. Plan 1 day here; it suits heritage tourism, architecture enthusiasts. Our Sukhothai Historical Park itineraries run from 6 to 6 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

November to February. Cool weather; Loy Krathong is celebrated spectacularly here.

How to reach Sukhothai Historical Park

  • By air: Sukhothai Airport (THS), flights from Bangkok
  • By rail: Phitsanulok station, about 60 km
  • By road: About 5 hours from Chiang Mai

Travel tips

Sukhothai Historical Park travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Thailand before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Thailand.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Thai baht (THB). Not compulsory; leaving small change or 20–100 baht for good service is appreciated.

Best time to visit

November to February. Cool weather; Loy Krathong is celebrated spectacularly here.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bangkok with Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai and Koh Samui in about an hour. Private transfers are the easiest way to travel between Bangkok and Pattaya; ferries and speedboats link the islands.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples and the Grand Palace, and remove shoes where asked.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
