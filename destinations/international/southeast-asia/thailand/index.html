Thailand overview

Thailand is our speciality and the most popular international holiday from South India. Bangkok's palaces and markets, Pattaya's island trips and shows, the Andaman beaches of Phuket, Krabi and Phi Phi, and the temples and lantern festivals of Chiang Mai give it something for every kind of traveller, all about four hours from Chennai.

We run our own group departures and honeymoon specials, and plan private trips for any route and season.

When to go

November to February. The cool, dry season across most of the country. The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi) is rough from May to October; the Gulf islands (Koh Samui) are wettest from October to December.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bangkok with Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai and Koh Samui in about an hour. Private transfers are the easiest way to travel between Bangkok and Pattaya; ferries and speedboats link the islands.

Tips from our planners

  • Fill in the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online before you land.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples and the Grand Palace, and remove shoes where asked.
  • Phuket and Krabi are best from November to April; plan the Gulf islands instead in July and August.

Good to know

Thailand travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Thailand?
November to February. The cool, dry season across most of the country. The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi) is rough from May to October; the Gulf islands (Koh Samui) are wettest from October to December.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Thailand?
Most first-time visitors pick Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket and Krabi. Our shortest Thailand itinerary is 4 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Thailand?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Thailand?
Thai baht (THB)
