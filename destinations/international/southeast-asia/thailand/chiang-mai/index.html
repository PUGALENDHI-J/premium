Chiang Mai

Best time: November to February  ·  Ideal stay: 2–3 days

About Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai is a cultural city ringed by mountains, known for historic temples, traditional Lanna culture and night markets. In November it hosts Yi Peng, when thousands of lanterns are released into the sky.

Top things to see in Chiang Mai

  • Doi Suthep – Hilltop temple overlooking the city
  • Old City temples – Wat Chedi Luang and Wat Phra Singh
  • Doi Inthanon – Thailand's highest mountain
  • Elephant sanctuaries – Ethical sanctuaries without riding
  • Night Bazaar and Sunday Walking Street – Markets and street food
  • Yi Peng lantern festival – Lanterns in November

Things to do

Thai cooking classEthical elephant sanctuary visitTemple tourNight marketsHill tribe village trek

What to eat

  • Khao soi
  • Sai ua (northern sausage)
  • Mango sticky rice

Best time to visit Chiang Mai

November to February. Cool mornings and clear skies; March–April can be hazy from crop burning.

How to reach Chiang Mai

  • By air: Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX), about 1 hour 15 minutes from Bangkok
  • By rail: Chiang Mai station, overnight sleeper trains from Bangkok
  • By road: About 3 hours from Chiang Rai

Planner's tip

Choose sanctuaries that do not offer elephant rides or shows.
