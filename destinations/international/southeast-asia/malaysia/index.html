Malaysia overview

Malaysia is a multicultural country where Malay, Chinese and Tamil cultures live side by side. Kuala Lumpur's Petronas Towers and Batu Caves, the duty-free island of Langkawi, Penang's street food and George Town heritage, Genting's highland resort and the rainforests of Borneo make it great value for families and honeymooners.

When to go

December to April (west coast); March to October (east coast islands). The two coasts have opposite monsoons, so there is always a good side.

Getting around

Low-cost domestic flights link KL with Langkawi, Penang and Kota Kinabalu. The ETS train and highways connect the peninsula's cities.

Tips from our planners

  • Malaysia has a large Tamil community; you will find South Indian food and temples in every city.
  • Dress modestly at mosques; robes are provided at the larger ones.

Good to know

Malaysia travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Malaysia?
December to April (west coast); March to October (east coast islands). The two coasts have opposite monsoons, so there is always a good side.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Malaysia?
Most first-time visitors pick Kuala Lumpur, Langkawi, Penang and Genting Highlands. Our shortest Malaysia itinerary is 3 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Malaysia?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Malaysia?
Malaysian ringgit (MYR)
