About Malacca

Malacca was a great trading port ruled by the Portuguese, Dutch and British, and its UNESCO-listed old town shows all three. Red Dutch buildings, Jonker Street's night market and Peranakan cuisine make it a good day trip from KL.

Top things to see in Malacca

  • Christ Church and Dutch Square – Red colonial buildings
  • A Famosa – Remains of the Portuguese fort
  • Jonker Street – Antiques and weekend night market
  • Malacca River cruise – Riverside murals and lights
  • Baba Nyonya Heritage Museum – Peranakan home

Things to do

River cruiseTrishaw rideNight marketHeritage walk

What to eat

  • Chicken rice balls
  • Nyonya laksa
  • Cendol

Best time to visit Malacca

Year-round. Warm and humid; weekends are busy.

How to reach Malacca

  • By air: Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL), about 1.5 hours
  • By rail: Pulau Sebang/Tampin station
  • By road: About 2 hours from Kuala Lumpur

Planner's tip

Stay for the Friday–Sunday Jonker Street night market.
