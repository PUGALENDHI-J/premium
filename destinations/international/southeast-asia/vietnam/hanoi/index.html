Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Hanoi

Currency and payments

Vietnamese dong (VND). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not expected but appreciated, especially for guides and drivers.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types A, C and F · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

113 (police), 115 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Vietnamese.

Getting around

Domestic flights link Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City in about 1–2 hours. Overnight cruises are the best way to see Ha Long Bay.

Planner's tip

Vietnamese food is light and herb-led; ask us for vegetarian-friendly restaurants in each city.

Planner's tip

Carry small dong notes; many street vendors do not accept cards.

Planner's tip

Walk around Hoan Kiem on weekend evenings when the streets close to traffic.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Hanoi

Hanoi is Vietnam's thousand-year-old capital, with a lively Old Quarter of narrow streets, French-colonial boulevards, lakeside temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. It is the gateway to Ha Long Bay and Sapa.

Highlights include Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake and Ngoc Son Temple, Temple of Literature and Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. Local food worth trying: Pho, Bun cha, Egg coffee and Banh mi. Plan 2 days here; it suits culture and food lovers. Our Hanoi itineraries run from 4 to 10 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to December and March to April. Mild and dry; summers are hot and humid, winters cool.

How to reach Hanoi

  • By air: Noi Bai International Airport (HAN)
  • By rail: Hanoi station for trains south and to Lao Cai
  • By road: About 2.5 hours to Ha Long Bay

Travel tips

Hanoi travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Vietnam before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Vietnam.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Vietnamese dong (VND). Not expected but appreciated, especially for guides and drivers.

Best time to visit

October to December and March to April. Mild and dry; summers are hot and humid, winters cool.

Getting around

Domestic flights link Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City in about 1–2 hours. Overnight cruises are the best way to see Ha Long Bay.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
