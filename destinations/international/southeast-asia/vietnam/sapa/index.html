About Sapa

Sapa is a mountain town in northern Vietnam surrounded by rice terraces and ethnic minority villages, and the base for Fansipan, the roof of Indochina, reachable by cable car.

Top things to see in Sapa

  • Fansipan cable car – To Indochina's highest peak
  • Muong Hoa Valley – Rice terraces
  • Cat Cat village – Hmong village walk
  • Silver Waterfall – Roadside falls
  • O Quy Ho Pass – Mountain pass views

Things to do

Village trekkingHomestayCable carMarket visits

What to eat

  • Grilled street food
  • Thang co
  • Salmon hotpot

Best time to visit Sapa

March to May and September to November. Terraces are golden in September; winter can be cold.

How to reach Sapa

  • By air: Noi Bai (HAN), then about 5 hours by road
  • By rail: Lao Cai station, overnight trains from Hanoi
  • By road: Expressway from Hanoi

Planner's tip

Travel by overnight sleeper train for the classic experience.
