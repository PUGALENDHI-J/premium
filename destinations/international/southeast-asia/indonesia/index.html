Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Indonesia

Currency and payments

Indonesian rupiah (IDR). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Appreciated; 5–10% where no service charge is added, and small tips for drivers and guides.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types C and F · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

112

Language

Languages spoken: Indonesian (Bahasa).

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bali with Yogyakarta and Labuan Bajo; fast boats run from Bali to the Gili Islands and Lombok. Private drivers are the norm within Bali.

Planner's tip

Bali's tourist levy is collected online or on arrival; we include it in your plan.

Planner's tip

Bali traffic is slow, so choose two bases (for example Ubud and the south coast) rather than one.

General guidance, reviewed October 2026. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Indonesia

Indonesia is an archipelago of more than 17,000 islands. Bali, with its Hindu temples, rice terraces and beach clubs, is one of India's favourite honeymoon destinations. Beyond it, Java has the great temples of Borobudur and Prambanan, Lombok has quieter beaches and Labuan Bajo is the gateway to Komodo National Park.

We plan trips to 6 destinations in Indonesia: Bali (South Coast), Ubud, Nusa Penida, Yogyakarta, Lombok & the Gili Islands and Labuan Bajo (Komodo). Highlights include Uluwatu Temple and Kecak dance, Tegallalang Rice Terraces, Kelingking Beach, Borobudur and Gili Trawangan, Meno and Air. Local food worth trying: Nasi goreng, Babi guling, Satay lilit and Indian restaurants in Kuta and Seminyak. Our Indonesia itineraries run from 4 to 10 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

April to October. The dry season across Bali, Java and the eastern islands; July and August are busiest.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bali with Yogyakarta and Labuan Bajo; fast boats run from Bali to the Gili Islands and Lombok. Private drivers are the norm within Bali.

Travel tips

Indonesia travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Indonesia before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Indonesia.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Indonesian rupiah (IDR). Appreciated; 5–10% where no service charge is added, and small tips for drivers and guides.

Best time to visit

April to October. The dry season across Bali, Java and the eastern islands; July and August are busiest.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bali with Yogyakarta and Labuan Bajo; fast boats run from Bali to the Gili Islands and Lombok. Private drivers are the norm within Bali.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
