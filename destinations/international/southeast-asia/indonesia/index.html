Indonesia overview

Indonesia is an archipelago of more than 17,000 islands. Bali, with its Hindu temples, rice terraces and beach clubs, is one of India's favourite honeymoon destinations. Beyond it, Java has the great temples of Borobudur and Prambanan, Lombok has quieter beaches and Labuan Bajo is the gateway to Komodo National Park.

When to go

April to October. The dry season across Bali, Java and the eastern islands; July and August are busiest.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Bali with Yogyakarta and Labuan Bajo; fast boats run from Bali to the Gili Islands and Lombok. Private drivers are the norm within Bali.

Tips from our planners

  • Bali's tourist levy is collected online or on arrival; we include it in your plan.
  • Bali traffic is slow, so choose two bases (for example Ubud and the south coast) rather than one.

Good to know

Indonesia travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Indonesia?
April to October. The dry season across Bali, Java and the eastern islands; July and August are busiest.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Indonesia?
Most first-time visitors pick Bali (South Coast), Ubud, Nusa Penida and Yogyakarta. Our shortest Indonesia itinerary is 4 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Indonesia?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Indonesia?
Indonesian rupiah (IDR)
