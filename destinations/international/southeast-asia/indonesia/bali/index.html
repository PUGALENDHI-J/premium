Indonesia

Bali (South Coast)

Best time: April to October  ·  Ideal stay: 3–4 days

About Bali (South Coast)

Bali's south coast is where most holidays begin: Seminyak's beach clubs, Kuta's surf, Nusa Dua's resorts and the clifftop temple of Uluwatu with its sunset Kecak dance. It is one of India's favourite honeymoon destinations.

Top things to see in Bali (South Coast)

  • Uluwatu Temple and Kecak dance – Clifftop temple at sunset
  • Tanah Lot – Sea temple on a rock
  • Seminyak – Beach clubs and boutiques
  • Nusa Dua – Resorts and calm beaches
  • Jimbaran Bay – Seafood dinners on the sand
  • Garuda Wisnu Kencana – Giant statue park

Things to do

Beach clubsWater sports at Tanjung BenoaSunset dinner at JimbaranSurfing lessonsSpa treatments

What to eat

  • Nasi goreng
  • Babi guling
  • Satay lilit
  • Indian restaurants in Kuta and Seminyak

Best time to visit Bali (South Coast)

April to October. Dry season; December to February is wettest.

How to reach Bali (South Coast)

  • By air: Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS)
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: Roads to Ubud (about 1.5 hours)

Planner's tip

Pick one base per area; Bali traffic makes long daily drives tiring.
