Argentina overview

Buenos Aires is a European-style capital of grand avenues, tango halls and steakhouses. From it, flights reach the Iguazu Falls in the north and the glaciers of Patagonia in the south.

When to go

October to April. Spring and summer in the southern hemisphere.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Buenos Aires with Iguazu and El Calafate.

Tips from our planners

  • Dinner starts late in Buenos Aires, often after 9 pm.
  • Card and cash exchange rates can differ; we advise on the best way to pay.

About Argentina

Buenos Aires is a European-style capital of grand avenues, tango halls and steakhouses. From it, flights reach the Iguazu Falls in the north and the glaciers of Patagonia in the south.

Top things to see in Argentina

  • La Boca and Caminito – Colourful tango district
  • Recoleta Cemetery – Ornate mausoleums including Eva Perón's
  • Plaza de Mayo and Casa Rosada – Historic centre
  • San Telmo market – Antiques and Sunday street fair
  • Teatro Colón – World-famous opera house
  • Tigre Delta – River delta day trip

Things to do

Tango showSteakhouse dinnerDelta boat tripDay trip to Iguazu (by air)

What to eat

  • Asado
  • Empanadas
  • Dulce de leche

Best time to visit Argentina

October to April. Spring and summer in the southern hemisphere.

How to reach Argentina

  • By air: Ministro Pistarini International Airport, Ezeiza (EZE)
  • By rail: Limited long-distance railways
  • By road: Ferries to Uruguay

Planner's tip

Book a tango show with dinner in San Telmo for the classic Buenos Aires night.
