South Asia holidays

India's neighbours are some of the most rewarding short trips from Chennai.

India's neighbours are some of the most rewarding short trips from Chennai. Sri Lanka packs beaches, tea country and ancient cities into a small island; Nepal has the Himalaya, Kathmandu's temples and jungle safaris; Bhutan is a carefully managed kingdom of monasteries and mountain valleys.

Flights are short and the cultures feel familiar yet distinct, which makes these countries ideal for first international trips and family holidays.

Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a short flight from Chennai with an astonishing variety: the rock fortress of Sigiriya and the cultural triangle, the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, tea-covered hills around Nuwara Eliya and Ella, leopards at Yala and palm-lined beaches from Bentota to Galle.

Best timeDecember to April (west and south); May to September (east coast)
