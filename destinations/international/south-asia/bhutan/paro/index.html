Paro

Best time: March to May and September to November  ·  Ideal stay: 2 days

About Paro

Paro is a broad valley of farmhouses, rice fields and Bhutan's only international airport. Above it, Taktsang, the Tiger's Nest monastery, clings to a cliff 900 metres above the valley floor.

Top things to see in Paro

  • Tiger's Nest (Taktsang) – Cliffside monastery hike
  • Rinpung Dzong – Fortress monastery
  • National Museum (Ta Dzong) – Watchtower museum
  • Kyichu Lhakhang – Ancient temple
  • Chele La Pass – High pass views

Things to do

Tiger's Nest hikeArchery viewingFarmhouse dinnerHot stone bath

What to eat

  • Ema datshi
  • Red rice
  • Momos

Best time to visit Paro

March to May and September to November. Clear and festive; the Paro Tshechu is in spring.

How to reach Paro

  • By air: Paro International Airport (PBH)
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: About 1.5 hours from Thimphu

Planner's tip

Do the Tiger's Nest hike on your last full day, when you are acclimatised.
