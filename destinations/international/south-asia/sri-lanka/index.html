Sri Lanka overview

Sri Lanka is a short flight from Chennai with an astonishing variety: the rock fortress of Sigiriya and the cultural triangle, the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, tea-covered hills around Nuwara Eliya and Ella, leopards at Yala and palm-lined beaches from Bentota to Galle. The Ramayana trail draws many Indian pilgrims.

When to go

December to April (west and south); May to September (east coast). Different monsoons hit each coast, so Sri Lanka has a good beach somewhere all year.

Getting around

Private cars with drivers are the norm. The Kandy–Ella train is one of the world's most scenic rides.

Tips from our planners

  • Book the Kandy–Ella train's reserved observation or first-class seats well in advance.
  • Cover shoulders and knees and remove hats at Buddhist temples.

Good to know

Sri Lanka travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?
December to April (west and south); May to September (east coast). Different monsoons hit each coast, so Sri Lanka has a good beach somewhere all year.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Sri Lanka?
Most first-time visitors pick Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Sigiriya & Dambulla. Our shortest Sri Lanka itinerary is 3 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Sri Lanka?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Sri Lanka?
Sri Lankan rupee (LKR)
