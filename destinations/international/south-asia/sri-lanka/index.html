Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Sri Lanka

Currency and payments

Sri Lankan rupee (LKR). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

A 10% service charge is common; small tips for drivers and guides are expected.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types D, G and M · 230 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter.

Emergency numbers

119 (police), 1990 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Sinhala, Tamil, English.

Getting around

Private cars with drivers are the norm. The Kandy–Ella train is one of the world's most scenic rides.

Planner's tip

Book the Kandy–Ella train's reserved observation or first-class seats well in advance.

Planner's tip

Cover shoulders and knees and remove hats at Buddhist temples.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a short flight from Chennai with an astonishing variety: the rock fortress of Sigiriya and the cultural triangle, the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, tea-covered hills around Nuwara Eliya and Ella, leopards at Yala and palm-lined beaches from Bentota to Galle. The Ramayana trail draws many Indian pilgrims.

We plan trips to 7 destinations in Sri Lanka: Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Sigiriya & Dambulla, Bentota and Ella and more. Highlights include Gangaramaya Temple, Temple of the Tooth, Tea factories, Sigiriya Rock Fortress and Bentota Beach. Local food worth trying: Kottu roti, Hoppers, Seafood curry and Rice and curry. Our Sri Lanka itineraries run from 3 to 11 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

Travel tips

Sri Lanka travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Sri Lanka before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Sri Lanka.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Sri Lankan rupee (LKR). A 10% service charge is common; small tips for drivers and guides are expected.

Best time to visit

December to April (west and south); May to September (east coast). Different monsoons hit each coast, so Sri Lanka has a good beach somewhere all year.

Getting around

Private cars with drivers are the norm. The Kandy–Ella train is one of the world's most scenic rides.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.
  • Cover shoulders and knees and remove hats at Buddhist temples.

What to pack

Pack a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites, a compact umbrella or rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
