Nepal overview

Nepal is home to eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including Everest. Kathmandu's Durbar Squares and Pashupatinath, the lakeside town of Pokhara under the Annapurnas, Chitwan's rhinos and Lumbini, the Buddha's birthplace, make a varied week even if you never set foot on a trek.

When to go

October to November and March to April. Clear mountain views in autumn; rhododendrons in spring.

Getting around

Short domestic flights and tourist buses connect Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan; private cars are more comfortable.

Tips from our planners

  • Indian citizens can travel with a valid passport or voter ID; carry both and check current rules before you fly.
  • Mountain flights over Everest leave at dawn and depend on weather.

Good to know

Nepal travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Nepal?
October to November and March to April. Clear mountain views in autumn; rhododendrons in spring.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Nepal?
Most first-time visitors pick Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan National Park and Lumbini. Our shortest Nepal itinerary is 4 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Nepal?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Nepal?
Nepalese rupee (NPR); Indian rupee notes are widely accepted, but rules on higher denominations have changed over time, so carry ₹100 notes
