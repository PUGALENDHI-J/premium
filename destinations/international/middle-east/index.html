Middle East holidays

Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer skyscrapers, desert safaris and shopping, Oman has mountains, wadis and a quieter Arabian coast, Qatar has world-class museums, and Jordan holds the rose-red city of Petra and the desert of Wadi Rum.

Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer skyscrapers, desert safaris and shopping, Oman has mountains, wadis and a quieter Arabian coast, Qatar has world-class museums, and Jordan holds the rose-red city of Petra and the desert of Wadi Rum.

The region is best from October to April, when daytime temperatures are pleasant. Summers are extremely hot.

Oman

Oman is the gentle side of Arabia: whitewashed Muscat, mountain forts at Nizwa, emerald wadis, desert camps in the Wahiba Sands and the frankincense coast of Salalah.

Best timeOctober to April (north); July to September for Salalah's monsoon greenery

Jordan holds Petra, the rose-red Nabataean city carved into canyon walls, the Martian landscapes of Wadi Rum, the salty waters of the Dead Sea and the Roman ruins of Jerash.
