Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Iraq

Currency and payments

Iraqi dinar (IQD). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Small tips for drivers, guides and hotel staff are customary.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types C, D and G · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Language

Languages spoken: Arabic, Kurdish.

Getting around

Most pilgrims fly into Najaf; Karbala is about 1 hour 30 minutes away by road. Baghdad and Kadhimiya can be added on the way.

Planner's tip

We plan ziyarat to Iraq only when government travel advice allows, and recheck it before departure.

Planner's tip

Women should wear an abaya at the shrines; it can be borrowed at the entrance.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Iraq

Iraq's holy cities of Najaf and Karbala are among the most visited pilgrimage sites in the world. Najaf holds the shrine of Imam Ali, and Karbala the golden-domed shrines of Imam Hussain and Hazrat Abbas, which draw millions of pilgrims for Muharram and the Arbaeen walk.

We plan trips to 2 destinations in Iraq: Najaf and Karbala. Highlights include Imam Ali Shrine and Imam Hussain Shrine. Local food worth trying: Iraqi biryani, Masgouf (grilled fish), Kebab and samoon bread and Iraqi kebab. Our Iraq itineraries run from 7 to 10 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to April. Mild weather for ziyarat; summers are extremely hot. Arbaeen and Muharram are the busiest times.

Getting around

Most pilgrims fly into Najaf; Karbala is about 1 hour 30 minutes away by road. Baghdad and Kadhimiya can be added on the way.

Travel tips

Iraq travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Iraq before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Iraq.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Iraqi dinar (IQD). Small tips for drivers, guides and hotel staff are customary.

Best time to visit

October to April. Mild weather for ziyarat; summers are extremely hot. Arbaeen and Muharram are the busiest times.

Getting around

Most pilgrims fly into Najaf; Karbala is about 1 hour 30 minutes away by road. Baghdad and Kadhimiya can be added on the way.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
