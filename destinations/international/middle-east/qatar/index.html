Qatar overview

Qatar is a compact Gulf state whose capital, Doha, has world-class museums, a restored souq and a waterfront Corniche. Desert safaris to the Inland Sea, where dunes meet the water, are the classic excursion, and Doha also makes a good stopover on flights to Europe.

When to go

November to March. Pleasant weather; summer is very hot.

Getting around

The Doha Metro and taxis cover the city; desert trips need a 4x4 with a driver.

Tips from our planners

  • The Museum of Islamic Art and the National Museum are each worth a half day; check current ticket prices before you go.
  • Dress modestly in public places.

About Qatar

Qatar is a compact Gulf state whose capital, Doha, has world-class museums, a restored souq and a waterfront Corniche. Desert safaris to the Inland Sea, where dunes meet the water, are the classic excursion, and Doha also makes a good stopover on flights to Europe.

Top things to see in Qatar

  • Museum of Islamic Art – I. M. Pei-designed museum
  • National Museum of Qatar – Desert rose-shaped museum
  • Souq Waqif – Restored traditional market
  • The Corniche – Waterfront promenade
  • Khor Al Adaid (Inland Sea) – Where dunes meet the sea
  • Katara Cultural Village – Arts district and beach

Things to do

Desert safariDhow cruiseMuseum visitsSouq shopping

What to eat

  • Machboos
  • Karak chai
  • Luqaimat

Best time to visit Qatar

November to March. Pleasant weather; summer is very hot.

How to reach Qatar

  • By air: Hamad International Airport (DOH)
  • By rail: Doha Metro
  • By road: Road border with Saudi Arabia only

Planner's tip

Use Qatar as a two-night stopover on the way to Europe.
