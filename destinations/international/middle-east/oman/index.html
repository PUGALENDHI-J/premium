Oman overview

Oman is the gentle side of Arabia: whitewashed Muscat, mountain forts at Nizwa, emerald wadis, desert camps in the Wahiba Sands and the frankincense coast of Salalah. It is quieter and more traditional than its neighbours.

When to go

October to April (north); July to September for Salalah's monsoon greenery. Cool winters in the north; the khareef season makes Salalah green.

Getting around

A car with driver or a 4x4 self-drive is the best way to explore; distances between regions are long.

Tips from our planners

  • Pack swimwear for the wadis but cover up in villages.
  • Friday mornings are quiet; plan souq visits for evenings.

Oman travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Oman?
October to April (north); July to September for Salalah's monsoon greenery. Cool winters in the north; the khareef season makes Salalah green.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Oman?
Most first-time visitors pick Muscat, Nizwa & Jebel Akhdar and Salalah. Our shortest Oman itinerary is 4 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Oman?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Oman?
Omani rial (OMR)
