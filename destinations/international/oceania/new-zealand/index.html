New Zealand overview

New Zealand is a land of dramatic scenery: the fjords of Milford Sound, glaciers and lakes in the Southern Alps, Queenstown's adventure sports, Rotorua's geysers and Māori culture, and Hobbiton near Matamata.

When to go

December to March for summer; June to August for skiing. Spring and autumn are quieter and beautiful.

Getting around

Fly between the islands; self-drive or coach tours on each island.

Tips from our planners

  • Driving distances look short but mountain roads are slow; plan no more than 4–5 hours a day.
  • Biosecurity rules are strict; declare all food and outdoor gear.

Good to know

New Zealand travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit New Zealand?
December to March for summer; June to August for skiing. Spring and autumn are quieter and beautiful.
Which places should I include on a first trip to New Zealand?
Most first-time visitors pick Auckland, Queenstown and Rotorua. Our shortest New Zealand itinerary is 6 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for New Zealand?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in New Zealand?
New Zealand dollar (NZD)
