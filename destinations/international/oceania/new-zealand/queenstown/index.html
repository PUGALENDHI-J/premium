Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Queenstown

Currency and payments

New Zealand dollar (NZD). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not expected.

Drinking water

Tap water is generally safe to drink.

Power plugs

Type I · 230 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter.

Emergency numbers

111

Language

Languages spoken: English, Māori.

Getting around

Fly between the islands; self-drive or coach tours on each island.

Planner's tip

Driving distances look short but mountain roads are slow; plan no more than 4–5 hours a day.

Planner's tip

Biosecurity rules are strict; declare all food and outdoor gear.

Planner's tip

Fly to Milford Sound to save a long drive.

Everything you need to know about Queenstown

Queenstown is the adventure capital of the world, on Lake Wakatipu below the Remarkables, with bungee jumping, jet boats and day trips to Milford Sound.

Highlights include Milford Sound, Skyline Gondola, Kawarau Bridge Bungy and Shotover Jet. Local food worth trying: Fergburger, Lamb and Local wines. Plan 3–4 days here; it suits honeymooners, adventure lovers. Our Queenstown itineraries run from 6 to 11 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

December to March; June to August for skiing. Year-round adventure.

How to reach Queenstown

  • By air: Queenstown Airport (ZQN)
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: State highways

Travel tips

Queenstown travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for New Zealand before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. Some places here are at high altitude: rest on arrival, drink plenty of water and ascend gradually. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in New Zealand.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the New Zealand dollar (NZD). Not expected.

Best time to visit

December to March; June to August for skiing. Year-round adventure.

Getting around

Fly between the islands; self-drive or coach tours on each island.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack warm layers, a down jacket and gloves, comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

What is Queenstown famous for?
Queenstown is known for Milford Sound, Skyline Gondola, Kawarau Bridge Bungy and Shotover Jet. Queenstown is the adventure capital of the world, on Lake Wakatipu below the Remarkables, with bungee jumping, jet boats and day trips to Milford Sound.

December to March; June to August for skiing. Year-round adventure.
How many days are enough for Queenstown?
Plan 3–4 days to see the main sights without rushing. Our itineraries that include Queenstown run from 6 to 11 days, usually combined with nearby places.
How do I reach Queenstown?
Queenstown Airport (ZQN). No railway. State highways.
What currency is used in Queenstown?
New Zealand dollar (NZD). Not expected.
What languages are spoken in Queenstown?
English, Māori.

Type I · 230 V. Indian plugs do not fit, so carry a universal adapter.

Tap water is generally safe to drink.
