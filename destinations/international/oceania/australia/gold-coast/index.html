About Gold Coast & Brisbane

The Gold Coast is Australia's theme park and surf capital, with Surfers Paradise, Movie World, Sea World and Dreamworld. Brisbane, an hour north, has koalas at Lone Pine.

Top things to see in Gold Coast & Brisbane

  • Warner Bros. Movie World – Movie theme park
  • Sea World – Marine park
  • Dreamworld – Rides and wildlife
  • Surfers Paradise – Beach and nightlife
  • SkyPoint – Observation deck
  • Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary – Koalas in Brisbane

Things to do

Theme parksSurfing lessonsRainforest day trip

What to eat

  • Seafood
  • Brunch

Best time to visit Gold Coast & Brisbane

April to October. Warm and dry.

How to reach Gold Coast & Brisbane

  • By air: Gold Coast Airport (OOL)
  • By rail: Rail to Brisbane
  • By road: Pacific Motorway

Planner's tip

Buy multi-park passes for the theme parks.
