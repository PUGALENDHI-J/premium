Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Xi'an

Currency and payments

Chinese yuan (CNY). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Not customary, though tour guides and drivers are often tipped.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types A, C and I · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

110 (police), 120 (ambulance)

Language

Languages spoken: Mandarin.

Getting around

High-speed trains link Beijing, Xi'an, Shanghai and Guilin; domestic flights cover longer hops.

Planner's tip

Set up mobile payment apps and a roaming plan before you go; many international apps are restricted.

Planner's tip

Carry your passport at all times; it is needed for trains and some attractions.

Planner's tip

Hire a guide at the Terracotta Army; the pits make much more sense with context.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Xi'an

Xi'an was the eastern end of the Silk Road and capital of thirteen dynasties. The Terracotta Army, buried with China's first emperor, is the star, with intact city walls and a lively Muslim Quarter.

Highlights include Terracotta Army, Xi'an City Wall, Muslim Quarter and Big Wild Goose Pagoda. Local food worth trying: Biangbiang noodles, Roujiamo and Yangrou paomo. Plan 2 days here; it suits history lovers. Our Xi'an itineraries run from 8 to 10 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

April to May and September to October. Mild seasons.

How to reach Xi'an

  • By air: Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY)
  • By rail: Xi'an North station, high-speed rail
  • By road: About 4.5 hours from Beijing by train

Travel tips

Xi'an travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for China before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in China.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Chinese yuan (CNY). Not customary, though tour guides and drivers are often tipped.

Best time to visit

April to May and September to October. Mild seasons.

Getting around

High-speed trains link Beijing, Xi'an, Shanghai and Guilin; domestic flights cover longer hops.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

What is Xi'an famous for?
Xi'an is known for Terracotta Army, Xi'an City Wall, Muslim Quarter and Big Wild Goose Pagoda. Xi'an was the eastern end of the Silk Road and capital of thirteen dynasties.
What is the best time to visit Xi'an?
April to May and September to October. Mild seasons.
How many days are enough for Xi'an?
Plan 2 days to see the main sights without rushing. Our itineraries that include Xi'an run from 8 to 10 days, usually combined with nearby places.
How do I reach Xi'an?
Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY). Xi'an North station, high-speed rail. About 4.5 hours from Beijing by train.
What currency is used in Xi'an?
Chinese yuan (CNY). Not customary, though tour guides and drivers are often tipped.
What languages are spoken in Xi'an?
Mandarin.
What plug type does China use?
Types A, C and I · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; bring an adapter for three-pin chargers.
Is tap water safe to drink in China?
Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Do Indian citizens need a visa for China?
