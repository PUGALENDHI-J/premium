East Asia holidays

Japan, South Korea, China, Hong Kong and Macau combine ultra-modern cities with ancient temples, gardens and some of the best food on earth.

Japan, South Korea, China, Hong Kong and Macau combine ultra-modern cities with ancient temples, gardens and some of the best food on earth. Cherry blossom in spring and autumn colours make these the most photogenic seasons.

East Asia suits travellers who like efficient trains, safe cities and a lot of variety in a short time. Most trips run seven to ten days.
