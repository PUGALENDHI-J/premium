Hiroshima & Miyajima

Best time: March to May and October to November  ·  Ideal stay: 1–2 days

About Hiroshima & Miyajima

Hiroshima's Peace Memorial Park and museum tell the story of the 1945 atomic bombing and the city's rebirth. A short ferry away, Miyajima's 'floating' torii gate of Itsukushima Shrine is one of Japan's most famous views.

Top things to see in Hiroshima & Miyajima

  • Peace Memorial Park and Museum – Memorial and museum
  • Atomic Bomb Dome – UNESCO-listed ruin
  • Itsukushima Shrine, Miyajima – Floating torii gate
  • Mount Misen – Ropeway and hike on Miyajima
  • Hiroshima Castle – Rebuilt castle

Things to do

Ferry to MiyajimaPeace museum visitOkonomiyaki tasting

What to eat

  • Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki
  • Grilled oysters
  • Momiji manju

Best time to visit Hiroshima & Miyajima

March to May and October to November. Mild seasons.

How to reach Hiroshima & Miyajima

  • By air: Hiroshima Airport (HIJ)
  • By rail: Hiroshima Shinkansen station
  • By road: About 1.5 hours from Osaka by bullet train

Planner's tip

Check tide tables: the torii appears to float at high tide and can be walked to at low tide.
