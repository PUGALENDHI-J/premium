Japan overview

Japan combines neon-lit Tokyo, the temples and geisha districts of Kyoto, Osaka's street food, the peace memorials of Hiroshima and Mount Fuji's lakes into a journey that runs like clockwork. Shinkansen bullet trains make a two-week trip feel effortless.

When to go

Late March to May and October to November. Cherry blossom in spring and red maples in autumn; winter is for snow in Hokkaido.

Getting around

Shinkansen trains link Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka and Hiroshima. We advise whether a rail pass or individual tickets work out cheaper for your route.

Tips from our planners

  • Forward big suitcases between hotels with a luggage-delivery service and travel light on trains.
  • Many restaurants are small and cash-friendly; carry yen, though cards are increasingly accepted.

Japan travel FAQs

What is the best time to visit Japan?
Late March to May and October to November. Cherry blossom in spring and red maples in autumn; winter is for snow in Hokkaido.
Which places should I include on a first trip to Japan?
Most first-time visitors pick Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka and Hiroshima & Miyajima. Our shortest Japan itinerary is 5 days.
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Japan?
Entry rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel, and we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What currency is used in Japan?
Japanese yen (JPY)
