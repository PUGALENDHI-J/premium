Mount Fuji & Hakone

Best time: October to February for clearest views  ·  Ideal stay: 1–2 days

About Mount Fuji & Hakone

Japan's sacred volcano is best seen from the Fuji Five Lakes or the hot-spring resort of Hakone, where a ropeway, pirate-ship cruise and open-air museum make a classic loop.

Top things to see in Mount Fuji & Hakone

  • Lake Kawaguchiko – Classic Fuji reflections
  • Chureito Pagoda – Pagoda framed by Fuji
  • Hakone ropeway and Owakudani – Volcanic valley
  • Lake Ashi cruise – Pirate-ship cruise
  • Hakone Open-Air Museum – Sculpture park
  • Fuji Subaru Line 5th Station – Road up the mountain (seasonal)

Things to do

Onsen (hot spring) stayRyokan dinnerLake cruiseFuji climb (July–early September)

What to eat

  • Hoto noodles
  • Black eggs at Owakudani
  • Kaiseki at a ryokan

Best time to visit Mount Fuji & Hakone

October to February for clearest views. Fuji is often hidden by cloud in summer.

How to reach Mount Fuji & Hakone

  • By air: Tokyo Haneda (HND) or Narita (NRT)
  • By rail: Odawara Shinkansen station for Hakone
  • By road: About 2 hours from Tokyo

Planner's tip

Stay overnight in a ryokan; the best Fuji views are early in the morning.
