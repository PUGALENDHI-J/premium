Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Masai Mara

Currency and payments

Kenyan shilling (KES). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Around 10% in restaurants; safari guides and drivers are customarily tipped.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Type G · 240 V. Indian plugs will not fit; carry a universal adapter.

Emergency numbers

999 or 112

Language

Languages spoken: Swahili, English.

Getting around

Safaris use 4x4 land cruisers; short bush flights save long drives between parks.

Planner's tip

Pack neutral-coloured clothes for safari and a warm layer for early-morning game drives.

Planner's tip

Soft bags are required on bush flights; hard suitcases may not be accepted.

Planner's tip

Fly in and out to save a long, bumpy drive.

Everything you need to know about Masai Mara

The Masai Mara is Kenya's most famous reserve, rolling grassland where lions, cheetahs and leopards hunt and where the Great Migration's river crossings unfold from about July to October.

Highlights include Mara River crossings, Big Five game drives, Hot-air balloon safari and Maasai village. Local food worth trying: Nyama choma, Ugali and Camp meals. Plan 3 days here; it suits wildlife lovers, families, photographers. Our Masai Mara itineraries run from 5 to 8 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

July to October for the migration; January to February. Long rains in April and May.

How to reach Masai Mara

  • By air: Wilson Airport, Nairobi, then bush flight
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: About 5–6 hours by road from Nairobi

Travel tips

Masai Mara travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Kenya before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Kenya.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Kenyan shilling (KES). Around 10% in restaurants; safari guides and drivers are customarily tipped.

Best time to visit

July to October for the migration; January to February. Long rains in April and May.

Getting around

Safaris use 4x4 land cruisers; short bush flights save long drives between parks.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack a compact umbrella or rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and a universal travel adapter.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
