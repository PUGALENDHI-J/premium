Before you go

Essential tips for travelling to Egypt

Currency and payments

Egyptian pound (EGP). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Tipping (baksheesh) is customary for most services.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types C and F · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

122 (police), 123 (ambulance), 126 (tourist police)

Language

Languages spoken: Arabic.

Getting around

Domestic flights or overnight trains connect Cairo with Luxor and Aswan; Nile cruises link the two.

Planner's tip

Reach Abu Simbel early; tours leave Aswan before dawn.

Planner's tip

Carry small notes for tips (baksheesh), which are customary for many services.

General guidance. Local rules and services change; we confirm details for your dates.

Everything you need to know about Egypt

Egypt is home to the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx and the treasures of Tutankhamun, now displayed in the new Grand Egyptian Museum. A Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan passes the Valley of the Kings, Karnak and the temples of Edfu and Kom Ombo, and Abu Simbel lies further south.

We plan trips to 3 destinations in Egypt: Cairo & Giza, Luxor and Aswan & Abu Simbel. Highlights include Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, Karnak Temple and Abu Simbel. Local food worth trying: Koshari, Ful medames, Falafel (ta'ameya) and Egyptian mezze. Our Egypt itineraries run from 4 to 9 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to April. Comfortable sightseeing weather; summer is very hot in Luxor and Aswan.

Getting around

Domestic flights or overnight trains connect Cairo with Luxor and Aswan; Nile cruises link the two.

Travel tips

Egypt travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Egypt before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Egypt.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Egyptian pound (EGP). Tipping (baksheesh) is customary for most services.

Best time to visit

October to April. Comfortable sightseeing weather; summer is very hot in Luxor and Aswan.

Getting around

Domestic flights or overnight trains connect Cairo with Luxor and Aswan; Nile cruises link the two.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.

FAQs

Egypt FAQs

What is Egypt famous for?
Egypt is known for Pyramids, Nile cruise and Temples. Egypt is home to the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx and the treasures of Tutankhamun, now displayed in the new Grand Egyptian Museum.
Where is Egypt?
Egypt is in Africa. Its capital is Cairo.
What is the best time to visit Egypt?
October to April. Comfortable sightseeing weather; summer is very hot in Luxor and Aswan.
How many days do I need for Egypt?
Our Egypt itineraries run from 4 to 9 days. A first trip usually covers Cairo & Giza, Luxor and Aswan & Abu Simbel.
What currency is used in Egypt?
Egyptian pound (EGP). Tipping (baksheesh) is customary for most services.
What languages are spoken in Egypt?
Arabic.
What plug type does Egypt use?
Types C and F · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; bring an adapter for three-pin chargers.
Is tap water safe to drink in Egypt?
Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.
What is the time difference between India and Egypt?
Egypt is 3 hours 30 minutes behind India (2 hours 30 minutes in summer).
Do Indian citizens need a visa for Egypt?
Rules for Indian passport holders vary and change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes. Check the official government or embassy website before you travel; we confirm the current rules when you book with us.
What are the emergency numbers in Egypt?
122 (police), 123 (ambulance), 126 (tourist police).
