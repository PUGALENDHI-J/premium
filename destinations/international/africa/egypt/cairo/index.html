About Cairo & Giza

Cairo is the largest city in the Arab world, and at its edge stand the Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx. The Grand Egyptian Museum now displays the treasures of Tutankhamun, and Islamic Cairo's mosques and Khan el-Khalili bazaar are full of life.

Top things to see in Cairo & Giza

  • Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx – Last of the Seven Wonders
  • Grand Egyptian Museum – Tutankhamun's treasures
  • Khan el-Khalili – Historic bazaar
  • Citadel of Saladin – Mosque of Muhammad Ali
  • Saqqara – Step Pyramid of Djoser
  • Nile dinner cruise – Evening on the river

Things to do

Camel ride at the pyramidsSound-and-light showBazaar shoppingNile felucca ride

What to eat

  • Koshari
  • Ful medames
  • Falafel (ta'ameya)

Best time to visit Cairo & Giza

October to April. Mild winter days.

How to reach Cairo & Giza

  • By air: Cairo International Airport (CAI) or Sphinx Airport (SPX)
  • By rail: Ramses station, sleeper trains south
  • By road: Desert roads to Alexandria

Planner's tip

Visit the pyramids at opening time; mid-day is hot and crowded.
