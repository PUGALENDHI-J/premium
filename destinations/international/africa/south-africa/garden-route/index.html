Essential tips for travelling to Garden Route

Currency and payments

South African rand (ZAR). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

10–15% in restaurants is customary.

Drinking water

Tap water is generally safe in the major cities.

Power plugs

Types C, D, M and N · 230 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

10111 (police), 10177 (ambulance), 112 from mobiles

Language

Languages spoken: English, Afrikaans, Zulu and others.

Getting around

Fly between Johannesburg, Cape Town and the Kruger area; self-drive or chauffeured cars on the Garden Route.

Planner's tip

Everything you need to know about Garden Route

The Garden Route runs along South Africa's southern coast past lagoons, forests and beaches, from Mossel Bay to Tsitsikamma, with Oudtshoorn's ostriches and caves inland.

Highlights include Knysna Lagoon, Tsitsikamma National Park, Bloukrans Bridge and Cango Caves. Local food worth trying: Knysna oysters and Seafood. Plan 3 days here; it suits road-trippers, adventure lovers. Our Garden Route itineraries run from 7 to 11 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to April. Warm and dry.

How to reach Garden Route

  • By air: George Airport (GRJ)
  • By rail: No passenger railway
  • By road: N2 coastal highway

Travel tips

Garden Route travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for South Africa before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in South Africa.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the South African rand (ZAR). 10–15% in restaurants is customary.

Best time to visit

October to April. Warm and dry.

Getting around

Fly between Johannesburg, Cape Town and the Kruger area; self-drive or chauffeured cars on the Garden Route.

Local etiquette

  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and swimwear and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
