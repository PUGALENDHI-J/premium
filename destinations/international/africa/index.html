Africa holidays

Africa holiday highlights include the Great Migration in Kenya and Tanzania, Cape Town and the Garden Route in South Africa, the pyramids and Nile temples of Egypt, and Morocco's medinas and Sahara dunes.

Africa holiday highlights include the Great Migration in Kenya and Tanzania, Cape Town and the Garden Route in South Africa, the pyramids and Nile temples of Egypt, and Morocco's medinas and Sahara dunes.

Safari seasons vary by country; the migration river crossings in the Masai Mara usually happen between July and October. Egypt and Morocco are most comfortable from October to April.

Tanzania

Tanzania holds the Serengeti, where the Great Migration spends most of the year, the Ngorongoro Crater with its dense wildlife, Mount Kilimanjaro and the spice island of Zanzibar for beach time after safari.

Best timeJune to October for safaris; January to February for calving season

South Africa

South Africa combines a big-game safari in Kruger with the beauty of Cape Town, Table Mountain and the Cape Peninsula, the vineyards of the Winelands and the coastal Garden Route.

Best timeOctober to April for Cape Town; May to September for Kruger safaris

Morocco

Morocco is a sensory journey: the souqs and riads of Marrakech, the medieval medina of Fes, the blue town of Chefchaouen and camel treks into the Sahara dunes at Merzouga, with the Atlas Mountains in between.

Best timeMarch to May and September to November

Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is home to Victoria Falls, one of the natural wonders of the world, where the Zambezi plunges into a misty gorge.

Best timeApril to October
