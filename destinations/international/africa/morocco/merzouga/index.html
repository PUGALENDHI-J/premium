Essential tips for travelling to Merzouga (Sahara)

Currency and payments

Moroccan dirham (MAD). Carry some local currency for markets, small shops and tips, and ask your bank about international card charges before you travel.

Tipping

Tipping is customary for most services.

Drinking water

Drink bottled or filtered water; it is cheap and easy to find.

Power plugs

Types C and E · 220 V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; carry an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Emergency numbers

19 (police), 15 (ambulance), 112 from mobiles

Language

Languages spoken: Arabic, Berber, French.

Getting around

Trains connect Marrakech, Casablanca and Fes; a car with driver is needed for the desert and mountains.

Planner's tip

Hire a licensed guide for the Fes medina; it is a maze of over 9,000 lanes.

Planner's tip

Everything you need to know about Merzouga (Sahara)

Merzouga is the gateway to Erg Chebbi, a sea of orange Sahara dunes. Camel treks at sunset to a desert camp, with drumming and stars, are a Moroccan highlight.

Highlights include Erg Chebbi dunes, Camel trek, Desert camp and Todra Gorge. Local food worth trying: Berber pizza (madfouna) and Tagine. Plan 1–2 days here; it suits adventure couples. Our Merzouga (Sahara) itineraries run from 5 to 7 days and every one can be changed to suit you.

When to go

October to April. Cold nights in winter; very hot summers.

How to reach Merzouga (Sahara)

  • By air: Errachidia Airport (ERH), about 2 hours
  • By rail: No railway
  • By road: Long drive from Marrakech or Fes

Travel tips

Merzouga (Sahara) travel tips

Visa and entry

Entry rules for Indian passport holders change, including visa-free, visa-on-arrival and e-visa schemes, so confirm the current rules on the official government or embassy website for Morocco before booking flights. Most countries require a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date. We check the current requirements when we prepare your quote; we cannot guarantee visa approval.

Health and safety

Buy travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation; healthcare abroad can be expensive. Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a copy of the prescription. For current safety advice, check the travel advisories published by the Government of India and the embassy in Morocco.

Currency and tipping

The currency is the Moroccan dirham (MAD). Tipping is customary for most services.

Best time to visit

October to April. Cold nights in winter; very hot summers.

Getting around

Trains connect Marrakech, Casablanca and Fes; a car with driver is needed for the desert and mountains.

Local etiquette

  • Dress modestly at temples, churches and mosques, and remove footwear where asked.
  • Ask before photographing people, and follow photography rules at religious sites and museums.

What to pack

Pack modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees for religious sites and comfortable walking shoes.

Phone and internet

Indian mobile operators sell international roaming packs, or you can buy a local SIM or eSIM on arrival with your passport. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM before you travel.

Budget

We don't publish fixed prices because the season, hotel category, flights and group size change the cost. Your written quote lists exactly what is included, so you can compare like for like.
