While you travel

Sightseeing and tickets

Tickets and tours booked ahead, so the day you planned is the day you get.

Popular attractions sell out, especially on weekends and school holidays, and many now use timed entry. We book them ahead so the day goes as planned.

Plan the order of days

We arrange activities around the weather, opening days and travel time. Outdoor tours go early in the trip so there is a spare day if rain cancels one. Families get the theme park on a weekday where possible. See The best theme park holidays for Indian families.

Private or group tours

  • Group tours cost less and are fine for most sightseeing.
  • Private tours suit families with young children, seniors, honeymooners and anyone who wants to set their own pace. See private tours.

On the day

Your vouchers come to WhatsApp with the meeting point, time and what to bring. If the operator cancels for weather, we help rebook or refund.

Ask about sightseeing and tickets

Tell us your dates and destination. A planner replies on WhatsApp, usually the same day.

Send an enquiry WhatsApp us Call +91 73059 71481

What we handle

  • Theme parks such as Disneyland, Universal Studios and Ferrari World
  • Attraction tickets with timed entry, such as observation decks and museums
  • Day tours, island hops, desert safaris and cruises
  • Private local guides and language-specific guides
  • Cultural shows and dinner cruises

Keep these ready

  • Dates you would like each activity
  • Ages of children (many tickets are priced by age or height)
  • Any mobility needs
FAQs

Sightseeing and tickets: common questions

Can I add tickets to a trip I booked elsewhere?
Yes. Send us your dates and hotel, and we suggest and book activities that fit.
Are tickets refundable?
It depends on the attraction. Some allow free changes; others are non-refundable once booked. We tell you before you pay.
