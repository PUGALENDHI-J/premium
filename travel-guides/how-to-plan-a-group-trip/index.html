How to plan a group trip with friends or family without the stress

Practical steps for organising a group holiday: choosing dates and a destination, budgets, rooming, payments, decision-making and keeping everyone happy.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Group trips are fun to take and tricky to organise. A few simple rules make them much easier.

1. Pick an organiser

One person should be the point of contact with the travel planner. Group chats are great for ideas but terrible for decisions.

2. Agree on dates first

Dates are the hardest thing to align. Share two or three options early and fix one. Book quickly once dates are agreed, as group seats on flights are limited.

3. Set a budget range

Ask everyone for a comfortable budget per person, privately if needed. Plan to the lower end so nobody feels pressured. You can offer optional upgrades.

4. Choose a destination that suits the group

  • Friends' trips: Pattaya and Bangkok, North Goa, Bali or Dubai for nightlife and activities.
  • Extended families: Kerala, Singapore or Dubai for easy logistics and something for every age.
  • Office teams: short flights and venues for activities. See Corporate travel.

5. Rooming

Decide early who shares with whom. Twin and triple sharing reduce costs. Single rooms usually carry a supplement.

6. Payments

Collect deposits from everyone before booking. Agree what happens if someone drops out: deposits may be non-refundable after booking, and the per-person cost can change if numbers fall.

7. Transport

Size vehicles for people and luggage. A 12-seater tempo traveller for 12 people with suitcases is uncomfortable; plan a bigger vehicle.

8. Build in free time

Not everyone will want every activity. Leave free afternoons and evenings for smaller groups to do their own thing.

9. Share the plan

Share the final itinerary, hotel names, emergency contacts and a packing list with everyone.
