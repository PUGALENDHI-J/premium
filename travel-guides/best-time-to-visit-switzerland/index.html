Best time to visit Switzerland: summer lakes or winter snow?

Switzerland changes completely between summer and winter. Compare seasons for scenery, snow, mountain excursions and prices.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Switzerland has two main seasons for visitors from India: green summers and snowy winters. Both are spectacular; they are just different holidays.

June to September: summer

This is the most popular time for Indian travellers. Meadows are green, lakes are blue and cable cars and mountain railways run on full schedules. You can cruise Lake Lucerne, ride panoramic trains and still find snow at high points like Jungfraujoch, Mount Titlis and the glaciers above Zermatt.

  • July and August are the busiest months and the most expensive.
  • June and September are often the best balance of weather and crowds.

December to March: winter

Snow covers villages and resorts open for skiing. Christmas markets fill towns in December. Some high mountain passes and scenic routes close, and days are short, but snow-covered landscapes around Interlaken & Jungfrau and Zermatt are magical. This season suits snow lovers and skiers.

April, May, October and November: shoulder seasons

  • April and May: spring flowers in the valleys, snow still on the peaks, fewer visitors. Some excursions run reduced schedules.
  • October: autumn colours and quiet trails; some lifts close for maintenance.
  • November: the quietest month, often grey, with many resorts between seasons.

Weather in the mountains

Mountain weather changes quickly at any time of year. Before going up to Jungfraujoch or Titlis, check the live webcams: if the summit is in cloud, switch days if your itinerary allows. Carry warm layers even in July.

Quick answer

  • First trip, best all-round: June or September.
  • Snow: January to March.
  • Christmas markets: December.
