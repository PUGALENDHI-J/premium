Best time to visit Dubai: winter, summer and festival months

When to visit Dubai for comfortable weather, the Shopping Festival, Global Village and good summer deals, and what Ramadan means for visitors.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Dubai has two seasons: a pleasant winter and a very hot summer. Most of the city is air-conditioned, but outdoor sightseeing depends on the season.

November to March: the best time

Daytime temperatures are warm but comfortable, and evenings are cool enough for desert safaris, dhow cruises and outdoor markets. This is when seasonal attractions run:

  • Global Village, the cultural theme park, opens roughly from late October to spring.
  • Dubai Miracle Garden is open in the cooler months.
  • Dubai Shopping Festival runs for several weeks around December and January, with sales, fireworks and events.

It is also the busiest season, so hotel rates are higher, especially around New Year.

April and October: shoulder months

Hot but manageable, with good hotel deals. Mornings and evenings are fine for outdoor activities; plan malls and museums for midday.

May to September: summer

Temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and humidity can be high. Outdoor sightseeing is limited to early mornings and evenings, and desert safaris are less comfortable. On the other hand, hotels are much cheaper and indoor attractions such as the Museum of the Future, Dubai Mall, indoor theme parks and the Burj Khalifa are unaffected.

Ramadan

The dates of Ramadan follow the Islamic calendar and move earlier each year. Visitors can still enjoy Dubai: most attractions stay open, and evenings are lively with iftar meals. Be respectful by not eating or drinking in public during daylight hours, and dress modestly.

Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi follows the same pattern. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is best visited in the morning in summer.

Quick answer

Visit between November and March for the best weather and seasonal attractions. Choose summer for value and indoor fun, but plan around the heat.
