How many nights in the Maldives, and should you split resorts?

Is three nights enough in the Maldives? How to decide between one resort or two, beach villa or water villa, and how transfers affect your plan.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

The Maldives is unusual: you mostly stay on one resort island. So the question is less "what to see" and more "how long, and where".

Three nights

Enough for a short escape from India, especially if your resort is close to the airport by speedboat. You get two full days of lagoon, snorkelling and spa. With seaplane transfers, though, three nights can feel rushed.

Four or five nights

The sweet spot for most honeymooners. You have time to try a sandbank picnic, a sunset dolphin cruise and snorkelling trips without feeling hurried, and you settle into "island time".

Six nights or more

Ideal if you want to split your stay or combine the Maldives with another destination such as Dubai, Sri Lanka or Singapore.

Beach villa, water villa or both?

  • Water villas sit over the lagoon, with steps straight into the water. They are the iconic Maldives experience.
  • Beach villas are often more spacious and private, with direct beach access, and usually cheaper.
  • Splitting your stay, for example two nights in a beach villa and two in a water villa, gives you both.

One resort or two?

Changing resorts means another transfer, which can take half a day. It makes sense only for stays of six nights or more, or if you want two very different experiences.

Transfers matter

  • Speedboat: resorts near Malé, quick and available any time.
  • Seaplane: scenic but costly, and daylight-only. Late arrivals may need a night in Malé.
  • Domestic flight plus boat: for distant atolls.

Budget options

Local islands with guesthouses offer the Maldives' water at much lower prices, with some restrictions (for example, on alcohol and beachwear outside designated areas).
