Best time to visit Kashmir: tulips, meadows, autumn gold or snow

Kashmir looks completely different in each season. Here is what spring, summer, autumn and winter offer and which months suit your trip.

By the Nexara Holidays travel desk · Updated 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Kashmir is one of the few places in India that is beautiful in every season, but each season is a different trip. Decide what you want to see first.

Spring (March to May): tulips and blossom

The valley wakes up in spring. The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden in Srinagar, Asia's largest, usually blooms from late March to April, and almond and apple orchards flower around the same time. Days are mild and nights still cool. This is a lovely time for honeymoons and photography.

Summer (June to August): green meadows

Summer is the peak season for families. The meadows of Pahalgam, Gulmarg and Sonamarg are green and full of flowers, and the weather is pleasant when the plains are hot. It is also the season of the Amarnath Yatra, which affects traffic and hotel availability around Pahalgam and Sonamarg, so book early.

Autumn (September to November): golden chinars

Autumn is many photographers' favourite. Chinar trees turn red and gold, the skies are clear and crowds thin out after the summer rush. Late October and early November are especially beautiful in Srinagar's Mughal gardens.

Winter (December to February): snow

Winter brings snow to Gulmarg, which becomes one of Asia's best ski areas, and often to Pahalgam and Srinagar too. Dal Lake can partially freeze in very cold spells. It is the season for snow lovers and skiers, but roads can close after heavy snowfall and flights can be delayed, so keep buffer days.

Which is best for you?

  • Honeymoon: spring or autumn for comfort and colour; winter if you want snow.
  • Family: summer, when all roads are open and the weather is easy.
  • Photography: autumn.
  • Skiing: January to March at Gulmarg.

Planning tips

Carry layers in every season; evenings get cold even in summer. Prepaid mobile connections from other states may not work in Jammu and Kashmir, so check before you travel. Local taxi unions operate for sightseeing at Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg, which is normal and should be included in your quote.
