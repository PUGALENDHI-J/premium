Group tours

From our fixed-date Thailand batches to private trips for your own group.

There are two kinds of group trip. One is a fixed-date departure that you join, like our Thailand batches from Chennai with a tour manager. The other is a private group of your own friends, family or colleagues, where we plan the whole trip around you.

Our fixed-date departures

Our Thailand Ultimate Boys Escape and Thailand Boys Unleashed trips leave Chennai on set dates with a Nexara representative, private transfers and pre-booked activities.

Planning a private group

  • Rooming and budgets. We quote per person on twin or triple sharing and flag single supplements.
  • One decision-maker. A single point of contact in your group keeps changes simple.
  • Coaches or tempo travellers. Vehicle size matters for comfort; we size it with luggage in mind.
  • Payment schedules. For groups we usually confirm with a deposit and collect the balance closer to departure, as per the terms in your quote.

Popular group destinations include Pattaya, Dubai, North Goa, Manali and Bali (South Coast).
