Group tours

There are two kinds of group trip. One is a fixed-date departure that you join, with a set route, set dates and a tour manager from Chennai. The other is a private group of your own friends, family or colleagues, where we plan the whole trip around you.

Our fixed-date departures

We run set-date departures through the year, each with a Nexara representative, private transfers and pre-booked activities. Ask us which batches are open for your dates, from a Europe Group Tour, Dubai Group Tour or Turkey Group Tour to a Bali Friends' Trip or an Andaman Group Tour.

Planning a private group

  • Rooming and budgets. We quote per person on twin or triple sharing and flag single supplements.
  • One decision-maker. A single point of contact in your group keeps changes simple.
  • Coaches or tempo travellers. Vehicle size matters for comfort; we size it with luggage in mind.
  • Payment schedules. For groups we usually confirm with a deposit and collect the balance closer to departure, as per the terms in your quote.

Popular group destinations include Dubai, Bali (South Coast), Istanbul, North Goa, Manali, the Andamans and Kuala Lumpur.
