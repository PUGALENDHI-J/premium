Journeys of faith

Pilgrimage tours

Temple circuits and sacred journeys, planned with darshan timings in mind.

A pilgrimage is planned around darshan timings, rituals and festival dates, not just sightseeing. We book darshan slots where they can be reserved, plan around temple closing hours and keep the pace comfortable for elders.

Popular pilgrimage circuits

  • South India: Tirupati, Madurai, Rameswaram, Kanyakumari and the temple towns of Kanchipuram and Thanjavur.
  • North India: Varanasi, Ayodhya, Mathura & Vrindavan, Haridwar and Rishikesh.
  • Himalayan shrines: Kedarnath, Badrinath and Katra (Vaishno Devi) for Vaishno Devi.
  • Buddhist circuit: Bodh Gaya, Nalanda & Rajgir, Sarnath near Varanasi and Lumbini in Nepal.
  • Abroad: Kathmandu's Pashupatinath and Sri Lanka's Ramayana sites near Nuwara Eliya.

Practical points

Many shrines need advance registration (Kedarnath, Badrinath, Vaishno Devi) or online darshan booking (Tirumala, Shirdi). Dress codes apply in most temples, and mobile phones are not allowed in some. We share these rules before you travel.
