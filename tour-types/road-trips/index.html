The journey is the point

Road trips

Itineraries where the drive is part of the experience.

Road trips are about the in-between: the passes, villages, dhabas and viewpoints between the famous stops.

India by road

  • Himalayan: Manali to Leh, or the Spiti Valley circuit via Kinnaur (summer only).
  • Desert: Jodhpur, Jaisalmer and Bikaner across the Thar.
  • South: the East Coast Road to Puducherry, or Kerala and Karnataka's Western Ghats via Wayanad and Coorg.

Abroad

South Africa's Garden Route, the Scottish Highlands, Iceland's Ring Road and the Icefields Parkway near Banff & the Rockies are among the world's great drives.

With a driver or self-drive?

In India we always recommend a car with an experienced local driver. Abroad, self-drive is common; see Self-drive holidays for what is involved.
