Couples' holidays

Not every romantic trip is a honeymoon. Many of our couples are celebrating a tenth anniversary, taking a babymoon or simply getting away from work and family for a few days. These trips are usually shorter and more focused than honeymoons.

Ideas by trip length

  • Three nights: Puducherry, Coorg, Udaipur or Langkawi for a quick reset.
  • Five nights: Kumarakom and Munnar, Ubud, Krabi or Maldives.
  • A week or more: Switzerland, Greece or Japan for a once-in-a-decade trip.

For babymoons

We pick destinations with short flights, good medical facilities nearby and gentle activities, avoid areas with altitude or vaccination concerns and ask you to check travel plans with your doctor. Kovalam, Singapore and Mauritius are common choices.

Make it feel special

We can arrange a private dinner, a sunset cruise or a spa ritual and confirm each with the hotel. If it is an anniversary, tell us the date; some hotels add a small surprise.
