Senior-friendly holidays

Gentle pacing, shorter transfers and comfortable stays.

Senior travellers often want the same destinations as everyone else, just at a kinder pace. We plan around energy, mobility and medical needs.

How we adjust the trip

  • Fewer hotel changes, with two- or three-night stays as the norm.
  • Short daily drives and comfortable vehicles; flights or trains for long distances.
  • Ground-floor or lift-access rooms requested at hotels, and confirmed where possible.
  • Gentle sightseeing with rest stops, and wheelchairs requested at airports and major sites where available.
  • Medical considerations: we avoid high altitude for travellers with heart or lung conditions unless their doctor approves.

Good choices

Pilgrimages to Tirupati, Madurai and Varanasi; hill stations like Kodaikanal and Munnar; and easy international trips to Singapore, Dubai and Colombo.

FAQs

Senior-friendly holidays FAQs

Can you arrange wheelchair assistance at airports?
Airlines provide wheelchair assistance on request; we help you request it when booking flights.
Are Kedarnath and Vaishno Devi possible for seniors?
Many seniors do them with helicopters, ponies or palanquins. Please consult your doctor first, especially about altitude.
