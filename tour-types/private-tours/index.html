Customised private tours

Apart from our fixed-date group departures, every itinerary on this website is a private tour: your own car and driver or private transfers, your own pace, and hotels chosen for you.

How custom planning works

  1. Tell us the basics: dates, number of travellers, the places or style you have in mind, and a rough budget.
  2. We send a draft plan on WhatsApp or email with a day-by-day route, suggested hotels and a price.
  3. You change what you like: swap hotels, add a night, remove a sight. We revise the quote.
  4. You confirm in writing and pay the deposit stated in your quote. Only then do we book.

What you can customise

Everything: the route, hotel category, room types, guides, activities, meal plans and flights from Chennai or any other city. Ask us about special requirements such as dietary needs, celebrations or mobility.
