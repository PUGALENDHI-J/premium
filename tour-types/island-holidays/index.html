Island holidays

Island trips need a little more planning than beach trips on the mainland: ferries and seaplanes run to schedules, weather matters more and some islands need permits.

India's islands

  • Andaman Islands: Port Blair, Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) and Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep), with private ferries between them.
  • Lakshadweep: Agatti and Bangaram, with entry permits arranged in advance.

Abroad

  • Indian Ocean: Maldives, Mauritius and Seychelles.
  • Southeast Asia: Phuket, Koh Samui, Phi Phi Islands, Langkawi, Bali (South Coast), Lombok & the Gili Islands and El Nido (Palawan).
  • Further afield: Santorini, Mykonos, Zanzibar, Jeju Island and Fiji.

Island tips

Never book a flight home on the same day as a ferry back from an outer island. Carry cash, as card machines can be unreliable, and use reef-safe sunscreen.

Do I need a permit for the Andamans?
Indian citizens do not need a permit for the main tourist islands; some restricted areas do. Lakshadweep needs a permit for every visitor.
