Hill-station holidays

Summer escapes and misty winter retreats in India's hills.

From Chennai, the nearest hills are a few hours away, and the classic Himalayan hill stations are a short flight plus a drive.

South Indian hills

Ooty and Coonoor in the Nilgiris, Kodaikanal in the Palanis, Munnar in Kerala and Coorg in Karnataka are best from October to June; April and May are peak summer escapes.

Himalayan hills

Shimla, Manali, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling and Gangtok are pleasant from March to June and again in autumn; December to February brings snow to many of them.

Hill stations abroad

Sri Lanka's Nuwara Eliya, Malaysia's Cameron Highlands and Vietnam's Sapa combine tea estates with cool weather.

Tips

Hill roads are slow; we plan realistic drive times. Pack layers even in summer, and a rain jacket in the monsoon months.
