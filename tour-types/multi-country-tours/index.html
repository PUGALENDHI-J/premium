Multi-country tours

Two or more countries, one plan, one quote.

Combining countries makes the most of a long flight, as long as the route is logical and visas are planned in advance.

Classic combinations

  • Southeast Asia: Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok or Phuket by short flights.
  • Europe: Paris, Lucerne and Interlaken & Jungfrau, then Venice and Rome by train.
  • Indochina: Hanoi, Siem Reap (Angkor) and Luang Prabang.
  • Middle East: Dubai with Istanbul and Cappadocia, or with Muscat.
  • Africa: Masai Mara and the Serengeti, or a safari with Zanzibar.

Visas

Each country has its own entry rules, and some (such as the Schengen area) cover several countries with one visa. We sequence applications and give you a document checklist.
