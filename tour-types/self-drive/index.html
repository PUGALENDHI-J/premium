Self-drive holidays

Pre-planned routes with car hire and hotels, driven by you.

On a self-drive holiday we plan the route, book the car and hotels, and send you a day-by-day driving plan. You set the pace.

Where self-drive works well

New Zealand, Iceland (in summer), the Scottish Highlands, South Africa's Garden Route, Canada's Rockies around Banff & the Rockies and Australia's Great Ocean Road from Melbourne & the Great Ocean Road are all popular with Indian drivers.

What you need

  • A valid Indian driving licence and, in most countries, an International Driving Permit from your RTO, issued before you leave.
  • A credit card in the driver's name for the car hire deposit.
  • Comfort driving on the left (UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa) or right (Iceland, Canada, USA, Europe).

Our advice

Avoid winter self-drives in Iceland and the Rockies unless you are experienced on snow and ice; we suggest guided tours instead. Keep daily drives to four or five hours so you can stop at viewpoints.
