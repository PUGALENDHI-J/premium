Food and culinary holidays

Street food, markets and cooking classes as the heart of the trip.

On a food holiday, meals are the sightseeing. We build days around markets, street-food walks and cooking classes, with time to rest in between.

Great food cities

  • India: Hyderabad for biryani and haleem, Lucknow for kebabs, Kolkata for sweets, Delhi's Old City, Chennai's tiffin rooms and Amritsar's kulchas.
  • Asia: Bangkok, Penang, Hoi An, Osaka and Seoul.
  • Europe: Florence & Tuscany and Rome for pasta and gelato, Lisbon for pastries, Barcelona for tapas.

Vegetarian travellers

We note where vegetarian food is easy (India, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand's cities, Italy) and where it takes planning (parts of Japan, Korea and Vietnam), and we recommend restaurants accordingly.
