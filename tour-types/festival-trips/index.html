Festival and seasonal trips

Some trips are worth timing to the week. Festival dates often follow lunar calendars, so we confirm them each year before you book.

Festivals in India

  • Durga Puja in Kolkata (September or October).
  • Pushkar Camel Fair at Pushkar (October or November).
  • Hornbill Festival near Kohima (early December).
  • Rann Utsav in Rann of Kutch (winter months).
  • Holi in Mathura & Vrindavan (February or March).

Festivals and seasons abroad

  • Yi Peng lantern festival in Chiang Mai (November); see our Thailand Honeymoon Special: Lantern of Love.
  • Cherry blossom in Kyoto and Tokyo (late March to early April).
  • Tulips at Keukenhof near Amsterdam (Netherlands, spring) and in Srinagar (April).
  • Northern lights in Tromsø, Rovaniemi (Lapland) and Iceland (autumn to early spring).
  • Snow Festival in Sapporo & Hokkaido (February).

Hotels near festivals fill up early and cost more, so plan three to six months ahead.
