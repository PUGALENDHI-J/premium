The Maldives

The most important decision for a beach holiday is the month, because monsoons affect coastlines very differently.

Which beach, which month

MonthsGood choices
November to FebruaryNorth Goa, South Goa, Kovalam, Varkala, Phuket, Krabi, Maldives
March to MayHavelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Bentota, Langkawi, Mauritius (late), Maldives
June to SeptemberBali (South Coast), Koh Samui, Zanzibar, Santorini, Nice & the French Riviera

Types of beach trip

  • Lively: North Goa, Bali (South Coast), Mykonos, Phuket.
  • Quiet and romantic: South Goa, Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep), Langkawi, Seychelles.
  • Snorkelling and diving: Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Maldives, El Nido (Palawan), Cairns & the Great Barrier Reef.
