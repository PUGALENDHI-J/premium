Into the wild

Wildlife safaris

Tigers, rhinos, lions and the Great Migration, with safaris booked early.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but good planning improves the odds a lot: the right season, the right zone, enough safaris and a good naturalist.

India's best parks

  • Tigers: Ranthambore, Bandhavgarh National Park, Kanha National Park and Jim Corbett National Park. Most parks close during the monsoon; April and May bring the best sightings.
  • Rhinos: Kaziranga National Park, open roughly November to April.
  • Asiatic lions: Gir National Park, the only place in the world to see them, closed mid-June to mid-October.
  • Elephants and leopards: Kabini and Thekkady.

Africa

The Great Migration moves between the Serengeti and the Masai Mara through the year; river crossings usually happen from July to October. Kruger National Park in South Africa offers excellent Big Five game drives, and Ngorongoro Crater is a natural amphitheatre full of wildlife.

Booking tips

Indian park permits open months in advance and sell out quickly, especially for popular zones. Book safaris as soon as your dates are fixed.
