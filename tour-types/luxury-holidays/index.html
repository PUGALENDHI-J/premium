The finer details

Luxury holidays

Premium hotels, private guides and every transfer handled.

Luxury travel is less about thread counts and more about removing friction: the right room, a guide who knows when to talk and when not to, a car waiting when you land and no queues where they can be avoided.

How a luxury trip differs

  • Hotel selection by experience, not just star rating: a lake-facing palace room in Udaipur, an overwater villa in the Maldives, a caldera suite in Santorini.
  • Private guides and vehicles throughout, with skip-the-line entry where it exists.
  • Fewer, longer stays so you enjoy the hotel rather than just sleeping in it.
  • Special access where possible, such as early entry or private tours, confirmed in writing before you travel.

Where luxury shines

Rajasthan's heritage palaces, Kerala's plantation bungalows and lake villas, Japan's ryokans near Mount Fuji & Hakone, safari lodges in Kruger National Park and Masai Mara, and private-island resorts in the Seychelles.
