Solo travel

Safe, sociable destinations with support when you need it.

Solo travel is one of the best ways to see a place, and it is easier than many people think. We plan the logistics and stay reachable on WhatsApp throughout.

Good first solo destinations

  • In India: Rishikesh for yoga and rafting, Varkala's cliff cafés, Hampi's ruins and Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj's monasteries.
  • Abroad: Chiang Mai, Ubud, Luang Prabang, Kyoto and Lisbon are safe, walkable and full of other travellers.

How we support solo travellers

  • Centrally located hotels with good reviews from solo guests.
  • Airport pick-ups on arrival, especially for late flights.
  • Small-group day tours where you can meet people.
  • A local contact and our team on WhatsApp.

Single rooms often cost almost the same as doubles; we show the single supplement clearly in your quote.
