Quick escapes

Short breaks

Trips of two to four days, in India and a short flight away.

A short break is about choosing well. One destination, one hotel and a plan that leaves time to relax.

Rules of thumb

  • Keep the journey to under four hours door to door.
  • Stay in one base.
  • Book an arrival-day activity that does not need energy, like a sunset or a spa.

Ideas

In India: Puducherry, Coorg, Hampi, Munnar and Udaipur. Abroad from Chennai: Colombo, Singapore, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur and Dubai.
