Learning on the road

Educational tours

Study trips with clear learning goals, safe logistics and supervised schedules.

An educational tour should connect to what students are studying. We start with your learning goals, then build the route, guides and activities around them.

Examples of curriculum links

  • History: the Mughal monuments of Delhi and Agra, the Chola temples of Thanjavur, the ruins of Hampi.
  • Science and astronomy: the Jantar Mantar observatories in Jaipur and Ujjain.
  • Geography and ecology: mangroves in the Sundarbans, tea estates in Munnar, volcanic landscapes in Iceland.
  • Engineering: the Statue of Unity and Sardar Sarovar Dam, the Pamban bridge at Rameswaram.

Safety and supervision

We plan hotels with secure floors for groups, keep the teacher-to-student ratio you require, and share daily schedules, emergency contacts and dietary lists in advance. Parental consent forms and ID requirements are the school's responsibility, but we send a checklist.
