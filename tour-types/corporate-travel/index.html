Corporate travel

Corporate travel has different priorities from a holiday: a clear budget owner, fixed dates, invoicing and someone on call if plans change.

What we handle

  • Group flights and visas for teams travelling from one or several cities.
  • Venues for meetings or offsites, with projector, breakout rooms and meals.
  • Team activities: a desert safari in Dubai, a cruise in Singapore, a beach day in North Goa or a cooking class in Bangkok.
  • Leisure extensions for employees who want to stay on after a conference.

Good offsite destinations

Within India, Coorg, Udaipur, North Goa and Rishikesh work well for two- to four-night retreats. Abroad, Dubai, Singapore, Bangkok and Kuala Lumpur are short flights with plenty of venues.

Tell us your headcount, dates and budget per person and we will send options with an itemised quote.
