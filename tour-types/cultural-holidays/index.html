Stories in stone

Cultural and heritage holidays

Monuments, museums, crafts and living traditions with expert guides.

A cultural holiday works best with fewer places and more time, plus a guide who can tell the stories behind what you are seeing.

Great cultural journeys

  • India: the forts and palaces of Rajasthan, the Chola temples of Thanjavur, Hampi's ruined capital, the caves at Aurangabad (Ajanta & Ellora) and the ghats of Varanasi.
  • Asia: Siem Reap (Angkor) for Angkor, Kyoto, Yogyakarta's Borobudur and Luang Prabang.
  • Europe and beyond: Rome, Florence & Tuscany, Istanbul, Cairo & Giza and Luxor, Petra and Cusco & the Sacred Valley.

What we add

Licensed local guides, timed tickets for sites that need them, craft workshops (block printing in Jaipur, silk weaving in Kanchipuram, cooking classes in Hoi An) and performances such as Kathakali in Kochi.
