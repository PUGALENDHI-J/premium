Long-stay holidays

A longer trip lets you see more without rushing, or go deep into one region.

Two approaches

  • Grand tours that cover a whole country or region: Rajasthan's royal cities, the length of Japan, or Australia and New Zealand together.
  • Slow stays in fewer places: a week of Ayurveda in Kovalam, a month in Ubud or Chiang Mai, or a base in Lisbon with day trips.

Planning tips

Build in rest days every four or five days, keep laundry stops in mind, and think about travel insurance that covers the full trip. For international long stays, check how many days your visa allows.
