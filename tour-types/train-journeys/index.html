Scenic train journeys

Toy trains, mountain railways and bullet trains.

Some journeys are better by rail. These are the ones we build trips around.

In India

  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway to Ooty, a UNESCO-listed rack railway climbing from Mettupalayam.
  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway in Darjeeling, with the Batasia Loop.
  • Kalka–Shimla Railway to Shimla, with over a hundred tunnels.
  • The Araku line from Visakhapatnam to Araku Valley, best in a vistadome coach.

Abroad

  • Sri Lanka's Kandy to Ella line through tea country.
  • Switzerland's Glacier Express and GoldenPass linking Zermatt, Interlaken & Jungfrau and Lucerne.
  • Norway's Flåm Railway near Bergen & the Fjords.
  • Japan's Shinkansen between Tokyo and Kyoto.

Booking tips

Toy train and panoramic train seats sell out early; we book them as soon as you confirm. Reserved seats are often needed even with a rail pass.
