Weekend getaways

A good weekend trip needs a short journey, one base and no more than two big activities. Anything longer than four or five hours each way usually eats into the fun.

From Chennai by road or rail

Puducherry and Mahabalipuram along the East Coast Road, Yercaud via Salem, and Tirupati for a pilgrimage weekend.

A short flight away

North Goa, Coorg (via Mangaluru or Kannur), Kochi and Alleppey, and Hyderabad for a food weekend.

International weekends

With a Friday evening flight, Colombo, Singapore and Dubai all work for a long weekend from Chennai, as long as your visa and entry paperwork are ready in advance.
