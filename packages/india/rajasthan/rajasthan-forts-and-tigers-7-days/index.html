Rajasthan Forts & Tigers

7 days / 6 nightsJaipur · Ranthambore · UdaipurOctober to March

Trip overview

Combine palaces with the wild: Jaipur, three safaris at Ranthambore and the lakes of Udaipur.

The route spends 2 nights in Jaipur, 2 nights in Ranthambore and 2 nights in Udaipur, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits wildlife safaris and cultural holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Jaipur 2N
  2. Ranthambore 2N
  3. Udaipur 2N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Jaipur

  • Arrive via Jaipur International Airport (JAI) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Hawa Mahal: Palace of Winds with 953 windows

Day 2Jaipur: City Palace and Jantar Mantar

  • City Palace: Royal residence and museum
  • Jantar Mantar: UNESCO-listed astronomical observatory

Day 3Jaipur to Ranthambore

  • Travel from Jaipur to Ranthambore by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Ranthambore hotel
  • Ranthambore National Park safaris: Jeep (gypsy) and canter safaris across zones

Day 4Ranthambore: Tiger safari and Ranthambore Fort

  • Tiger safari
  • Ranthambore Fort: UNESCO-listed hill fort

Day 5Ranthambore to Udaipur

  • Travel from Ranthambore to Udaipur by road or train, or fly where it saves time (we compare options in your quote)
  • Check in at your Udaipur hotel
  • Lake Pichola boat ride: Views of the Lake Palace and Jag Mandir

Day 6Udaipur: Jagdish Temple and Saheliyon ki Bari

  • Jagdish Temple: Seventeenth-century Indo-Aryan temple
  • Saheliyon ki Bari: Garden of the maidens

Day 7Depart from Udaipur

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Safari permits booked as soon as dates are fixed, as zones sell out
  • Early-morning and afternoon drives when animals are most active
  • Lodges chosen close to park gates
  • Naturalist-led drives where available
  • Licensed local guides at the major heritage sites

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Jaipur: Reach Amber Fort at opening time to avoid the heat and the crowds.
  • Ranthambore: Book at least two or three safaris to improve your chances of a tiger sighting.
  • Udaipur: Book a lake-view room in the old city; the evening view over Pichola is the reason to come.
