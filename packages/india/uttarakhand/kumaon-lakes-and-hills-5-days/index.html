Kumaon Lakes & Hills

5 days / 4 nightsNainitalMarch to June and September to November

Trip overview

Nainital, Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal, with a day trip to Mukteshwar's orchards and Himalayan views.

The route spends 4 nights in Nainital, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits hill-station holidays and couples' holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Nainital 4N

Highlights

Naini LakeNaina Devi TempleSnow View Point

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Nainital

  • Arrive via Pantnagar Airport (PGH) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Naina Devi Temple: Lakeside Shakti shrine

Day 2Nainital: Snow View Point and Tiffin Top

  • Snow View Point: Cable car to Himalayan views
  • Tiffin Top: Viewpoint reached on foot or horseback

Day 3Nainital: Bhimtal and Sattal and Mukteshwar

  • Bhimtal and Sattal: Quieter lakes nearby
  • Mukteshwar: Orchards and Himalayan views, 50 km away

Day 4Nainital: Cable car and Lake-hopping day

  • Cable car
  • Lake-hopping day

Day 5Depart from Nainital

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Pantnagar Airport (PGH) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Realistic drive times on hill roads
  • Viewpoints planned for clear mornings
  • Warm layers advised even in summer
  • A relaxed pace with two-night stays where possible
  • One special dinner or experience suggested at each stop

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Nainital: Stay at Bhimtal or Sattal if you prefer quiet; Nainital is a short drive away.

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.

Nainital: March to June and September to November.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Nainital (Pantnagar Airport (PGH), about 65 km). We can add flights or train tickets from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
