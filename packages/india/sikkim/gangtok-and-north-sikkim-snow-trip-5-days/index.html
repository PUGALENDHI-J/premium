Gangtok & North Sikkim Snow Trip

5 days / 4 nightsGangtok · Lachung & YumthangMarch to May and October to December

Trip overview

Snow at Zero Point, rhododendrons in Yumthang and Tsomgo Lake from Gangtok, with all permits arranged.

The route spends 2 nights in Gangtok and 2 nights in Lachung & Yumthang, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits hill-station holidays and adventure holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Gangtok 2N
  2. Lachung & Yumthang 2N

Highlights

MG MargTsomgo (Changu) LakeNathula PassYumthang ValleyZero Point (Yumesamdong)Lachung Monastery

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Gangtok

  • Arrive via Pakyong Airport (PYG) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Banjhakri Falls: Park and waterfall

Day 2Gangtok: Ropeway and MG Marg

  • Ropeway
  • MG Marg: Car-free promenade

Day 3Gangtok to Lachung & Yumthang

  • Travel from Gangtok to Lachung & Yumthang by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Lachung & Yumthang hotel
  • Yumthang Valley: Rhododendron valley with hot springs

Day 4Lachung & Yumthang: Zero Point (Yumesamdong) and Bhim Nala Falls

  • Zero Point (Yumesamdong): Snowfields at about 4,700 m
  • Bhim Nala Falls: Waterfall on the road

Day 5Depart from Lachung & Yumthang

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Bagdogra (IXB) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Realistic drive times on hill roads
  • Viewpoints planned for clear mornings
  • Warm layers advised even in summer
  • Activities booked with licensed, insured operators
  • Fitness level and experience checked before we confirm each activity

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Gangtok: Carry ID photos and copies; permits for Tsomgo and Nathula are issued a day ahead.
  • Lachung & Yumthang: North Sikkim needs a Protected Area Permit and a registered local vehicle; we arrange both.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Gangtok: March to May and October to December. Lachung & Yumthang: March to June and October to December.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Gangtok (Pakyong Airport (PYG), about 35 km (limited flights); Bagdogra (IXB), about 125 km). We can add flights or train tickets from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
