Goa Monsoon & Dudhsagar Trip

4 days / 3 nightsSouth GoaNovember to February

Trip overview

Goa in its green season at lower prices, with a jeep trip to the Dudhsagar falls once the forest route reopens after the rains.

The route spends 3 nights in South Goa, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits adventure holidays and budget holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. South Goa 3N

Highlights

Palolem BeachColva and Benaulim beachesBasilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in South Goa

  • Arrive via Dabolim Airport (GOI) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Kayaking

Day 2South Goa: Palolem Beach and Colva and Benaulim beaches

  • Palolem Beach: Crescent bay with calm water
  • Colva and Benaulim beaches: Long, wide beaches

Day 3South Goa: Basilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa and Cabo de Rama Fort

  • Basilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa: UNESCO church holding St Francis Xavier's remains
  • Cabo de Rama Fort: Clifftop fort with sea views

Day 4Depart from South Goa

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Dabolim Airport (GOI) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Activities booked with licensed, insured operators
  • Fitness level and experience checked before we confirm each activity
  • Weather and season buffers built in for outdoor days
  • Travel insurance covering the activities strongly recommended
  • Well-reviewed three-star or guesthouse stays in central locations

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • South Goa: Visit Dudhsagar soon after the monsoon (October–November) when the falls are fullest and jeeps are running.

Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
