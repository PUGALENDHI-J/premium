Pachmarhi Hill Weekend

3 days / 2 nightsPachmarhiOctober to June

Trip overview

Bee Falls, Jata Shankar caves and sunset at Dhoopgarh in Madhya Pradesh's only hill station.

The route spends 2 nights in Pachmarhi, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits hill-station holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Pachmarhi

  • Arrive via Bhopal (BHO) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Waterfall walks

Day 2Pachmarhi: Sunset at Dhoopgarh and Bee Falls

  • Sunset at Dhoopgarh
  • Bee Falls: Waterfall with a natural pool

Day 3Depart from Pachmarhi

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Bhopal (BHO) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Fits a Friday-to-Sunday break
  • Short journey from Chennai or nearby cities
  • A relaxed final morning before heading home
  • Realistic drive times on hill roads
  • Viewpoints planned for clear mornings

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Pachmarhi: Hire a registered gypsy from the town office; private vehicles cannot enter most sights.
