Trip overview

India's classic Golden Triangle as a group tour with a tour manager: Old and New Delhi, sunrise at the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri, and Jaipur's Amber Fort and bazaars.

The group meets in Delhi for Akshardham, then spends a full day in Old Delhi's Chandni Chowk by cycle rickshaw and New Delhi's India Gate, Qutub Minar and Lotus Temple. An expressway drive leads to Agra for the Agra Fort and a sunset view of the Taj from Mehtab Bagh, with the Taj itself at sunrise the next morning. You stop at Fatehpur Sikri on the way to Jaipur, where two nights cover the Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and the bazaars.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Delhi

  • Arrive at Delhi airport or railway station, meet the group and your tour manager
  • Swaminarayan Akshardham and the evening water show (closed on Mondays)
  • Welcome dinner

Day 2Old and New Delhi

  • Jama Masjid and a cycle-rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk
  • Red Fort (outside view) and Raj Ghat
  • India Gate, the President's House drive-past and Qutub Minar
  • Lotus Temple (closed on Mondays)

Day 3Delhi to Agra

  • Drive to Agra on the Yamuna Expressway (about 4 hours)
  • Agra Fort
  • Sunset view of the Taj Mahal from Mehtab Bagh
  • Marble inlay workshop

Day 4Taj Mahal sunrise, Fatehpur Sikri, Jaipur

  • Taj Mahal at sunrise (closed on Fridays)
  • Fatehpur Sikri and the Buland Darwaza on the way
  • Arrive in Jaipur and check in

Day 5Jaipur, the Pink City

  • Amber Fort, with a jeep up to the fort
  • Photo stop at Jal Mahal
  • City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal
  • Shopping at Johari and Bapu bazaars

Day 6Depart from Jaipur

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Drop at Jaipur airport, or drive back to Delhi airport (about 5 to 6 hours)

Good to know

  • Delhi: Start Old Delhi early, before the lanes fill up, and keep New Delhi's wide avenues for the afternoon.
  • Agra: Enter the Taj at sunrise through the East Gate; it is the least crowded and the light is beautiful.
  • Jaipur: Reach Amber Fort at opening time to avoid the heat and the crowds.
