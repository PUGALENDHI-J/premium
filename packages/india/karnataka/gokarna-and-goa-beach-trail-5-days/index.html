Gokarna & Goa Beach Trail

5 days / 4 nightsGokarna · South GoaOctober to March

Trip overview

Quiet coves at Gokarna and the long beaches of South Goa, linked by a short drive along the Konkan coast.

The route spends 2 nights in Gokarna and 2 nights in South Goa, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits beach holidays, couples' holidays and solo travel, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Gokarna 2N
  2. South Goa 2N

Highlights

Mahabaleshwar TempleOm BeachKudle BeachPalolem BeachColva and Benaulim beachesBasilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Gokarna

  • Arrive via Goa's Dabolim Airport (GOI) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Om Beach: Beach shaped like the Om symbol

Day 2Gokarna: Kudle Beach and Half Moon and Paradise beaches

  • Kudle Beach: Relaxed beach with cafés
  • Half Moon and Paradise beaches: Secluded coves reached by trail or boat

Day 3Gokarna to South Goa

  • Travel from Gokarna to South Goa by private vehicle
  • Check in at your South Goa hotel
  • Colva and Benaulim beaches: Long, wide beaches

Day 4South Goa: Cabo de Rama Fort and Dudhsagar Falls

  • Cabo de Rama Fort: Clifftop fort with sea views
  • Dudhsagar Falls: Four-tier waterfall in the forest (seasonal jeep access)

Day 5Depart from South Goa

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Dabolim Airport (GOI) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Beachfront or walk-to-beach hotels requested
  • Boat and island trips planned for calm-sea mornings
  • Free beach days between excursions
  • A relaxed pace with two-night stays where possible
  • One special dinner or experience suggested at each stop

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Gokarna: Do the beach trek from Kudle to Paradise early in the day and boat back.
  • South Goa: Visit Dudhsagar soon after the monsoon (October–November) when the falls are fullest and jeeps are running.

Gokarna: October to March. South Goa: November to February.
