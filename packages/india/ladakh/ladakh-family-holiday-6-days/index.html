Ladakh Family Holiday

6 days / 5 nightsLeh · Nubra Valley · Pangong LakeMay to September

Trip overview

A gentler Ladakh itinerary with an extra day in Leh, a heated camp at Pangong and short daily drives.

The route spends 3 nights in Leh, 1 night in Nubra Valley and 1 night in Pangong Lake, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits family holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Leh 3N
  2. Nubra Valley 1N
  3. Pangong Lake 1N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Leh

  • Arrive via Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport (IXL) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Hall of Fame: Army museum

Day 2Leh: Leh Palace and Shanti Stupa

  • Leh Palace: Seventeenth-century royal palace
  • Shanti Stupa: White stupa with sunset views

Day 3Leh: Thiksey Monastery and Hemis Monastery

  • Thiksey Monastery: Monastery resembling the Potala Palace
  • Hemis Monastery: Largest monastery, with a summer festival

Day 4Leh to Nubra Valley

  • Travel from Leh to Nubra Valley by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Nubra Valley hotel
  • Hunder sand dunes: Bactrian camel rides

Day 5Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake

  • Travel from Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Pangong Lake hotel
  • Pangong lakeshore: Changing colours of the lake

Day 6Depart from Pangong Lake

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Leh Airport (IXL) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote
  • One active day followed by a slower morning
  • Child-friendly sights and meals noted for each stop

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Leh: Rest completely on arrival day; altitude sickness is common if you rush.
  • Nubra Valley: An Inner Line Permit is needed for Nubra; carry several printed copies.
  • Pangong Lake: Nights at Pangong are freezing even in summer; camps provide heaters or electric blankets at a cost.
