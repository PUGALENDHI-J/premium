Kerala & Kanyakumari Grand Tour

9 days / 8 nightsKochi · Munnar · Thekkady · Kumarakom · Kovalam · KanyakumariOctober to March

Trip overview

Nine days from Kochi to the tip of India through tea hills, spice country, Vembanad Lake and the Kovalam coast.

The route spends 1 night in Kochi, 2 nights in Munnar, 1 night in Thekkady, 1 night in Kumarakom, 2 nights in Kovalam and 1 night in Kanyakumari, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits family holidays and cultural holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Kochi

  • Arrive via Cochin International Airport (COK) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Kerala Folklore Museum: Three floors of masks, costumes and woodwork

Day 2Kochi to Munnar

  • Travel from Kochi to Munnar by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Munnar hotel
  • Tea Museum (KDHP): History of tea and a working factory

Day 3Munnar: Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point

  • Mattupetty Dam: Lake with boating and elephant sightings nearby
  • Echo Point: Lakeside spot known for its echoes

Day 4Munnar to Thekkady

  • Travel from Munnar to Thekkady by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Thekkady hotel
  • Periyar Lake boat safari: Wildlife cruise on the reserve's lake

Day 5Thekkady to Kumarakom

  • Travel from Thekkady to Kumarakom by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Kumarakom hotel
  • Pathiramanal Island: Small island reached by boat

Day 6Kumarakom to Kovalam

  • Travel from Kumarakom to Kovalam by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Kovalam hotel
  • Lighthouse Beach: Main beach with a climbable lighthouse

Day 7Kovalam: Hawa Beach and Samudra Beach

  • Hawa Beach: Rocky headland beach
  • Samudra Beach: Quieter northern beach

Day 8Kovalam to Kanyakumari

  • Travel from Kovalam to Kanyakumari by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Kanyakumari hotel
  • Thiruvalluvar Statue: Giant statue of the Tamil poet, linked by a glass bridge

Day 9Depart from Kanyakumari

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Kochi: Stay in Fort Kochi rather than Ernakulam to walk the heritage streets in the evening.
  • Munnar: Book Eravikulam tickets online and go early; the park shuttle has long queues by mid-morning.
  • Thekkady: Book the first Periyar boat of the morning; animal sightings are best early and tickets are limited.
  • Kumarakom: Pick a lake-facing room; the sunset over Vembanad is the highlight of a stay.
  • Kovalam: Lifeguards patrol only parts of the beach; swim between their flags.
  • Kanyakumari: Be at the ferry jetty early; queues for the rock memorial build quickly after sunrise.
