Varkala, Kerala

Trip overview

Clifftop cafés, yoga classes, surf lessons and sunset over Papanasam beach: an easy three-night reset on Kerala's red cliffs.

The route spends 3 nights in Varkala, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits solo travel and beach holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Varkala 3N

Highlights

North CliffPapanasam BeachJanardana Swamy Temple

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Varkala

  • Arrive via Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: North Cliff: Clifftop path with cafés and sea views

Day 2Varkala: Yoga classes and Clifftop café hopping

  • Yoga classes
  • Clifftop café hopping

Day 3Varkala: Papanasam Beach and Janardana Swamy Temple

  • Papanasam Beach: Sacred beach below the cliffs
  • Janardana Swamy Temple: Two-thousand-year-old Vishnu temple

Day 4Depart from Varkala

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Central, well-reviewed hotels with good solo-guest feedback
  • Airport pick-up on arrival
  • Small-group day tours where you can meet people
  • One or two bases only
  • Easy arrival-day plans after the journey

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Varkala: Stay on North Cliff for the atmosphere; the walk down to the beach is steep, so pack light.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Varkala: October to March.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Varkala (Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV), about 50 km). We can add flights or train tickets from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
