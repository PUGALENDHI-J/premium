Spiti Valley Road Trip

9 days / 8 nightsShimla · Spiti Valley · ManaliMarch to June; December to February for snow

Trip overview

A high-desert circuit for experienced travellers: in through Kinnaur, monasteries at Key and Tabo, Chandratal Lake and out over Kunzum Pass.

The route spends 1 night in Shimla, 6 nights in Spiti Valley and 1 night in Manali, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits adventure holidays, road trips and photography holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Shimla 1N
  2. Spiti Valley 6N
  3. Manali 1N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Shimla

  • Arrive via Shimla Airport (SLV) has limited flights; Chandigarh (IXC) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Christ Church: Neo-Gothic church from the 1850s

Day 2Shimla to Spiti Valley

  • Travel from Shimla to Spiti Valley by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Spiti Valley hotel
  • Key Monastery: Hilltop monastery above Kaza

Day 3Spiti Valley: Chandratal Lake and Tabo Monastery

  • Chandratal Lake: Crescent-shaped high-altitude lake
  • Tabo Monastery: Founded in 996 CE, with ancient murals

Day 4Spiti Valley: Dhankar Monastery and Langza and Komic

  • Dhankar Monastery: Clifftop monastery and lake
  • Langza and Komic: High villages with fossils and views

Day 5Spiti Valley: Pin Valley National Park and Road trip

  • Pin Valley National Park: Snow leopard habitat
  • Road trip

Day 6Spiti Valley: Monastery visits and Stargazing

  • Monastery visits
  • Stargazing

Day 7Spiti Valley: Homestays and Short high-altitude walks

  • Homestays
  • Short high-altitude walks

Day 8Spiti Valley to Manali

  • Travel from Spiti Valley to Manali by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Manali hotel
  • Paragliding

Day 9Depart from Manali

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Kullu–Manali Airport (Bhuntar for your journey home

Good to know

  • Shimla: Ride the toy train one way and drive the other, to see both the tunnels and the valley views.
  • Spiti Valley: Go in via Kinnaur and out via Manali so you gain altitude gradually.
  • Manali: Rohtang needs an online permit and is closed on Tuesdays; Sissu via the Atal Tunnel is an easy alternative.

Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Shimla: March to June; December to February for snow. Spiti Valley: June to September. Manali: March to June; December to February for snow.
