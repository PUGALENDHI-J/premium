Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Trip overview

From Manali over the Hampta Pass at about 4,270 metres into the barren Lahaul valley, with a camp near Chandratal lake, June to September.

The route spends 6 nights in Manali, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits adventure holidays and group tours, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Manali 6N

Highlights

Hadimba TempleOld ManaliSolang Valley

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Manali

  • Arrive via Kullu–Manali Airport (Bhuntar and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Solang Valley: Paragliding, ropeway and snow sports

Day 2Manali: Paragliding and River rafting in Kullu

  • Paragliding
  • River rafting in Kullu

Day 3Manali: Trek to Jogini Falls and Hadimba Temple

  • Trek to Jogini Falls
  • Hadimba Temple: Wooden temple in a cedar forest

Day 4Manali: Old Manali and Atal Tunnel and Sissu

  • Old Manali: Cafés and village lanes
  • Atal Tunnel and Sissu: Tunnel to Lahaul and waterfalls beyond

Day 5Manali: Rohtang Pass and Vashisht hot springs

  • Rohtang Pass: High pass with snow (permit required, seasonal)
  • Vashisht hot springs: Natural hot springs and temple

Day 6Manali: Snow activities and Café hopping

  • Snow activities
  • Café hopping

Day 7Depart from Manali

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Kullu–Manali Airport (Bhuntar for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Activities booked with licensed, insured operators
  • Fitness level and experience checked before we confirm each activity
  • Weather and season buffers built in for outdoor days
  • Travel insurance covering the activities strongly recommended
  • Vehicle sized for the whole group and its luggage

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Manali: Rohtang needs an online permit and is closed on Tuesdays; Sissu via the Atal Tunnel is an easy alternative.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Manali: March to June; December to February for snow.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Manali (Kullu–Manali Airport (Bhuntar, KUU), about 50 km, with limited flights; Chandigarh (IXC), about 300 km). We can add flights or train tickets from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
