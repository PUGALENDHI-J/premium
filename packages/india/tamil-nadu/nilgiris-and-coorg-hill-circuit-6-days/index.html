Nilgiris & Coorg Hill Circuit

6 days / 5 nightsOoty · Mysuru · CoorgOctober to June

Trip overview

A cross-state hill loop: Ooty's gardens and toy train, Mysuru's palace on the way down and two nights among Coorg's coffee estates.

The route spends 2 nights in Ooty, 1 night in Mysuru and 2 nights in Coorg, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits hill-station holidays, family holidays and road trips, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Ooty 2N
  2. Mysuru 1N
  3. Coorg 2N

Highlights

Nilgiri Mountain RailwayGovernment Botanical GardenDoddabetta PeakMysore PalaceChamundi HillBrindavan GardensAbbey FallsRaja's SeatNamdroling Monastery (Golden Temple)

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Ooty

  • Arrive via Coimbatore International Airport (CJB) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Nilgiri Mountain Railway: Steam-hauled toy train from Mettupalayam, UNESCO-listed

Day 2Ooty: Government Botanical Garden and Doddabetta Peak

  • Government Botanical Garden: Terraced gardens laid out in 1848
  • Doddabetta Peak: Highest point in the Nilgiris

Day 3Ooty to Mysuru

  • Travel from Ooty to Mysuru by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Mysuru hotel
  • Market walk

Day 4Mysuru to Coorg

  • Travel from Mysuru to Coorg by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Coorg hotel
  • Abbey Falls: Waterfall set in coffee and spice plantations

Day 5Coorg: Raja's Seat and Mandalpatti

  • Raja's Seat: Sunset viewpoint over the valleys
  • Mandalpatti: Jeep ride to a hilltop viewpoint

Day 6Depart from Coorg

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Mangaluru International Airport (IXE) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Realistic drive times on hill roads
  • Viewpoints planned for clear mornings
  • Warm layers advised even in summer
  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Ooty: Book the toy train well ahead and ride uphill from Mettupalayam in the morning for the best views.
  • Mysuru: Visit the palace by day for the interiors and come back on a Sunday evening for the lights.
  • Coorg: Stay on a working coffee estate; the hosts often include a plantation walk.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Ooty: October to June. Mysuru: October to February. Coorg: October to March.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Ooty (Coimbatore International Airport (CJB), about 85 km). We can add flights or train tickets from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
