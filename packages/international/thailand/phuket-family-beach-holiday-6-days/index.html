Phuket Family Beach Holiday

6 days / 5 nightsPhuketNovember to April

Trip overview

Five nights at a family-friendly Kata or Karon resort with island trips, an elephant sanctuary and a show night.

The route spends 5 nights in Phuket, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits family holidays and beach holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Phuket 5N

Highlights

Patong BeachKata and Karon beachesBig Buddha

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Phuket

  • Arrive via Phuket International Airport (HKT) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Patong Beach: Busiest beach and Bangla Road nightlife

Day 2Phuket: Kata and Karon beaches and Phuket FantaSea show

  • Kata and Karon beaches: Family-friendly beaches
  • Phuket FantaSea show

Day 3Phuket: Big Buddha and Phang Nga Bay

  • Big Buddha: Hilltop statue with island views
  • Phang Nga Bay: Limestone islands and James Bond Island

Day 4Phuket: Old Phuket Town and Promthep Cape

  • Old Phuket Town: Sino-Portuguese shophouses
  • Promthep Cape: Sunset viewpoint

Day 5Phuket: Phi Phi day trip and Phang Nga Bay sea-canoeing

  • Phi Phi day trip
  • Phang Nga Bay sea-canoeing

Day 6Depart from Phuket

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Phuket International Airport (HKT) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote
  • One active day followed by a slower morning
  • Child-friendly sights and meals noted for each stop
  • Beachfront or walk-to-beach hotels requested

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Phuket: Stay at Kata or Karon for a calmer base than Patong, with nightlife still close.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Phuket: November to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Phuket (Phuket International Airport (HKT)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
