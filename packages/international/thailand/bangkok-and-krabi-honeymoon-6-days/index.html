Bangkok & Krabi Honeymoon

6 days / 5 nightsBangkok · KrabiNovember to February

Trip overview

Bangkok's palaces and rooftop dinners, then three nights of cliffs, islands and sunsets in Krabi.

The route spends 2 nights in Bangkok and 3 nights in Krabi, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits honeymoon packages, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Bangkok 2N
  2. Krabi 3N

Highlights

Grand Palace and Wat Phra KaewWat ArunWat PhoRailay BeachAo NangHong Islands

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Bangkok

  • Arrive via Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew: Royal complex and the Emerald Buddha

Day 2Bangkok: Wat Pho and Chao Phraya dinner cruise

  • Wat Pho: Giant reclining Buddha and Thai massage school
  • Chao Phraya dinner cruise

Day 3Bangkok to Krabi

  • Fly from Bangkok to Krabi (flight timings confirmed in your quote)
  • Check in at your Krabi hotel
  • Ao Nang: Main beach town

Day 4Krabi: Hong Islands and Four Islands tour

  • Hong Islands: Lagoons and snorkelling
  • Four Islands tour: Poda, Chicken, Tup and Phra Nang Cave

Day 5Krabi: Island hopping by longtail and Sunset at Railay West

  • Island hopping by longtail
  • Sunset at Railay West
  • Optional: a private candlelight dinner (arranged on request)

Day 6Depart from Krabi

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Krabi International Airport (KBV) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Fewer hotel changes and later starts, so mornings are yours
  • A room upgrade request and a cake or flower decoration on one night, on request and subject to the hotel
  • Evenings kept free for sunsets and dinners rather than packed with sightseeing
  • Private transfers throughout instead of shared coaches

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Bangkok: Visit the Grand Palace at opening time and dress modestly; shoulders and knees must be covered.
  • Krabi: Take the earliest boat to the Four Islands to beat the day-trip crowds.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Bangkok: November to February. Krabi: November to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Bangkok (Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), about 4 hours from Chennai; Don Mueang (DMK) for some budget flights). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
