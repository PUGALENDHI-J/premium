Hong Kong & Macau

6 days / 5 nightsHong Kong · MacauOctober to December

Trip overview

Hong Kong Disneyland, the Peak and the Big Buddha, then Macau's historic centre and Cotai shows.

The route spends 3 nights in Hong Kong and 2 nights in Macau, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits family holidays and multi-country tours, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Hong Kong 3N
  2. Macau 2N

Highlights

Victoria PeakHong Kong DisneylandOcean ParkRuins of St Paul'sSenado SquareMacau Tower

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Hong Kong

  • Arrive via Hong Kong International Airport (HKG) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Hong Kong Disneyland: Theme park on Lantau

Day 2Hong Kong: Ocean Park and Tian Tan Buddha and Ngong Ping 360

  • Ocean Park: Marine theme park and cable car
  • Tian Tan Buddha and Ngong Ping 360: Giant Buddha reached by cable car

Day 3Hong Kong: Victoria Peak and Star Ferry

  • Victoria Peak: Skyline views from the Peak
  • Star Ferry: Classic harbour crossing

Day 4Hong Kong to Macau

  • Travel from Hong Kong to Macau and cross into Macau by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Macau hotel
  • Shows at the resorts

Day 5Macau: Ruins of St Paul's and Senado Square

  • Ruins of St Paul's: Iconic church façade
  • Senado Square: Wave-patterned colonial square

Day 6Depart from Macau

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Macau International Airport (MFM) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote
  • One active day followed by a slower morning
  • Child-friendly sights and meals noted for each stop
  • Visa applications sequenced for each country

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Hong Kong: Watch the Symphony of Lights from the Tsim Sha Tsui promenade at 8 pm.
  • Macau: Visit the historic centre by day and Cotai in the evening.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Hong Kong: October to December. Macau: October to December.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Hong Kong (Hong Kong International Airport (HKG)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
