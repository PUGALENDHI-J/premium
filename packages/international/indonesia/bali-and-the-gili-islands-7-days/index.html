Bali & the Gili Islands

7 days / 6 nightsUbud · Bali (South Coast) · Lombok & the Gili IslandsApril to October

Trip overview

Bali's culture and beaches, then a fast boat to the car-free Gilis for turtles and sunsets.

The route spends 2 nights in Ubud, 2 nights in Bali (South Coast) and 2 nights in Lombok & the Gili Islands, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits island holidays and honeymoon packages, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Ubud 2N
  2. Bali (South Coast) 2N
  3. Lombok & the Gili Islands 2N

Highlights

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Ubud

  • Arrive via Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Tegallalang Rice Terraces: Iconic terraced paddies

Day 2Ubud: Sacred Monkey Forest and Tirta Empul

  • Sacred Monkey Forest: Temple forest with macaques
  • Tirta Empul: Holy spring water temple

Day 3Ubud to Bali (South Coast)

  • Travel from Ubud to Bali (South Coast) by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Bali (South Coast) hotel
  • Seminyak: Beach clubs and boutiques

Day 4Bali (South Coast): Nusa Dua and Beach clubs

  • Nusa Dua: Resorts and calm beaches
  • Beach clubs

Day 5Bali (South Coast) to Lombok & the Gili Islands

  • Ferry from Bali (South Coast) to Lombok & the Gili Islands (sailing times vary by season and are confirmed in your quote)
  • Check in at your Lombok & the Gili Islands hotel
  • Gili Trawangan, Meno and Air: Car-free islands with turtles

Day 6Lombok & the Gili Islands: Kuta Lombok and Snorkelling with turtles

  • Kuta Lombok: Surf beaches and bays
  • Snorkelling with turtles
  • Optional: a private candlelight dinner (arranged on request)

Day 7Depart from Lombok & the Gili Islands

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Lombok International Airport (LOP) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Ferries and boats booked with buffer time before flights
  • No flight home on the same day as an outer-island ferry
  • Snorkelling and reef trips planned for calm-sea days
  • Fewer hotel changes and later starts, so mornings are yours
  • A room upgrade request and a cake or flower decoration on one night, on request and subject to the hotel

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Ubud: Visit Tegallalang at sunrise for soft light and empty paths.
  • Bali (South Coast): Pick one base per area; Bali traffic makes long daily drives tiring.
  • Lombok & the Gili Islands: Stay on Gili Air or Meno for quiet; Trawangan is the party island.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Ubud: April to October. Bali (South Coast): April to October. Lombok & the Gili Islands: April to October.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Ubud (Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 1.5 hours). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
