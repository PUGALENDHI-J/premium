Ubud Wellness Retreat

6 days / 5 nightsUbudApril to October

Trip overview

Five nights of yoga, healthy food, rice-field walks and Balinese massage in Ubud.

The route spends 5 nights in Ubud, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits solo travel, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Ubud 5N

Highlights

Tegallalang Rice TerracesSacred Monkey ForestTirta Empul

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Ubud

  • Arrive via Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Ubud Palace and Market: Royal palace and art market

Day 2Ubud: Campuhan Ridge Walk and Rice terrace walk

  • Campuhan Ridge Walk: Easy ridge walk
  • Rice terrace walk

Day 3Ubud: Tegallalang Rice Terraces and Sacred Monkey Forest

  • Tegallalang Rice Terraces: Iconic terraced paddies
  • Sacred Monkey Forest: Temple forest with macaques

Day 4Ubud: Tirta Empul and Bali Swing

  • Tirta Empul: Holy spring water temple
  • Bali Swing: Jungle swings for photos

Day 5Ubud: Balinese cooking class and Yoga

  • Balinese cooking class
  • Yoga

Day 6Depart from Ubud

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Rest days built in every few days
  • Laundry stops planned
  • Travel insurance covering the full trip recommended
  • Central, well-reviewed hotels with good solo-guest feedback
  • Airport pick-up on arrival

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Ubud: Visit Tegallalang at sunrise for soft light and empty paths.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Ubud: April to October.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Ubud (Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 1.5 hours). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
