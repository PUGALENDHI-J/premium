Malaysia & Singapore Budget Tour

6 days / 5 nightsKuala Lumpur · Malacca · SingaporeMay to July and December to February

Trip overview

A good-value two-country trip by road from KL through Malacca to Singapore.

The route spends 2 nights in Kuala Lumpur, 1 night in Malacca and 2 nights in Singapore, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits budget holidays and multi-country tours, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Kuala Lumpur 2N
  2. Malacca 1N
  3. Singapore 2N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Kuala Lumpur

  • Arrive via Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Jalan Alor food street

Day 2Kuala Lumpur: Petronas Twin Towers and Batu Caves

  • Petronas Twin Towers: Skybridge and observation deck
  • Batu Caves: Limestone caves and giant Murugan statue

Day 3Kuala Lumpur to Malacca

  • Travel from Kuala Lumpur to Malacca by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Malacca hotel
  • Jonker Street: Antiques and weekend night market

Day 4Malacca to Singapore

  • Travel from Malacca to Singapore and cross into Singapore by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Singapore hotel
  • Universal Studios Singapore: Movie theme park on Sentosa

Day 5Singapore: Sentosa Island and Little India and Chinatown

  • Sentosa Island: Beaches, S.E.A. Aquarium and cable car
  • Little India and Chinatown: Heritage districts, temples and food

Day 6Depart from Singapore

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Changi Airport (SIN) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Well-reviewed three-star or guesthouse stays in central locations
  • Shared tours where they save money without spoiling the experience
  • Free and low-cost sights given priority
  • Fewer hotel changes to cut transfer costs
  • Visa applications sequenced for each country

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Kuala Lumpur: Book Petronas skybridge tickets online for a specific time; they sell out.
  • Malacca: Stay for the Friday–Sunday Jonker Street night market.
  • Singapore: Visit Jewel Changi on arrival or departure day; it is inside the airport and free to enter.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Kuala Lumpur: May to July and December to February. Malacca: Year-round. Singapore: February to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Kuala Lumpur (Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL), about 4 hours from Chennai). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
