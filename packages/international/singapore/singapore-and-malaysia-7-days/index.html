Trip overview

Singapore's attractions, then Kuala Lumpur's Petronas Towers and Batu Caves and Genting's cool highlands.

The route spends 3 nights in Singapore, 2 nights in Kuala Lumpur and 1 night in Genting Highlands, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits multi-country tours and family holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Singapore 3N
  2. Kuala Lumpur 2N
  3. Genting Highlands 1N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Singapore

  • Arrive via Changi Airport (SIN) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: River cruise

Day 2Singapore: Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay Sands SkyPark

  • Gardens by the Bay: Supertrees, the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome
  • Marina Bay Sands SkyPark: Observation deck over the bay

Day 3Singapore: Universal Studios Singapore and Sentosa Island

  • Universal Studios Singapore: Movie theme park on Sentosa
  • Sentosa Island: Beaches, S.E.A. Aquarium and cable car

Day 4Singapore to Kuala Lumpur

  • Travel from Singapore to Kuala Lumpur and cross into Malaysia by road or train, or fly where it saves time (we compare options in your quote)
  • Check in at your Kuala Lumpur hotel
  • Petronas Twin Towers: Skybridge and observation deck

Day 5Kuala Lumpur: KL Tower and Batu Caves

  • KL Tower: Observation deck and sky box
  • Batu Caves: Limestone caves and giant Murugan statue

Day 6Kuala Lumpur to Genting Highlands

  • Travel from Kuala Lumpur to Genting Highlands by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Genting Highlands hotel
  • Awana SkyWay: Cable car through the rainforest

Day 7Depart from Genting Highlands

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Visa applications sequenced for each country
  • Short flights or trains between countries chosen to save time
  • Currency and SIM tips for each country
  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Singapore: Visit Jewel Changi on arrival or departure day; it is inside the airport and free to enter.
  • Kuala Lumpur: Book Petronas skybridge tickets online for a specific time; they sell out.
  • Genting Highlands: Visit on a weekday; weekends and Malaysian holidays are very crowded.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Singapore: February to April. Kuala Lumpur: May to July and December to February. Genting Highlands: Year-round.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Singapore (Changi Airport (SIN), about 4 hours from Chennai). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
