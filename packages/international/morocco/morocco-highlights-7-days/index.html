Morocco Highlights

7 days / 6 nightsMarrakech · Merzouga (Sahara) · FesMarch to May and September to November

Trip overview

Marrakech's souqs, a Sahara camp at Merzouga and the medieval medina of Fes.

The route spends 3 nights in Marrakech, 1 night in Merzouga (Sahara) and 2 nights in Fes, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits cultural holidays and adventure holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Marrakech 3N
  2. Merzouga (Sahara) 1N
  3. Fes 2N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Marrakech

  • Arrive via Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Bahia Palace: Ornate palace

Day 2Marrakech: Koutoubia Mosque and Souqs

  • Koutoubia Mosque: Landmark minaret
  • Souqs: Spice, leather and lamp markets

Day 3Marrakech: Jemaa el-Fnaa and Atlas Mountains

  • Jemaa el-Fnaa: Main square at night
  • Atlas Mountains: Day trip to Imlil or Ourika

Day 4Marrakech to Merzouga (Sahara)

  • Travel from Marrakech to Merzouga (Sahara) by road or train, or fly where it saves time (we compare options in your quote)
  • Check in at your Merzouga (Sahara) hotel
  • Erg Chebbi dunes: Towering sand dunes

Day 5Merzouga (Sahara) to Fes

  • Travel from Merzouga (Sahara) to Fes by road or train, or fly where it saves time (we compare options in your quote)
  • Check in at your Fes hotel
  • Chouara Tannery: Leather tanning pits

Day 6Fes: Al-Qarawiyyin and Bou Inania Madrasa

  • Al-Qarawiyyin: Ancient university
  • Bou Inania Madrasa: Ornate school

Day 7Depart from Fes

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Fès–Saïs Airport (FEZ) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Licensed local guides at the major heritage sites
  • Timed tickets booked for sites that need them
  • Craft, music or cooking experiences added where they fit
  • Time built in to explore old quarters on foot
  • Activities booked with licensed, insured operators

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Marrakech: Stay in a riad inside the medina for the full experience.
  • Merzouga (Sahara): Break the long drive from Marrakech with a night in the Dades Valley.
  • Fes: Hire a licensed guide for the medina.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Marrakech: March to May and September to November. Merzouga (Sahara): October to April. Fes: March to May and September to November.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Marrakech (Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
