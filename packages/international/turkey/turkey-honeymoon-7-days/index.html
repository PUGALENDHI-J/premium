Turkey Honeymoon

7 days / 6 nightsIstanbul · CappadociaApril to June and September to November

Trip overview

A Bosphorus-view hotel and a cave suite in Cappadocia with a sunrise balloon flight.

The route spends 3 nights in Istanbul and 3 nights in Cappadocia, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits honeymoon packages, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Istanbul 3N
  2. Cappadocia 3N

Highlights

Hagia SophiaBlue MosqueTopkapı PalaceHot-air balloon flightGöreme Open-Air MuseumDerinkuyu Underground City

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Istanbul

  • Arrive via Istanbul Airport (IST) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Bosphorus cruise: Between two continents

Day 2Istanbul: Bosphorus cruise and Hagia Sophia

  • Bosphorus cruise
  • Hagia Sophia: Byzantine wonder

Day 3Istanbul: Blue Mosque and Grand Bazaar

  • Blue Mosque: Six minarets
  • Grand Bazaar: One of the largest covered markets

Day 4Istanbul to Cappadocia

  • Fly from Istanbul to Cappadocia (flight timings confirmed in your quote)
  • Check in at your Cappadocia hotel
  • Hot-air balloon flight: Sunrise over the valleys

Day 5Cappadocia: Göreme Open-Air Museum and Derinkuyu Underground City

  • Göreme Open-Air Museum: Rock-cut churches
  • Derinkuyu Underground City: Multi-level underground city

Day 6Cappadocia: Love Valley and Pigeon Valley and Uçhisar Castle

  • Love Valley and Pigeon Valley: Hiking trails
  • Uçhisar Castle: Rock fortress
  • Optional: a private candlelight dinner (arranged on request)

Day 7Depart from Cappadocia

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Kayseri (ASR) or Nevşehir (NAV) airports for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Fewer hotel changes and later starts, so mornings are yours
  • A room upgrade request and a cake or flower decoration on one night, on request and subject to the hotel
  • Evenings kept free for sunsets and dinners rather than packed with sightseeing
  • Private transfers throughout instead of shared coaches

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Istanbul: Visit mosques outside prayer times and dress modestly.
  • Cappadocia: Book two nights so you have a back-up morning for the balloon.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Istanbul: April to June and September to November. Cappadocia: April to June and September to November.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Istanbul (Istanbul Airport (IST)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
