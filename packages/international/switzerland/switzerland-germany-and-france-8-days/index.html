Switzerland, Germany & France

8 days / 7 nightsParis · Black Forest · Lucerne · Interlaken & JungfrauApril to June and September to October

Trip overview

Three countries by road and rail: Paris, a night among the Black Forest's villages and cuckoo clocks, then Lucerne and Interlaken.

The route spends 2 nights in Paris, 1 night in Black Forest, 2 nights in Lucerne and 2 nights in Interlaken & Jungfrau, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits multi-country tours and family holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Paris 2N
  2. Black Forest 1N
  3. Lucerne 2N
  4. Interlaken & Jungfrau 2N

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Paris

  • Arrive via Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Louvre Museum: The Mona Lisa and much more

Day 2Paris: Seine river cruise and Palace of Versailles day trip

  • Seine river cruise
  • Palace of Versailles day trip

Day 3Paris to Black Forest

  • Travel from Paris to Black Forest and cross into Germany by road or train, or fly where it saves time (we compare options in your quote)
  • Check in at your Black Forest hotel
  • Lake cruise

Day 4Black Forest to Lucerne

  • Travel from Black Forest to Lucerne and cross into Switzerland by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Lucerne hotel
  • Swiss Museum of Transport: Trains, planes and space

Day 5Lucerne: Lake cruise and Old town walk

  • Lake cruise
  • Old town walk

Day 6Lucerne to Interlaken & Jungfrau

  • Travel from Lucerne to Interlaken & Jungfrau by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Interlaken & Jungfrau hotel
  • Harder Kulm: Viewpoint above Interlaken

Day 7Interlaken & Jungfrau: Lake cruises and Jungfraujoch

  • Lake cruises
  • Jungfraujoch: Highest railway station in Europe

Day 8Depart from Interlaken & Jungfrau

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Zurich (ZRH) or Bern for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Visa applications sequenced for each country
  • Short flights or trains between countries chosen to save time
  • Currency and SIM tips for each country
  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Paris: Book the Eiffel Tower summit and the Louvre with timed tickets.
  • Black Forest: Combine with a Switzerland trip; Zurich is about 1.5 hours away.
  • Lucerne: Titlis has year-round snow; carry warm layers even in summer.
  • Interlaken & Jungfrau: Check the Jungfraujoch webcam before going; cloudy days hide the views.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Paris: April to June and September to October. Black Forest: May to October. Lucerne: May to October; December to March for snow on Titlis. Interlaken & Jungfrau: June to September; December to March for snow.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Paris (Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
