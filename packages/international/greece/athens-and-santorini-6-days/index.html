Athens & Santorini

6 days / 5 nightsAthens · SantoriniApril to June and September to October

Trip overview

Two nights in Athens for the Acropolis and Plaka, then a ferry or flight to Santorini for three nights among its white clifftop villages.

The route spends 2 nights in Athens and 3 nights in Santorini, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits cultural holidays and couples' holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Athens 2N
  2. Santorini 3N

Highlights

Acropolis and ParthenonAcropolis MuseumPlakaOia sunsetFiraCaldera boat cruise

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Athens

  • Arrive via Athens International Airport (ATH) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Ancient Agora: Ancient marketplace

Day 2Athens: Temple of Poseidon, Cape Sounion and Acropolis and Parthenon

  • Temple of Poseidon, Cape Sounion: Sunset temple
  • Acropolis and Parthenon: Ancient citadel

Day 3Athens to Santorini

  • Ferry from Athens to Santorini (sailing times vary by season and are confirmed in your quote)
  • Check in at your Santorini hotel
  • Oia sunset: The island's famous sunset

Day 4Santorini: Fira and Caldera boat cruise

  • Fira: Capital on the caldera edge
  • Caldera boat cruise: Volcano and hot springs

Day 5Santorini: Red Beach and Akrotiri

  • Red Beach: Volcanic beach
  • Akrotiri: Bronze Age town

Day 6Depart from Santorini

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Santorini Airport (JTR) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Licensed local guides at the major heritage sites
  • Timed tickets booked for sites that need them
  • Craft, music or cooking experiences added where they fit
  • Time built in to explore old quarters on foot
  • A relaxed pace with two-night stays where possible

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Athens: Visit the Acropolis at opening time in summer.
  • Santorini: Stay in Imerovigli for caldera views with fewer crowds than Oia.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Athens: April to June and September to October. Santorini: May to June and September to October.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Athens (Athens International Airport (ATH)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
