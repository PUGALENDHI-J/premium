Prague & Vienna

7 days / 6 nightsCzech Republic (Czechia) · ViennaApril to June and September to October; December for Christmas markets

Trip overview

Prague's castle and Old Town, then the train to Vienna for imperial palaces, Klimt's The Kiss and an evening concert.

The route spends 3 nights in Czech Republic (Czechia) and 3 nights in Vienna, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits multi-country tours, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Czech Republic (Czechia) 3N
  2. Vienna 3N

Highlights

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Czech Republic (Czechia)

  • Arrive via Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Old Town Square and Astronomical Clock: Medieval square and clock

Day 2Czech Republic (Czechia): Vltava river cruise and Christmas markets

  • Vltava river cruise
  • Christmas markets

Day 3Czech Republic (Czechia): Prague Castle and Charles Bridge

  • Prague Castle: Largest ancient castle complex
  • Charles Bridge: Statue-lined medieval bridge

Day 4Czech Republic (Czechia) to Vienna

  • Travel from Czech Republic (Czechia) to Vienna and cross into Austria by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Vienna hotel
  • Hofburg and Sisi Museum: Imperial apartments

Day 5Vienna: Christmas markets and Danube cruise

  • Christmas markets
  • Danube cruise

Day 6Vienna: St Stephen's Cathedral and Belvedere (The Kiss)

  • St Stephen's Cathedral: Gothic landmark
  • Belvedere (The Kiss): Klimt's masterpiece

Day 7Depart from Vienna

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Vienna International Airport (VIE) for your journey home

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Czech Republic (Czechia): April to June and September to October; December for Christmas markets. Vienna: April to October; December for markets.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Czech Republic (Czechia) (Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.

What documents do I need?
