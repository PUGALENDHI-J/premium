Angkor & Singapore

7 days / 6 nightsSiem Reap (Angkor) · SingaporeNovember to March

Trip overview

A trip of contrasts: three days among the Khmer temples of Angkor, then three nights in Singapore for gardens, Sentosa and hawker food.

The route spends 3 nights in Siem Reap (Angkor) and 3 nights in Singapore, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits multi-country tours and family holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Siem Reap (Angkor) 3N
  2. Singapore 3N

Highlights

Angkor WatBayonTa ProhmGardens by the BayMarina Bay Sands SkyParkUniversal Studios Singapore

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Siem Reap (Angkor)

  • Arrive via Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport (SAI) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Ta Prohm: Jungle temple wrapped in roots

Day 2Siem Reap (Angkor): Banteay Srei and Temple cycling

  • Banteay Srei: Pink sandstone temple with fine carvings
  • Temple cycling

Day 3Siem Reap (Angkor): Angkor Wat and Tonle Sap floating villages

  • Angkor Wat: The largest religious monument in the world
  • Tonle Sap floating villages: Lake communities

Day 4Siem Reap (Angkor) to Singapore

  • Fly from Siem Reap (Angkor) to Singapore and cross into Singapore (flight timings confirmed in your quote)
  • Check in at your Singapore hotel
  • Little India and Chinatown: Heritage districts, temples and food

Day 5Singapore: River cruise and Gardens by the Bay

  • River cruise
  • Gardens by the Bay: Supertrees, the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome

Day 6Singapore: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark and Universal Studios Singapore

  • Marina Bay Sands SkyPark: Observation deck over the bay
  • Universal Studios Singapore: Movie theme park on Sentosa

Day 7Depart from Singapore

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Changi Airport (SIN) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Visa applications sequenced for each country
  • Short flights or trains between countries chosen to save time
  • Currency and SIM tips for each country
  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Siem Reap (Angkor): Buy a three-day Angkor pass; the temples are spread over a large area.
  • Singapore: Visit Jewel Changi on arrival or departure day; it is inside the airport and free to enter.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Siem Reap (Angkor): November to March. Singapore: February to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Siem Reap (Angkor) (Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport (SAI)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
