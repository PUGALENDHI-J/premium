Sri Lanka Honeymoon

8 days / 7 nightsKandy · Nuwara Eliya · Ella · BentotaJanuary to April

Trip overview

The scenic Ella train, tea-country bungalows and three beach nights on the southwest coast.

The route spends 1 night in Kandy, 2 nights in Nuwara Eliya, 1 night in Ella and 3 nights in Bentota, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits honeymoon packages, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Kandy 1N
  2. Nuwara Eliya 2N
  3. Ella 1N
  4. Bentota 3N

Highlights

Temple of the ToothKandy LakeRoyal Botanical Gardens, PeradeniyaTea factoriesGregory LakeHorton Plains and World's EndNine Arches BridgeLittle Adam's PeakElla RockBentota Beach

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Kandy

  • Arrive via Colombo (CMB) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Kandy Lake: Lakeside walk

Day 2Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

  • Travel from Kandy to Nuwara Eliya by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Nuwara Eliya hotel
  • Boating

Day 3Nuwara Eliya: Tea factories and Horton Plains and World's End

  • Tea factories: Tea tours and tastings
  • Horton Plains and World's End: Plateau hike to a cliff edge

Day 4Nuwara Eliya to Ella

  • Travel from Nuwara Eliya to Ella by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Ella hotel
  • Little Adam's Peak: Easy hike with views

Day 5Ella to Bentota

  • Travel from Ella to Bentota by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Bentota hotel
  • Madu River safari: Mangrove boat ride

Day 6Bentota: Brief Garden and Beach time

  • Brief Garden: Landscaped garden
  • Beach time

Day 7Bentota: Kosgoda turtle hatchery and Lunuganga

  • Kosgoda turtle hatchery: Turtle conservation
  • Lunuganga: Geoffrey Bawa's country estate
  • Optional: a private candlelight dinner (arranged on request)

Day 8Depart from Bentota

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Colombo (CMB) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Fewer hotel changes and later starts, so mornings are yours
  • A room upgrade request and a cake or flower decoration on one night, on request and subject to the hotel
  • Evenings kept free for sunsets and dinners rather than packed with sightseeing
  • Private transfers throughout instead of shared coaches

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Kandy: Board the Kandy–Ella train for one of the world's most beautiful rail journeys.
  • Nuwara Eliya: Start the Horton Plains hike by 6 am; mist covers World's End by late morning.
  • Ella: Check train timings at Nine Arches to see one cross the bridge.
  • Bentota: Do the Madu River safari in the morning for birdlife.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Kandy: January to April. Nuwara Eliya: January to April. Ella: January to April. Bentota: November to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Kandy (Colombo (CMB), about 3 hours). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
