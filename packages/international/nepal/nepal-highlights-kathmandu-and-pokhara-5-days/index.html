Nepal Highlights: Kathmandu & Pokhara

5 days / 4 nightsKathmandu · PokharaOctober to November and March to April

Trip overview

Kathmandu's temples and stupas, then Pokhara's lake and sunrise over the Annapurnas.

The route spends 2 nights in Kathmandu and 2 nights in Pokhara, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits family holidays, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Kathmandu 2N
  2. Pokhara 2N

Highlights

Pashupatinath TempleBoudhanath StupaSwayambhunathPhewa LakeSarangkotWorld Peace Pagoda

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Kathmandu

  • Arrive via Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Boudhanath Stupa: One of the largest stupas in the world

Day 2Kathmandu: Swayambhunath and Kathmandu Durbar Square

  • Swayambhunath: Monkey temple overlooking the city
  • Kathmandu Durbar Square: Royal palace square

Day 3Kathmandu to Pokhara

  • Travel from Kathmandu to Pokhara by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Pokhara hotel
  • Phewa Lake: Boating and lakeside cafés

Day 4Pokhara: International Mountain Museum and Boating

  • International Mountain Museum: Himalayan mountaineering
  • Boating

Day 5Depart from Pokhara

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Pokhara International Airport (PKR) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Daily drives kept short, with stops for children
  • Family rooms, connecting rooms or extra beds requested and confirmed in the quote
  • One active day followed by a slower morning
  • Child-friendly sights and meals noted for each stop

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Kathmandu: Book the Everest flight for your first morning so there is a back-up day if weather cancels it.
  • Pokhara: Paraglide from Sarangkot in the morning for the clearest views.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Kathmandu: October to November and March to April. Pokhara: October to November and March to April.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Kathmandu (Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM)). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
