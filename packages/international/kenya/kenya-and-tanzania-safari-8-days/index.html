Kenya & Tanzania Safari

8 days / 7 nightsMasai Mara · Serengeti · Ngorongoro CraterJuly to October for the migration; January to February

Trip overview

Follow the migration across the Mara and the Serengeti, with Ngorongoro Crater.

The route spends 3 nights in Masai Mara, 3 nights in Serengeti and 1 night in Ngorongoro Crater, so you get time for the main sights without changing hotels every day. It suits wildlife safaris and multi-country tours, and every day can be shortened, swapped or extended.

  1. Masai Mara 3N
  2. Serengeti 3N
  3. Ngorongoro Crater 1N

Highlights

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1Arrive in Masai Mara

  • Arrive via Wilson Airport and transfer to your hotel
  • Check in and settle in
  • Later: Big Five game drives: Lions, leopards, elephants, buffalo, rhino

Day 2Masai Mara: Hot-air balloon safari and Game drives

  • Hot-air balloon safari: Sunrise flight
  • Game drives

Day 3Masai Mara: Balloon safari and Mara River crossings

  • Balloon safari
  • Mara River crossings: Wildebeest migration (seasonal)

Day 4Masai Mara to Serengeti

  • Travel from Masai Mara to Serengeti and cross into Tanzania by private vehicle
  • Check in at your Serengeti hotel
  • Balloon safari: Sunrise flights

Day 5Serengeti: Game drives and Balloon safari

  • Game drives
  • Balloon safari

Day 6Serengeti: Walking safari and Seronera Valley

  • Walking safari
  • Seronera Valley: Big cats year-round

Day 7Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater

  • Travel from Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater by private vehicle, with stops on the way
  • Check in at your Ngorongoro Crater hotel
  • Crater floor game drive: Big Five in one day

Day 8Depart from Ngorongoro Crater

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Transfer to Kilimanjaro (JRO) for your journey home

How this version is planned

  • Safari permits booked as soon as dates are fixed, as zones sell out
  • Early-morning and afternoon drives when animals are most active
  • Lodges chosen close to park gates
  • Naturalist-led drives where available
  • Visa applications sequenced for each country

This is a suggested itinerary. Sightseeing order may change with opening days, weather and local conditions; your final plan is in your written quote.

Good to know

  • Masai Mara: Fly in and out to save a long, bumpy drive.
  • Serengeti: We choose your camp by month to follow the herds.
  • Ngorongoro Crater: Descend at opening time to beat the crowds.

FAQs

Questions about this trip

Can I change this itinerary?
Yes. This is a private, customisable itinerary. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, swap sightseeing and adjust the pace before you book.
When is the best time for this trip?
Masai Mara: July to October for the migration; January to February. Serengeti: June to October; January to March for calving. Ngorongoro Crater: June to October.
How do I start the trip from Chennai?
The trip begins in Masai Mara (Wilson Airport, Nairobi, then bush flight). We can add return flights from Chennai or any other city to your quote.
How much does this package cost?
We quote each trip individually because the price depends on travel dates, hotel category, room sharing and group size. Request a quote and we send a price on WhatsApp.
What documents do I need?
A passport valid for at least six months beyond your return date is commonly required. Visa and entry rules for Indian citizens vary by country and change, so we confirm the current requirements from official sources when you book.
