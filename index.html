Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are flights included in the package price?
No. Flight tickets, visa/TDAC and personal expenses are not included unless stated. We can help you plan flights from Chennai when you enquire.
When do the group batches leave Chennai?
Group batches fly out of Chennai every month, in the last two weeks of the month. Seats are limited, so enquire early to hold yours.
When is the Yi Peng lantern festival?
Yi Peng is on Nov 24–25, 2026 in Chiang Mai. Our Honeymoon Special: Lantern of Love is timed so you are there for the grand lantern evening.
How do I book?
Tap “Enquire now”, share a few details and we reply on WhatsApp. You can also call us on +91 73059 71481. Final inclusions are confirmed at booking.
Can you add Krabi, Koh Samui or other places?
Yes, ask us. Mention the places you want in the enquiry notes and we will suggest a plan around them.
