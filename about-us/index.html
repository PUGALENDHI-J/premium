About Nexara Holidays

Who we are

We don't plan trips. We craft stories worth retelling.

From Chennai to seventy-plus countries, Nexara Holidays designs private journeys for travellers who expect more than a package: considered itineraries, trusted partners on the ground, and a real person beside you from the first call to the journey home.

Nexara Holidays is a Chennai-based travel house crafting holidays across India and the world. Our desk plans more than 580 itineraries, spanning 70+ countries and 30+ Indian states: from Maldives water villas and Swiss lakeside rail journeys to Kashmir houseboats and the high passes of Ladakh.

Every journey is built the same way. Flights, hotels, visas, transfers and experiences arrive in one written quote, designed around your dates, your pace and your priorities. Honeymoons, family holidays, sacred journeys, corporate delegations and trade-fair travel each receive the same attention to detail, because each one marks an important moment in someone's life or business.

We stay deliberately personal. You are never passed between departments. You speak to a Nexara planner who knows your trip, and who stays reachable on WhatsApp while you travel.

Our emblem

The Arctic tern

Look closely at our N. It is not only a letter; it is a flight path.

Our emblem is the Arctic tern, a seabird that travels from the Arctic to the Antarctic and back every year, roughly 70,000 kilometres, one of the longest migrations of any animal on earth. It crosses oceans, seasons and entire hemispheres with precision and purpose. That is the standard we hold our journeys to.

  • The N is your foundation: strong, composed and enduring.
  • The bird rising from the N shows a journey and an identity that are one.
  • The golden swoosh is the trail a Nexara traveller leaves behind.
  • The gold X at the heart of the name is where your ambition and our expertise meet.

Our vision and mission

Measured in memories, not miles

VisionTo be the most trusted name in premium journeys from South India, measured not in miles travelled but in memories made and clients who return.

Mission. To design every journey with four commitments:

  1. 01

    Crafted, never copied

    Every itinerary is built around you. Choose a fully private journey, or a small-group departure with limited seats and a dedicated tour manager.

  2. 02

    Experiences with meaning

    A candlelit dinner above the Maldivian lagoon, Petra at night, a heritage journey to your ancestral hometown, a private yacht at sunset. We plan chapters of your life story, not just hotel nights.

  3. 03

    Seamless from start to finish

    Visa guidance, flights, forex, insurance, hotels and transfers are handled together, so the only decision left for you is where to look first.

  4. 04

    Service that stays with you

    Private air-conditioned transfers, a Nexara representative to meet and greet you on arrival, and a planner on WhatsApp around the clock while you travel.

How we work

From your first message to a confirmed trip

Four simple steps, and you decide at every one of them.

  1. 01

    Tell us your idea

    On WhatsApp, by phone or through our custom tour form, from wherever you live.

  2. 02

    Get a written plan

    We send a written plan with a price. Itineraries on this website are starting points, not fixed products.

  3. 03

    Change anything

    Swap hotels, add a day or change the route until the plan is right for you.

  4. 04

    Confirm when ready

    Nothing is booked until you confirm in writing and pay the deposit stated in your quote.
