Tamil Nadu roots journeys

Come home to Tamil Nadu

For Tamil families in Malaysia, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Fiji, South Africa, the Gulf, Europe and North America, and for families within India, a roots journey often means finding the native village (ooru), visiting the family deity's temple (kula deivam) and meeting relatives. We plan it region by region, with drivers and guides who speak Tamil.

  • Travel to your native village with a Tamil-speaking driver-guide
  • Visits to your kula deivam temple, with poojas booked through the temple office
  • Family reunions, with stays and transport for every generation
  • Trips timed for Pongal, temple festivals and family occasions
  • Local food, crafts and the stories of each region

Chennai & Thondai Nadu

The northern region around Chennai: old Mylapore and George Town, the Pallava temples of Kanchipuram and Mahabalipuram, and inland forts and temple towns such as Vellore, Gingee and Tiruvannamalai.

ChennaiKanchipuramMahabalipuramTiruvannamalai

Things to explore

  • Mylapore's Kapaleeshwarar temple and tank
  • Kanchipuram's silk-weaving streets and temples
  • Pallava shore temples at Mahabalipuram
  • Vellore and Gingee forts
  • Girivalam at Tiruvannamalai

When to go: December–January brings Chennai's music and dance season; Karthigai Deepam at Tiruvannamalai falls in November or December.

Chola Nadu: the Kaveri delta

The rice bowl of Tamil Nadu and heartland of the Cholas: temple towns, agraharam streets and delta villages where many families trace their ancestors.

ThanjavurKumbakonamTiruchirappalliChidambaram

Things to explore

  • Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur
  • Agraharam streets and village temples of the delta
  • Bronze casting at Swamimalai
  • Srirangam and Thiruvanaikaval in Trichy
  • Thanjavur paintings and veena makers

When to go: The Thyagaraja Aradhana music festival at Thiruvaiyaru is in January; Pongal is in mid-January.

Chettinad

The home villages of the Nattukottai Chettiars, whose trading families went to Burma, Malaya, Ceylon and Saigon and built grand mansions back home. Many diaspora families return here to find the family house.

ChettinadMadurai

Things to explore

  • Mansions of Kanadukathan and Karaikudi
  • Athangudi tile workshops
  • Pillayarpatti cave temple
  • Chettinad cooking in a family kitchen
  • Antique and kandangi sari markets

When to go: October to March is most comfortable; many families return for weddings and temple festivals.

Pandya Nadu: Madurai and the south

The old Pandya country, from Madurai's Meenakshi temple to the coast at Rameswaram and the southern tip at Kanyakumari, with village festivals, Tirunelveli's temples and family shrines across the dry south.

MaduraiRameswaramTiruchendurKanyakumari

Things to explore

  • Meenakshi Amman Temple and Madurai's old streets
  • Village festivals and family deity temples
  • Rameswaram and Dhanushkodi
  • Tiruchendur's seashore temple
  • Sunrise at Kanyakumari

When to go: The Chithirai festival in Madurai is in April–May; Pongal village celebrations are in mid-January.

Kongu Nadu: the west

The western uplands of textile towns, farming villages and hill temples, with its own Kongu cuisine and long family histories in Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Erode and Salem.

CoimbatorePalaniYercaud

Things to explore

  • Marudhamalai and Perur temples near Coimbatore
  • Palani's hilltop Murugan temple
  • Textile towns of Tiruppur and Erode
  • Kongu home cooking
  • Farm and village visits

When to go: October to March; Thai Poosam at Palani is in January or February.

The hills: Nilgiris, Kodaikanal and Yercaud

Hill towns with tea estates, old schools and churches, and the Badaga and Toda communities of the Nilgiris, whose villages are visited only with local guides and respect for their customs.

OotyCoonoorKodaikanalYercaud

Things to explore

  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway, a UNESCO site
  • Tea estates and factories
  • Hill-station schools and churches families attended
  • Badaga village visits with local guides
  • Kodaikanal's lake and forest walks

When to go: March to June and September to November; the monsoon is heavy from June to August.

The Coromandel coast

A coast of many faiths and trading histories: Velankanni's basilica, the Nagore Dargah, the Danish fort at Tharangambadi and French Puducherry, home to old Tamil Christian, Muslim and Hindu families.

VelankanniPuducherryChidambaram

Things to explore

  • Velankanni Basilica and Nagore Dargah
  • Fort Dansborg at Tharangambadi
  • Puducherry's French and Tamil quarters
  • Fishing villages and coastal temples
  • Pichavaram mangroves

When to go: December to March; the Velankanni feast falls in late August and early September.

Global heritage experiences

Journeys that start with your story

Roots journeys are personal. We do not assume where your family is from or what you will find; we start with what you know and plan carefully around it, with local guides, translators and archive visits where they help.

  1. Ancestral hometown and village visitsFind the street, temple, church or farm your family came from, with a local guide and translator where needed.
  2. Family heritage and genealogy journeysVisits planned around archives, registry offices and heritage centres, with time to explore what you find.
  3. Family reunions and multi-generation tripsPacing, rooms and transport that work for grandparents, parents and children travelling together.
  4. Traditional communities and rural staysHomestays and village visits arranged with community operators, on their terms.
  5. Historic neighbourhoods and architectureOld towns, mansions, forts and preserved districts, explained by local historians.
  6. Festivals and local traditionsTrips timed for harvest festivals, religious celebrations and family events.
  7. Regional food and cookingMarkets, home kitchens and cooking sessions that pass on family recipes.
  8. Pilgrimage and spiritual journeysFor travellers who want them; see our Sacred journeys for faith pilgrimages.
