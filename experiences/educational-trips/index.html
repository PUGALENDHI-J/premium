Space science in Florida

United States · 7–9 days suggested

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex, science museums and theme-park physics days around Orlando.

  • Science & space
  • Technology & robotics

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Technology and robotics in Japan

Japan · 7–8 days suggested

Tokyo's science museums such as Miraikan, rail and robotics technology, and Kyoto for traditional culture.

  • Technology & robotics
  • Science & space
  • Language & culture

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Science and sustainability in Singapore

Singapore · 4–6 days suggested

Science Centre Singapore, Gardens by the Bay, water management at NEWater and wildlife parks; short flights from India.

  • Science & space
  • Sustainability & agriculture
  • Environment & wildlife

Suits: Primary (8–11), Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18)

History and museums in London

United Kingdom · 7–10 days suggested

The British Museum, Natural History and Science Museums, Greenwich and university-town visits to Oxford or Cambridge.

  • History & heritage
  • Science & space
  • University visits

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Classical history and art in Italy

Italy · 7–9 days suggested

Ancient Rome and Pompeii, Renaissance Florence and the Uffizi, with art-history guides.

  • History & heritage
  • Art & architecture

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university

Ancient civilisations in Egypt

Egypt · 6–8 days suggested

The Giza pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum, then Luxor's temples and the Valley of the Kings.

  • History & heritage
  • Geography & geology

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Wildlife conservation in Kenya

Kenya · 7–9 days suggested

Game drives in the Masai Mara and conservation talks in Nairobi on wildlife protection and community conservancies.

  • Environment & wildlife

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Rainforest ecology in Costa Rica

Costa Rica · 8–10 days suggested

Cloud-forest reserves, volcano landscapes, sustainable coffee farms and biodiversity field days.

  • Environment & wildlife
  • Sustainability & agriculture

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university

Marine science on the Great Barrier Reef

Australia · 6–8 days suggested

Reef trips with marine educators, the Daintree rainforest and reef conservation programmes from Cairns.

  • Marine science
  • Environment & wildlife

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Volcanoes and glaciers in Iceland

Iceland · 6–8 days suggested

Geothermal power, tectonic rift valleys, glaciers and waterfalls on the Golden Circle and south coast.

  • Geography & geology
  • Environment & wildlife

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university

Architecture in Barcelona

Spain · 5–7 days suggested

Gaudí's Sagrada Família and Park Güell, the Eixample's urban grid and modern waterfront design.

  • Art & architecture
  • Language & culture

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university

Innovation and design in Dubai

United Arab Emirates · 4–5 days suggested

The Museum of the Future, Burj Khalifa engineering and desert ecology, a short flight from India.

  • Art & architecture
  • Technology & robotics
  • Industry & business

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Water and agriculture in the Netherlands

Netherlands · 6–8 days suggested

Dutch water management and flood defences, greenhouse horticulture and university campuses.

  • Sustainability & agriculture
  • Geography & geology
  • University visits

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university

Engineering and industry in Germany

Germany · 7–9 days suggested

Stuttgart's automotive museums, Munich's Deutsches Museum and technical university visits.

  • Industry & business
  • Technology & robotics
  • University visits

Suits: Senior school (16–18), College & university, Teachers & professionals

French language and culture in Paris

France · 6–8 days suggested

Language sessions, the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay, and day trips to Versailles.

  • Language & culture
  • Art & architecture
  • History & heritage

Suits: Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18), College & university

Heritage and science in India

India · 4–6 days suggested

Delhi's museums and monuments, the Taj Mahal and Jaipur's Jantar Mantar observatory, for domestic and visiting school groups.

  • History & heritage
  • Science & space
  • Language & culture

Suits: Primary (8–11), Secondary (12–15), Senior school (16–18)

Safety and supervision

Planned with your institution

  • Group flights, hotels and private coaches booked together
  • Licensed local guides and venue bookings for museums and visits
  • A named planner on WhatsApp before and during the trip
  • Room lists, day plans and emergency contacts shared with your staff
  • Travel insurance guidance and visa document checklists

Supervision ratios, consent forms and safeguarding policies are set by your institution; we plan the trip around them.
