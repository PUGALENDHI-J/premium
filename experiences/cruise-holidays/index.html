Cruise regions

Explore worldwide cruise regions

Seasons and ports below are typical patterns, not schedules: routes change every year and not every route sails from every port. Lines that sail many of these regions include Royal Caribbean, MSC Cruises, Norwegian Cruise Line, Celebrity, Princess, Costa, Viking, AmaWaterways, Hurtigruten, Silversea and Cordelia Cruises.

Mediterranean

Rome, the Greek islands, Barcelona and the Riviera on week-long sailings, easy to combine with time on land in Europe.

Typical season
Main season about April to November; some western Mediterranean sailings year-round
Common departure ports
Barcelona · Civitavecchia (Rome) · Piraeus (Athens) · Venice area

  • Island hopping

Norwegian fjords & Northern Europe

Fjord villages, glaciers and Baltic and Scandinavian capitals in the long summer days.

Typical season
Mostly May to September
Common departure ports
Southampton · Copenhagen · Kiel · Bergen

  • Coastal
  • Couples
  • Luxury

Caribbean

Beach islands, private cruise-line islands and big resort ships, often combined with Florida's theme parks.

Typical season
Year-round; hurricane season runs June to November
Common departure ports
Miami · Fort Lauderdale · Port Canaveral · San Juan

  • Island hopping

Alaska

The Inside Passage, glaciers and whales, with land extensions to Denali.

Typical season
About May to September
Common departure ports
Seattle · Vancouver
  • Ocean
  • Expedition
  • Couples
  • Cruise + land

Short sailings from Singapore to Malaysia and Thailand, popular with first-time cruisers from India.

Typical season
Many sailings from Singapore through the year
Common departure ports
Singapore · Port Klang · Phuket · Ho Chi Minh City

  • Short getaway

Japan & East Asia

Japanese ports, Korean cities and Chinese coastal capitals, with cherry blossom and autumn-colour itineraries.

Typical season
Spring (cherry blossom) and autumn are the busiest seasons
Common departure ports
Yokohama (Tokyo) · Kobe · Busan · Shanghai
  • Ocean
  • Couples

Typical season
Roughly November to March
Common departure ports
Dubai · Abu Dhabi · Doha

  • Family

Domestic sailings run in the dry months; routes and ports change by season

Sydney sailings to New Caledonia, Vanuatu, Fiji and New Zealand's sounds.

Busiest from about October to April

Danube & Rhine river cruises

Small river ships docking in the heart of Budapest, Vienna, Cologne and Strasbourg.

Typical season
Roughly March to December, with Christmas-market sailings
Common departure ports
Budapest · Vienna · Passau · Amsterdam · Basel
  • River
  • Couples

Nile river cruise

Three to four nights between Luxor and Aswan, with temples at Edfu and Kom Ombo along the way.

Typical season
Most comfortable October to April
Common departure ports
Luxor · Aswan
  • River
  • Couples
  • Family

Mekong river cruise

Vietnam's delta and Cambodia's riverside temples and villages on small river ships.

Typical season
Typically August to May; water levels shape the route
Common departure ports
Ho Chi Minh City area · Phnom Penh · Siem Reap
  • River
  • Couples
  • Cruise + land

Antarctica expedition

Expedition ships crossing the Drake Passage from Ushuaia for penguins, ice and Zodiac landings.

Typical season
Southern summer only, about November to March
Common departure ports
Ushuaia
  • Expedition
  • Luxury

Galápagos

Small expedition ships with naturalist guides among the islands' wildlife; often paired with Peru or mainland Ecuador.

Typical season
Year-round; seas and wildlife change with the season
Common departure ports
Baltra · San Cristóbal
