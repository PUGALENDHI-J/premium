Canton Fair & Global Business Travel

China's largest trade fair, twice a year in Guangzhou: we plan your travel, business visa, hotel near the Canton Fair Complex, interpreters and factory visits, and travel to trade fairs and industry hubs worldwide.

Plan My Canton Fair Trip →Canton Fair guide
The main event · Guangzhou, China

Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair)

The Canton Fair is China's largest and longest-running trade fair, held since 1957 and run by the Ministry of Commerce and the Guangdong provincial government. It takes place twice a year in Guangzhou, in spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November), at the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou, one of the largest exhibition centres in the world.

Each session is split into three phases of about five days, each devoted to different industries, so buyers choose the phase that matches what they want to source. Tens of thousands of Chinese manufacturers and exporters exhibit, and there is also an International Pavilion for exhibitors from other countries.

For Indian importers, wholesalers, retailers and manufacturers it is the single most efficient way to meet many suppliers in one trip. Nexara plans the whole journey from Chennai or any Indian city: travel, the China business visa, a hotel near the complex, transfers, interpreters and supplier visits after the fair.

How to visit the Canton Fair from India

  1. Choose your phase

    Pick the phase that matches your products. Many buyers cover two phases; the gap between them is a good time for factory visits.

  2. Register as a buyer

    Pre-register free on the Canton Fair's official Buyer Service System or app with your passport and company details. First-time buyers receive the invitation letter through the same system.

  3. Apply for your China visa

    Indian passport holders need a visa for mainland China. Most buyers apply for a business (M) visa with the fair's invitation letter; the name and passport number must match on the letter, the visa and the badge.

  4. Book your travel and hotel early

    Fix your travel as soon as you know your phase. Hotels near the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou fill up and raise prices months before each session.

  5. Collect your badge

    First-time buyers collect the buyer badge at the registration counters in Guangzhou, including at the airport and at the complex; returning buyers can reuse their badge.

  6. Visit, then follow up

    Walk the halls with an interpreter if you need one, collect catalogues and quotes, and leave a day or two to visit shortlisted factories in Guangzhou, Foshan or Shenzhen.

Tips from the fair floor

  • Carry plenty of business cards and a short company profile; exhibitors exchange cards first and talk prices later.
  • Most suppliers quote in US dollars. Ask for the minimum order quantity, lead time and sample cost at the stand.
  • Google, WhatsApp and Gmail are blocked in mainland China without a roaming plan or an international eSIM, so set that up before you fly. Suppliers mostly use WeChat.
  • Cash is rarely used: Alipay and WeChat Pay accept many foreign cards, so link yours before you travel.
  • The halls are huge: wear comfortable shoes, use the fair's app or map, and plan each day by hall and zone.
  • Metro Line 8 stops at Xingang East and Pazhou stations, right beside the complex, and is the fastest way in each morning.

Canton Fair questions

When is the Canton Fair held?
Twice a year in Guangzhou: a spring session in April and May and an autumn session in October and November, each in three phases of about five days. The organiser announces the exact dates a few months ahead, and we confirm them before booking any non-refundable travel.
Do Indians need a visa for the Canton Fair?
Yes. Indian passport holders need a visa for mainland China. Most buyers apply for a business (M) visa using the invitation letter from the fair's Buyer Service System. We help with the checklist, but visa decisions are made by the Chinese visa office.
Is the Canton Fair free to attend?
Yes. The buyer badge is free for overseas buyers who register in advance. You pay only for your travel, visa, hotel and any services such as interpreters.
Which phase should I attend?
Phase 1 is for electronics, appliances, machinery, tools, energy and vehicles; Phase 2 for building materials, furniture, kitchenware, home goods and gifts; Phase 3 for textiles, garments, shoes, bags, office supplies, health products and food. Tell us your products and we will suggest the phase.
How many days do I need?
Four to six nights covers one phase with a spare day for meetings. To cover two phases, plan nine to ten nights; to visit factories after the fair, add two to four nights in Foshan, Shenzhen or Yiwu.
How do I get from India to Guangzhou?
Guangzhou has a large international airport with good connections from Indian cities. We plan the route from your city, timed to your phase, along with transfers to your hotel near the fair.
Can you arrange an interpreter and factory visits?
Yes. We book English-Chinese interpreters by the day and arrange transport for factory and showroom visits. Supplier introductions and factory access depend on the companies themselves.
Can you plan a group delegation?
Yes. We plan Canton Fair delegations for associations, chambers of commerce and companies, with group travel, hotels together near the complex, transfers and a shared day-by-day plan.

Session dates, phase layouts and registration rules are set by the organiser and change each season; we confirm them before booking any non-refundable travel.
