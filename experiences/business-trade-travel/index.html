Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair)

Guangzhou, China

Spring session (April–May) and autumn session (October–November), each in three phases

  • Technology & electronics
  • Manufacturing & machinery
  • Consumer products & home
  • Textiles & fashion
  • Healthcare & medical
  • Food & beverage
About this event

China's largest trade fair, held twice a year at the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou. Phase one covers electronics, machinery and vehicles; phase two housewares, gifts and building materials; phase three textiles, fashion, toys, medical and food products. Buyers register online in advance.

Official site ↗

Hannover Messe

Held every year, usually in April

  • Manufacturing & machinery

One planner for the whole group: flights from your city, hotels near the venue, transfers and a shared day-by-day plan.

  5. Incentive travelReward trips for teams and dealers, from city breaks to cruises.
